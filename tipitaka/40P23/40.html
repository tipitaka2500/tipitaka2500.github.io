Sanidassanattika, Vedanāttika

49. 156 Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ nasukhāya vedanāya sampayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca anidassanaappaṭigho sukhāya vedanāya sampayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.
