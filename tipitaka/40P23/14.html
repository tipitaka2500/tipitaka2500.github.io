18. 63 Nakusalaṁ namicchattaniyataṁ dhammaṁ paccayā akusalo micchattaniyato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.
