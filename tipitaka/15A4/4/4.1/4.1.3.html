Indriyavagga

Paññābalasutta

153. 911 [377] “Cattārimāni, bhikkhave, balāni. Katamāni cattāri? Paññābalaṁ, vīriyabalaṁ, anavajjabalaṁ, saṅgahabalaṁ[378]— imāni kho, bhikkhave, cattāri balānī”ti.

912 Tatiyaṁ.

  1. dutiyabalasuttaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. saṅgahabalaṁ: saṅgāhabalaṁ (sī, pā1), saṅgāhakabalaṁ (syā1-3, kaṃ) (Word Variant) ↩
