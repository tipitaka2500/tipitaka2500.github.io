5.2.3. อกตั์ุตาสุต์ต

Pañcamapaṇṇāsaka

Parisāvagga

Akataññutāsutta

213. 1379 [543] “Catūhi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ niraye. Katamehi catūhi? Kāyaduccaritena, vacīduccaritena, manoduccaritena, akataññutā akataveditā[544]— imehi kho, bhikkhave, catūhi dhammehi samannāgato yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ niraye.

1380 Catūhi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ sagge. Katamehi catūhi? Kāyasucaritena, vacīsucaritena, manosucaritena, kataññutā kataveditā[545]— imehi kho, bhikkhave, catūhi dhammehi samannāgato yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ sagge”ti.

1381 Tatiyaṁ.

  1. dutiyaniraya-sagganikkhittasuttaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. akataveditā: akataññutāakataveditāya (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. kataveditā: kataññutākataveditāya (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
