5.8.3 Iddhipādasutta

5.8.3. อิท์ธิปาทสุต์ต

Pañcamapaṇṇāsaka

Rāgapeyyāla

Iddhipādasutta

276. 1603 [606] “Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya cattāro dhammā bhāvetabbā. Katame cattāro? Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu chandasamādhipadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṁ iddhipādaṁ bhāveti; vīriyasamādhi…pe… cittasamādhi…pe… vīmaṁsāsamādhipadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṁ iddhipādaṁ bhāveti. Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya ime cattāro dhammā bhāvetabbā”ti.

1604 Tatiyaṁ.
