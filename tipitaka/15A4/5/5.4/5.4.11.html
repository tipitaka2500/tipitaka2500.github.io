5.4.11 Sappurisānisaṃsasutta

5.4.11. สัป์ปุริสานิสํสสุต์ต

Pañcamapaṇṇāsaka

Kammavagga

Sappurisānisaṃsasutta

242. 1476 “Sappurisaṁ, bhikkhave, nissāya cattāro ānisaṁsā pāṭikaṅkhā. Katame cattāro? Ariyena sīlena vaḍḍhati, ariyena samādhinā vaḍḍhati, ariyāya paññāya vaḍḍhati, ariyāya vimuttiyā vaḍḍhati— sappurisaṁ, bhikkhave, nissāya ime cattāro ānisaṁsā pāṭikaṅkhā”ti.

1477 Ekādasamaṁ.

1478 Kammavaggo catuttho.

1479

1480 Saṁkhitta vitthāra soṇakāyana,
Sikkhāpadaṁ ariyamaggo bojjhaṅgaṁ;
Sāvajjañceva abyābajjhaṁ,
Samaṇo ca sappurisānisaṁsoti.
