3.4.2. ปฏิภานสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Puggalavagga

Paṭibhānasutta

132. 844 [358] [357] [356] “Cattārome, bhikkhave, puggalā santo saṁvijjamānā lokasmiṁ. Katame cattāro? Yuttappaṭibhāno, no muttappaṭibhāno; muttappaṭibhāno, no yuttappaṭibhāno; yuttappaṭibhāno ca muttappaṭibhāno ca; neva yuttappaṭibhāno na muttappaṭibhāno— ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro puggalā santo saṁvijjamānā lokasmin”ti.
