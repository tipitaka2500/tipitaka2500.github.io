2.2.6 Sarāgasutta

2.2.6. สราคสุต์ต

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Pattakammavagga

Sarāgasutta

66. 484 [215] “Cattārome, bhikkhave, puggalā santo saṁvijjamānā lokasmiṁ. Katame cattāro? Sarāgo, sadoso, samoho, samāno— ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro puggalā santo saṁvijjamānā lokasminti.

485 Sārattā rajanīyesu,
Piyarūpābhinandino;
Mohena āvutā sattā[216],
Baddhā vaḍḍhenti bandhanaṁ. [217]

486 Rāgajaṁ dosajañcāpi,
mohajaṁ cāpaviddasū;
Karontākusalaṁ kammaṁ[218],
savighātaṁ dukhudrayaṁ.

487 Avijjānivutā posā,
andhabhūtā acakkhukā;
Yathā dhammā tathā santā,
na tassevanti[219] maññare”ti.

488 Chaṭṭhaṁ.

  1. sarāga (puggala) suttaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. sattā: adhamā sattā (sī, syā1, syā2, kaṃ), adhamasattā (syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Baddhā: bandhā (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. kammaṁ: dhammaṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. tassevanti: nassevanti (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
