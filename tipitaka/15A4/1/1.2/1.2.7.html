1.2.7. ปฐมอคติสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Caravagga

Paṭhamaagatisutta

17. 112 [47] “Cattārimāni, bhikkhave, agatigamanāni. Katamāni cattāri? Chandāgatiṁ gacchati, dosāgatiṁ gacchati, mohāgatiṁ gacchati, bhayāgatiṁ gacchati— imāni kho, bhikkhave, cattāri agatigamanānīti.

113 [48] Chandā dosā bhayā mohā,
yo dhammaṁ ativattati;
Nihīyati tassa yaso,
kāḷapakkheva candimā”ti.

114 Sattamaṁ.
