1.2.5 Paññattisutta

1.2.5. ปัญ์ญัต์ติสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Caravagga

Paññattisutta

15. 104 [45] “Catasso imā, bhikkhave, aggapaññattiyo. Katamā catasso? Etadaggaṁ, bhikkhave, attabhāvīnaṁ yadidaṁ— rāhu asurindo. Etadaggaṁ, bhikkhave, kāmabhogīnaṁ yadidaṁ— rājā mandhātā. Etadaggaṁ, bhikkhave, ādhipateyyānaṁ yadidaṁ— māro pāpimā. Sadevake, bhikkhave, loke samārake sabrahmake sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiyā pajāya sadevamanussāya tathāgato aggamakkhāyati arahaṁ sammāsambuddho. Imā kho, bhikkhave, catasso aggapaññattiyoti.

105 Rāhuggaṁ attabhāvīnaṁ,
mandhātā kāmabhoginaṁ;
Māro ādhipateyyānaṁ,
iddhiyā yasasā jalaṁ. [46]

106 Uddhaṁ tiriyaṁ apācīnaṁ,
yāvatājagatogati;
Sadevakassa lokassa,
buddho aggo pavuccatī”ti.

107 Pañcamaṁ.

  1. aggapaññattisuttaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Rāhuggaṁ: rāhaggaṁ (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
