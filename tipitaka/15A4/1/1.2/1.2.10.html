1.2.10 Bhattuddesakasutta

1.2.10. ภัต์ตุท์เทสกสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Caravagga

Bhattuddesakasutta

20. 123 [53] “Catūhi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato bhattuddesako yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ niraye. Katamehi catūhi? Chandāgatiṁ gacchati, dosāgatiṁ gacchati, mohāgatiṁ gacchati, bhayāgatiṁ gacchati— imehi kho, bhikkhave, catūhi dhammehi samannāgato bhattuddesako yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ niraye.

124 Catūhi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato bhattuddesako yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ sagge. Katamehi catūhi? Na chandāgatiṁ gacchati, na dosāgatiṁ gacchati, na mohāgatiṁ gacchati, na bhayāgatiṁ gacchati— imehi kho, bhikkhave, catūhi dhammehi samannāgato bhattuddesako yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ saggeti.

125 Ye keci kāmesu asaññatā janā,
Adhammikā honti adhammagāravā;
Chandā dosā mohā ca bhayā gāmino[54],
Parisākasaṭo ca panesa vuccati. [55]

126 Evañhi vuttaṁ samaṇena jānatā,
Tasmā hi te sappurisā pasaṁsiyā;
Dhamme ṭhitā ye na karonti pāpakaṁ,
Na chandā na dosā na mohā na bhayā ca gāmino[56];
Parisāya maṇḍo ca panesa vuccati,
Evañhi vuttaṁ samaṇena jānatā”ti.

127 Dasamaṁ.

128 Caravaggo dutiyo.

129

130 Caraṁ sīlaṁ padhānāni,
saṁvaraṁ paññatti pañcamaṁ;
Sokhummaṁ tayo agatī,
bhattuddesena te dasāti.

  1. bhattuddesasuttaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. gāmino: chandā ca dosā ca bhayā ca gāmino (sī, syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Parisākasaṭo: parisakkasāvo (sī, pā1), parisakasaṭo (syā1-3, kaṃ) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. gāmino: na chandadosā na bhayā ca gāmino (sī, syā1-3, kaṃ), na chandā dosā na bhayā ca gāmino (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
