215 Ārammaṇe adhipatiyā upanissaye ekaṁ.

92. 216 Araṇo sanidassano dhammo naaraṇassa nasanidassanassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. Araṇo sanidassano dhammo nasaraṇassa nasanidassanassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)
