1.4.5 Dānānisaṃsasutta

1.4.5. ทานานิสํสสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Sumanavagga

Dānānisaṃsasutta

35. 183 “Pañcime, bhikkhave, dāne ānisaṁsā. Katame pañca? Bahuno janassa piyo hoti manāpo; santo sappurisā bhajanti; kalyāṇo kittisaddo abbhuggacchati; gihidhammā anapagato[95] hoti; kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ upapajjati. Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca dāne ānisaṁsāti.

184 [96] Dadamāno piyo hoti,
sataṁ dhammaṁ anukkamaṁ;
Santo naṁ sadā bhajanti[97],
saññatā brahmacārayo.

185 Te tassa dhammaṁ desenti,
Sabbadukkhāpanūdanaṁ;
Yaṁ so dhammaṁ idhaññāya,
Parinibbāti anāsavo”ti.

186 Pañcamaṁ.

  1. anapagato: gihidhammā anapeto (sī, pā1), gihidhammamanupagato (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. (Open) ↩
  3. bhajanti: bhajanti sappurisā (sī) (Close) ↩
