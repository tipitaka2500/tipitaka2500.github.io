1.3.9. จํกมสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Pañcaṅgikavagga

Caṅkamasutta

29. 123 [61] “Pañcime, bhikkhave, caṅkame ānisaṁsā. Katame pañca? Addhānakkhamo hoti, padhānakkhamo hoti, appābādho hoti, asitaṁ pītaṁ khāyitaṁ sāyitaṁ sammā pariṇāmaṁ gacchati, caṅkamādhigato samādhi ciraṭṭhitiko hoti. Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca caṅkame ānisaṁsā”ti.
