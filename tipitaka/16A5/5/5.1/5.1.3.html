5.1.3 Assājānīyasutta

5.1.3. อัส์สาชานียสุต์ต

Pañcamapaṇṇāsaka

Kimilavagga

Assājānīyasutta

203. 1196 [521] “Pañcahi, bhikkhave, aṅgehi samannāgato rañño bhadro[522] assājānīyo rājāraho hoti rājabhoggo, rañño aṅgantveva saṅkhaṁ gacchati.

1197 Katamehi pañcahi? Ajjavena, javena, maddavena, khantiyā, soraccena— imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi aṅgehi samannāgato rañño bhadro assājānīyo rājāraho hoti rājabhoggo, rañño aṅgantveva saṅkhaṁ gacchati. Evamevaṁ kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu āhuneyyo hoti pāhuneyyo dakkhiṇeyyo añjalikaraṇīyo anuttaraṁ puññakkhettaṁ lokassa.

1198 Katamehi pañcahi? Ajjavena, javena, maddavena, khantiyā, soraccena— imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu āhuneyyo hoti pāhuneyyo dakkhiṇeyyo añjalikaraṇīyo anuttaraṁ puññakkhettaṁ lokassā”ti.

1199 Tatiyaṁ.
