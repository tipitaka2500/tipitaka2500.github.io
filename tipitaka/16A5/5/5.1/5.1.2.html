5.1.2 Dhammassavanasutta

5.1.2. ธัม์มัส์สวนสุต์ต

Pañcamapaṇṇāsaka

Kimilavagga

Dhammassavanasutta

202. 1194 [518] “Pañcime, bhikkhave, ānisaṁsā dhammassavane[519]. Katame pañca? Assutaṁ suṇāti, sutaṁ pariyodāpeti, kaṅkhaṁ vitarati[520], diṭṭhiṁ ujuṁ karoti, cittamassa pasīdati. Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca ānisaṁsā dhammassavane”ti.

1195 Dutiyaṁ.

  1. dhammasavaṇasuttaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. dhammassavane: dhammasavaṇe (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. vitarati: vihanati (syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
