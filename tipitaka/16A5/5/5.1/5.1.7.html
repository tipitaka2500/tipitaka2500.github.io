Yāgusutta

207. 1208 [527] “Pañcime, bhikkhave, ānisaṁsā yāguyā. Katame pañca? Khuddaṁ[528] paṭihanati, pipāsaṁ paṭivineti, vātaṁ anulometi, vatthiṁ sodheti, āmāvasesaṁ pāceti. Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca ānisaṁsā yāguyā”ti.
