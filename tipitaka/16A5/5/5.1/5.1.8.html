5.1.8 Dantakaṭṭhasutta

5.1.8. ทัน์ตกัฏ์สุต์ต

Pañcamapaṇṇāsaka

Kimilavagga

Dantakaṭṭhasutta

208. 1210 “Pañcime, bhikkhave, ādīnavā dantakaṭṭhassa akhādane. Katame pañca? Acakkhussaṁ, mukhaṁ duggandhaṁ hoti, rasaharaṇiyo na visujjhanti, pittaṁ semhaṁ bhattaṁ pariyonandhati[529], bhattamassa nacchādeti. Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca ādīnavā dantakaṭṭhassa akhādane.

1211 Pañcime, bhikkhave, ānisaṁsā dantakaṭṭhassa khādane. Katame pañca? Cakkhussaṁ, mukhaṁ na duggandhaṁ hoti, rasaharaṇiyo visujjhanti, pittaṁ semhaṁ bhattaṁ na pariyonandhati, bhattamassa chādeti. Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca ānisaṁsā dantakaṭṭhassa khādane”ti.

1212 Aṭṭhamaṁ.

  1. pariyonandhati: pariyonaddhati (syā1-3, kaṃ, ka-ma), pariyonaddhanti (pā1) 4V:1183 (Word Variant) ↩
