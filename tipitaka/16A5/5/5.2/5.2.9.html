5.2.9 Aggisutta

5.2.9. อัค์คิสุต์ต

Pañcamapaṇṇāsaka

Akkosakavagga

Aggisutta

219. 1251 “Pañcime, bhikkhave, ādīnavā aggismiṁ. Katame pañca? Acakkhusso, dubbaṇṇakaraṇo, dubbalakaraṇo, saṅgaṇikāpavaḍḍhano[537], tiracchānakathāpavattaniko hoti. Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca ādīnavā aggismin”ti.

1252 Navamaṁ.

  1. saṅgaṇikāpavaḍḍhano: saṅgaṇikāpavaddhano (sī), saṅgaṇikārāmabandhano (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
