5.3.9 Paṭhamakaṇhasappasutta

5.3.9. ปฐมกัณ๎หสัป์ปสุต์ต

Pañcamapaṇṇāsaka

Dīghacārikavagga

Paṭhamakaṇhasappasutta

229. 1280 “Pañcime, bhikkhave, ādīnavā kaṇhasappe. Katame pañca? Asuci, duggandho, sabhīru, sappaṭibhayo, mittadubbhī. Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca ādīnavā kaṇhasappe.

1281 Evamevaṁ kho, bhikkhave, pañcime ādīnavā mātugāme. Katame pañca? Asuci, duggandho, sabhīru, sappaṭibhayo, mittadubbhī. Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca ādīnavā mātugāme”ti.

1282 Navamaṁ.
