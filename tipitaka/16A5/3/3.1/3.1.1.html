3.1.1 Sārajjasutta

3.1.1. สารัช์ชสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Phāsuvihāravagga

Sārajjasutta

101. 608 [274] “Pañcime, bhikkhave, sekhavesārajjakaraṇā dhammā. Katame pañca? Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu saddho hoti, sīlavā hoti, bahussuto hoti, āraddhavīriyo hoti, paññavā hoti.

609 Yaṁ, bhikkhave, assaddhassa sārajjaṁ hoti, saddhassa taṁ sārajjaṁ na hoti. Tasmāyaṁ dhammo sekhavesārajjakaraṇo.

610 Yaṁ, bhikkhave, dussīlassa sārajjaṁ hoti, sīlavato taṁ sārajjaṁ na hoti. Tasmāyaṁ dhammo sekhavesārajjakaraṇo.

611 Yaṁ, bhikkhave, appassutassa sārajjaṁ hoti, bahussutassa taṁ sārajjaṁ na hoti. Tasmāyaṁ dhammo sekhavesārajjakaraṇo.

612 Yaṁ, bhikkhave, kusītassa sārajjaṁ hoti, āraddhavīriyassa taṁ sārajjaṁ na hoti. Tasmāyaṁ dhammo sekhavesārajjakaraṇo.

613 Yaṁ, bhikkhave, duppaññassa sārajjaṁ hoti, paññavato taṁ sārajjaṁ na hoti. Tasmāyaṁ dhammo sekhavesārajjakaraṇo. Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca sekhavesārajjakaraṇā dhammā”ti.

614 Paṭhamaṁ.
