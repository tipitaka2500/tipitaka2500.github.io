4.1.10 Duppaṭivinodayasutta

4.1.10. ทุป์ปฏิวิโนทยสุต์ต

Catutthapaṇṇāsaka

Saddhammavagga

Duppaṭivinodayasutta

160. 927 “Pañcime, bhikkhave, uppannā duppaṭivinodayā. Katame pañca? Uppanno rāgo duppaṭivinodayo, uppanno doso duppaṭivinodayo, uppanno moho duppaṭivinodayo, uppannaṁ paṭibhānaṁ duppaṭivinodayaṁ, uppannaṁ gamikacittaṁ[392] duppaṭivinodayaṁ. Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca uppannā duppaṭivinodayā”ti.

928 Dasamaṁ.

929 Saddhammavaggo paṭhamo.
