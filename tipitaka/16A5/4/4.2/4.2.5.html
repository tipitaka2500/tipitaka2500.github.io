4.2.5 Pañhapucchāsutta

4.2.5. ปัญ๎หปุจ์ฉาสุต์ต

Catutthapaṇṇāsaka

Āghātavagga

Pañhapucchāsutta

165. 949 Tatra kho āyasmā sāriputto bhikkhū āmantesi…pe… “yo hi koci, āvuso, paraṁ pañhaṁ pucchati, sabbo so pañcahi ṭhānehi, etesaṁ vā aññatarena. Katamehi pañcahi? Mandattā momūhattā[409] paraṁ pañhaṁ pucchati, pāpiccho icchāpakato paraṁ pañhaṁ pucchati, paribhavaṁ paraṁ pañhaṁ pucchati, aññātukāmo paraṁ pañhaṁ pucchati, atha vā panevaṁcitto[410] paraṁ pañhaṁ pucchati— ‘sace me pañhaṁ puṭṭho sammadeva byākarissati iccetaṁ kusalaṁ, no ce[411] me pañhaṁ puṭṭho sammadeva byākarissati ahamassa sammadeva byākarissāmī’ti. Yo hi koci, āvuso, paraṁ pañhaṁ pucchati, sabbo so imehi pañcahi ṭhānehi, etesaṁ vā aññatarena. Ahaṁ kho panāvuso, evaṁcitto paraṁ pañhaṁ pucchāmi— ‘sace me pañhaṁ puṭṭho sammadeva byākarissati iccetaṁ kusalaṁ, no ce me pañhaṁ puṭṭho sammadeva byākarissati, ahamassa sammadeva byākarissāmī’”ti.

950 Pañcamaṁ.

  1. momūhattā: momuhattā (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. panevaṁcitto: atha vā pakuppanto (ka-sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. ce: no ca (syā1-3) (Word Variant) ↩
