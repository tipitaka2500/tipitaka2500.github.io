4.3.5 Caṇḍālasutta

4.3.5. จัณ์ฑาลสุต์ต

Catutthapaṇṇāsaka

Upāsakavagga

Caṇḍālasutta

175. 1020 [430] “Pañca[431]hi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato upāsako upāsakacaṇḍālo ca hoti upāsakamalañca upāsakapatikuṭṭho ca. Katamehi pañcahi? Assaddho hoti; dussīlo hoti; kotūhalamaṅgaliko hoti, maṅgalaṁ pacceti no kammaṁ; ito ca bahiddhā dakkhiṇeyyaṁ gavesati; tattha ca pubbakāraṁ karoti. Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato upāsako upāsakacaṇḍālo ca hoti upāsakamalañca upāsakapatikuṭṭho ca.

1021 Pañcahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato upāsako upāsakaratanañca hoti upāsakapadumañca upāsakapuṇḍarīkañca[432]. Katamehi pañcahi? Saddho hoti; sīlavā hoti; akotūhalamaṅgaliko hoti, kammaṁ pacceti no maṅgalaṁ; na ito bahiddhā dakkhiṇeyyaṁ gavesati; idha ca pubbakāraṁ karoti. Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato upāsako upāsakaratanañca hoti upāsakapadumañca upāsakapuṇḍarīkañcā”ti.

1022 Pañcamaṁ.

  1. upāsakacaṇḍālasuttaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. ca: upāsakapatikiṭṭho ca (sī, syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. upāsakapuṇḍarīkañca: upāsakapuṇḍarīko ca (pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
