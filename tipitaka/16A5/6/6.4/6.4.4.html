Rāgapeyyāla

306. 1409 “Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya pañca dhammā bhāvetabbā. Katame pañca? Saddhindriyaṁ, vīriyindriyaṁ, satindriyaṁ, samādhindriyaṁ, paññindriyaṁ— rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya ime pañca dhammā bhāvetabbā”ti. (4)
