Chaṭṭhapaṇṇāsaka

Rāgapeyyāla

303. 1406 [564] “Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya pañca dhammā bhāvetabbā. Katame pañca? Asubhasaññā, maraṇasaññā, ādīnavasaññā, āhāre paṭikūlasaññā, sabbaloke anabhiratasaññā[565]— rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya ime pañca dhammā bhāvetabbā”ti. (1)

  1. rāgādipeyyālaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. anabhiratasaññā: sabbatthapi evameva (Word Variant) ↩
