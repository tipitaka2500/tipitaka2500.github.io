Chaṭṭhapaṇṇāsaka

Rāgapeyyāla

304. 1407 “Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya pañca dhammā bhāvetabbā. Katame pañca? Aniccasaññā, anattasaññā, maraṇasaññā, āhāre paṭikūlasaññā, sabbaloke anabhiratasaññā— rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya ime pañca dhammā bhāvetabbā”ti. (2)
