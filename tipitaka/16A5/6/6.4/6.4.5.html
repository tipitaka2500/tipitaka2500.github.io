Rāgapeyyāla

307. 1410 “Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya pañca dhammā bhāvetabbā. Katame pañca? Saddhābalaṁ, vīriyabalaṁ, satibalaṁ, samādhibalaṁ, paññābalaṁ— rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya ime pañca dhammā bhāvetabbā”ti. (5)
