6.3.4. นิคัณ์สุต์ตาทิ

Chaṭṭhapaṇṇāsaka

Sikkhāpadapeyyāla

Nigaṇṭhasuttādi

294--302. 1403 “Pañcahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato nigaṇṭho… muṇḍasāvako… jaṭilako… paribbājako… māgaṇḍiko… tedaṇḍiko… āruddhako… gotamako… devadhammiko yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ niraye. Katamehi pañcahi? Pāṇātipātī hoti, adinnādāyī hoti…pe… surāmerayamajjapamādaṭṭhāyī hoti. Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato devadhammiko yathābhataṁ nikkhitto evaṁ niraye”ti.

1404 Sattarasamaṁ.

1405 Sikkhāpadapeyyālaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
