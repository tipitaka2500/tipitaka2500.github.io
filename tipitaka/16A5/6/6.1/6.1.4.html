Chaṭṭhapaṇṇāsaka

Upasampadāvagga

Pañcamacchariyasutta

254. 1357 “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, macchariyāni. Katamāni pañca? Āvāsamacchariyaṁ, kulamacchariyaṁ, lābhamacchariyaṁ, vaṇṇamacchariyaṁ, dhammamacchariyaṁ— imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañca macchariyāni. Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, pañcannaṁ macchariyānaṁ etaṁ paṭikuṭṭhaṁ[558], yadidaṁ dhammamacchariyan”ti.

1358 Catutthaṁ.

  1. paṭikuṭṭhaṁ: patikiṭṭhaṁ (sī, pā1), patikkiṭṭhaṁ (syā1-3, kaṃ), paṭikiṭṭhaṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
