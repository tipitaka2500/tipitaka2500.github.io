6.1.3 Sāmaṇerasutta

6.1.3. สามเณรสุต์ต

Chaṭṭhapaṇṇāsaka

Upasampadāvagga

Sāmaṇerasutta

253. 1355 [557] “Pañcahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgatena bhikkhunā sāmaṇero upaṭṭhāpetabbo. Katamehi pañcahi? Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu asekhena sīlakkhandhena samannāgato hoti; asekhena samādhikkhandhena… asekhena paññākkhandhena… asekhena vimuttikkhandhena… asekhena vimuttiñāṇadassanakkhandhena samannāgato hoti. Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgatena bhikkhunā sāmaṇero upaṭṭhāpetabbo”ti.

1356 Tatiyaṁ.
