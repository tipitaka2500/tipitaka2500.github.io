2.1.9 Paṭhamavuḍḍhapabbajitasutta

2.1.9. ปมวุฑ์ฒปัพ์พชิตสุต์ต

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Nīvaraṇavagga

Paṭhamavuḍḍhapabbajitasutta

59. 392 “Pañcahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato dullabho vuḍḍhapabbajito. Katamehi pañcahi? Dullabho, bhikkhave, vuḍḍhapabbajito nipuṇo, dullabho ākappasampanno, dullabho bahussuto, dullabho dhammakathiko, dullabho vinayadharo. Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato dullabho vuḍḍhapabbajitoti.

393 Navamaṁ.
