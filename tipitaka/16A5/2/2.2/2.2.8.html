2.2.8 Dutiyaiddhipādasutta

2.2.8. ทุติยอิท์ธิปาทสุต์ต

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Saññāvagga

Dutiyaiddhipādasutta

68. 416 “Pubbevāhaṁ, bhikkhave, sambodhā anabhisambuddho bodhisattova samāno pañca dhamme bhāvesiṁ, pañca dhamme bahulīkāsiṁ[205]. Katame pañca? Chandasamādhipadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṁ iddhipādaṁ bhāvesiṁ, vīriyasamādhi… cittasamādhi… vīmaṁsāsamādhipadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṁ iddhipādaṁ bhāvesiṁ, ussoḷhiññeva pañcamiṁ. So kho ahaṁ, bhikkhave, imesaṁ ussoḷhipañcamānaṁ dhammānaṁ bhāvitattā bahulīkatattā yassa yassa abhiññāsacchikaraṇīyassa dhammassa cittaṁ abhininnāmesiṁ abhiññāsacchikiriyāya, tatra tatreva sakkhibhabbataṁ pāpuṇiṁ sati sati āyatane.

417 So sace ākaṅkhiṁ— ‘anekavihitaṁ iddhividhaṁ paccanubhaveyyaṁ…pe… yāva brahmalokāpi kāyena vasaṁ vatteyyan’ti, tatra tatreva sakkhibhabbataṁ pāpuṇiṁ sati sati āyatane.

418 So sace ākaṅkhiṁ…pe… ‘āsavānaṁ khayā anāsavaṁ cetovimuttiṁ paññāvimuttiṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja vihareyyan’ti, tatra tatreva sakkhibhabbataṁ pāpuṇiṁ sati sati āyatane”ti.

419 Aṭṭhamaṁ.

  1. bahulīkāsiṁ: bahulimakāsiṁ (syā1-3, pā1, ka-ma), bahulamakāsiṁ (katthaci) (Word Variant) ↩
