2.2.9 Nibbidāsutta

2.2.9. นิพ์พิทาสุต์ต

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Saññāvagga

Nibbidāsutta

69. 420 “Pañcime, bhikkhave, dhammā bhāvitā bahulīkatā ekantanibbidāya virāgāya nirodhāya upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattanti.

421 Katame pañca? Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu asubhānupassī kāye viharati, āhāre paṭikūlasaññī, sabbaloke anabhiratasaññī[206], sabbasaṅkhāresu aniccānupassī, maraṇasaññā kho panassa ajjhattaṁ sūpaṭṭhitā hoti. Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca dhammā bhāvitā bahulīkatā ekantanibbidāya virāgāya nirodhāya upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattantī”ti.

422 Navamaṁ.

  1. anabhiratasaññī: anabhiratisaññī (ka-ma) 16A5:700-704, 16A5:1406-1407 (Word Variant) ↩
