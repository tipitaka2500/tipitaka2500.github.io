2.2.3 Paṭhamavaḍḍhisutta

2.2.3. ปมวัฑ์ฒิสุต์ต

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Saññāvagga

Paṭhamavaḍḍhisutta

63. 403 “Pañcahi, bhikkhave, vaḍḍhīhi vaḍḍhamāno ariyasāvako ariyāya vaḍḍhiyā vaḍḍhati, sārādāyī ca hoti varādāyī ca kāyassa. Katamāhi pañcahi? Saddhāya vaḍḍhati, sīlena vaḍḍhati, sutena vaḍḍhati, cāgena vaḍḍhati, paññāya vaḍḍhati— imāhi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi vaḍḍhīhi vaḍḍhamāno ariyasāvako ariyāya vaḍḍhiyā vaḍḍhati, sārādāyī ca hoti varādāyī ca kāyassā”ti.

404 “Saddhāya sīlena ca yo pavaḍḍhati[196],
Paññāya cāgena sutena cūbhayaṁ;
So tādiso sappuriso vicakkhaṇo,
Ādīyatī sāramidheva attano”ti.

405 Tatiyaṁ.

  1. pavaḍḍhati: yodha vaḍḍhati (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
