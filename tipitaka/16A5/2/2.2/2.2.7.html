2.2.7 Paṭhamaiddhipādasutta

2.2.7. ปมอิท์ธิปาทสุต์ต

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Saññāvagga

Paṭhamaiddhipādasutta

67. 413 “Yo hi koci, bhikkhave, bhikkhu vā bhikkhunī vā pañca dhamme[202] bhāveti, pañca dhamme[203] bahulīkaroti, tassa dvinnaṁ phalānaṁ aññataraṁ phalaṁ pāṭikaṅkhaṁ— diṭṭheva dhamme aññā, sati vā upādisese anāgāmitā.

414 Katame pañca? Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu chandasamādhipadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṁ iddhipādaṁ bhāveti, vīriyasamādhi…pe… cittasamādhi… vīmaṁsāsamādhipadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṁ iddhipādaṁ bhāveti, ussoḷhiññeva pañcamiṁ[204]. Yo hi koci, bhikkhave, bhikkhu vā bhikkhunī vā ime pañca dhamme bhāveti, ime pañca dhamme bahulīkaroti, tassa dvinnaṁ phalānaṁ aññataraṁ phalaṁ pāṭikaṅkhaṁ— diṭṭheva dhamme aññā, sati vā upādisese anāgāmitā”ti.

415 Sattamaṁ.

  1. dhamme: ime pañca dhamme (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. dhamme: ime pañca dhamme (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. pañcamiṁ: ussoḷhī yeva pañcamī (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
