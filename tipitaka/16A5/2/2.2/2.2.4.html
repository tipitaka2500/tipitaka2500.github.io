2.2.4. ทุติยวัฑ์ฒิสุต์ต

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Saññāvagga

Dutiyavaḍḍhisutta

64. 406 “Pañcahi, bhikkhave, vaḍḍhīhi vaḍḍhamānā ariyasāvikā ariyāya vaḍḍhiyā vaḍḍhati, sārādāyinī ca hoti varādāyinī ca kāyassa. Katamāhi pañcahi? Saddhāya vaḍḍhati, sīlena vaḍḍhati, sutena vaḍḍhati, cāgena vaḍḍhati, paññāya vaḍḍhati— imāhi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi vaḍḍhīhi vaḍḍhamānā ariyasāvikā ariyāya vaḍḍhiyā vaḍḍhati, sārādāyinī ca hoti varādāyinī ca kāyassāti.

407 Saddhāya sīlena ca yā pavaḍḍhati[197],
Paññāya cāgena sutena cūbhayaṁ;
Sā tādisī sīlavatī upāsikā,
Ādīyatī sāramidheva attano”ti.

408 Catutthaṁ.

  1. pavaḍḍhati: yādha vaḍḍhati (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
