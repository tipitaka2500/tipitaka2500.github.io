1.3.5 Tatiyasattakasutta

1.3.5. ตติยสัต์ตกสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Vajjisattakavagga

Tatiyasattakasutta

25. 133 “Satta vo, bhikkhave, aparihāniye dhamme desessāmi. Taṁ suṇātha, sādhukaṁ manasi karotha…pe… katame ca, bhikkhave, satta aparihāniyā dhammā? Yāvakīvañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhū saddhā bhavissanti; vuddhiyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhūnaṁ pāṭikaṅkhā, no parihāni.

134 Yāvakīvañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhū hirimanto[35] bhavissanti…pe… ottappino[36] bhavissanti… bahussutā bhavissanti… āraddhavīriyā bhavissanti… satimanto bhavissanti… paññavanto bhavissanti; vuddhiyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhūnaṁ pāṭikaṅkhā, no parihāni.

135 Yāvakīvañca, bhikkhave, ime satta aparihāniyā dhammā bhikkhūsu ṭhassanti, imesu ca sattasu aparihāniyesu dhammesu bhikkhū sandississanti; vuddhiyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhūnaṁ pāṭikaṅkhā, no parihānī”ti.

136 Pañcamaṁ.

  1. hirimanto: hirīmā (sī), hirimanā (7D:273) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. ottappino: ottāpino (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
