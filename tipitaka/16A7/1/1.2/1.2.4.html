1.2.4. ปุค์คลสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Anusayavagga

Puggalasutta

14. 66 [12] “Sattime, bhikkhave, puggalā āhuneyyā pāhuneyyā dakkhiṇeyyā añjalikaraṇīyā anuttaraṁ puññakkhettaṁ lokassa. Katame satta? Ubhatobhāgavimutto, paññāvimutto, kāyasakkhī, diṭṭhippatto[13], saddhāvimutto, dhammānusārī, saddhānusārī. Ime kho, bhikkhave, satta puggalā āhuneyyā pāhuneyyā dakkhiṇeyyā añjalikaraṇīyā anuttaraṁ puññakkhettaṁ lokassā”ti.

67 Catutthaṁ.

  1. āhuneyyapuggalasuttaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. diṭṭhippatto: diṭṭhappatto (sī, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
