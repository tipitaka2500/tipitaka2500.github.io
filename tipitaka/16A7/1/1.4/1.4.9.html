1.4.9 Paṭhamavasasutta

1.4.9. ปมวสสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Devatāvagga

Paṭhamavasasutta

40. 205 [61] [60] “Sattahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu cittaṁ vase[62] vatteti, no ca bhikkhu cittassa vasena vattati. Katamehi sattahi? Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu samādhikusalo hoti, samādhissa samāpattikusalo hoti, samādhissa ṭhitikusalo hoti, samādhissa vuṭṭhānakusalo hoti, samādhissa kalyāṇakusalo hoti, samādhissa gocarakusalo hoti, samādhissa abhinīhārakusalo hoti. Imehi kho, bhikkhave, sattahi dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu cittaṁ vase vatteti, no ca bhikkhu cittassa vasena vattatī”ti.
