2.6.2.

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Rāgapeyyāla

624. 676 “Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya satta dhammā bhāvetabbā. Katame satta? Aniccasaññā, anattasaññā, asubhasaññā, ādīnavasaññā, pahānasaññā, virāgasaññā, nirodhasaññā— rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya ime satta dhammā bhāvetabbā”ti.
