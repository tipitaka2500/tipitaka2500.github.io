127 Noganthañca nanoganthañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nogantho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Noganthañca nanoganthañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nanogantho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Noganthañca nanoganthañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nogantho ca nanogantho ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā. (3) (Saṁkhittaṁ.)
