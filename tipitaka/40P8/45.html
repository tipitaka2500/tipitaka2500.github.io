45. อัช์ฌัต์ติกทุก

Ajjhattikaduka

64. 196 Naajjhattikaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naajjhattiko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi.

197 Nabāhiraṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nabāhiro dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— paṭisandhikkhaṇe cittaṁ paṭicca ajjhattikaṁ kaṭattārūpaṁ. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

198 Hetuyā nava, ārammaṇe pañca…pe… avigate nava. (Sabbattha vitthāro.)
