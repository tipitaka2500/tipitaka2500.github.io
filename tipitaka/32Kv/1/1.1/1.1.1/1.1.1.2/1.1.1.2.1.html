Puggalakathā

Okāsasaccikaṭṭha

Anulomapaccanīka

11. 40 ๐ Puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenāti? Āmantā. Sabbattha puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenāti? Na hevaṁ vattabbe.

41 Ājānāhi niggahaṁ. Hañci puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthena, tena vata re vattabbe— “sabbattha puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā”ti. Yaṁ tattha vadesi— “vattabbe kho— ‘puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthena’, no ca vattabbe— ‘sabbattha puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā’”ti micchā.

42 No ce pana vattabbe— “sabbattha puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā”ti, no ca vata re vattabbe— “puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā”ti. Yaṁ tattha vadesi— “vattabbe kho— ‘puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthena’, no ca vattabbe— ‘sabbattha puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā’”ti micchā…pe… .

43 Tatiyo niggaho.
