Mahāpaṇṇāsaka

Paṭhamavagga

Puggalakathā

Avayavasaccikaṭṭha

Anulomapaccanīka

13. 48 ๐ Puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenāti? Āmantā. Sabbesu puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenāti? Na hevaṁ vattabbe.

49 Ājānāhi niggahaṁ. Hañci puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthena, tena vata re vattabbe— “sabbesu puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā”ti. Yaṁ tattha vadesi— “vattabbe kho— ‘puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthena’, no ca vattabbe— ‘sabbesu puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā’”ti micchā.

50 No ce pana vattabbe— “sabbesu puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā”ti, no ca vata re vattabbe— “puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā”ti. Yaṁ tattha vadesi— “vattabbe kho— ‘puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthena’, no ca vattabbe— ‘sabbesu puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā’”ti micchā…pe… .

51 Pañcamo niggaho.
