Mahāpaṇṇāsaka

Paṭhamavagga

Puggalakathā

Kālasaccikaṭṭha

Paccanīkānuloma

15. 56 × Puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenāti? Āmantā. Sabbadā puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenāti? Na hevaṁ vattabbe.

57 Ājānāhi niggahaṁ. Hañci puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthena, tena vata re vattabbe— “sabbadā puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā”ti. Yaṁ tattha vadesi— “vattabbe kho— ‘puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthena’, no ca vattabbe— ‘sabbadā puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā’”ti micchā.

58 No ce pana vattabbe— “sabbadā puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā”ti, no ca vata re vattabbe— “puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā”ti. Yaṁ tattha vadesi— “vattabbe kho— ‘puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthena’, no ca vattabbe— ‘sabbadā puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā’”ti micchā…pe… .

59 Sattamo niggaho.
