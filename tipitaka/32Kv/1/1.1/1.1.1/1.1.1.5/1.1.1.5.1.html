Puggalakathā

Okāsasaccikaṭṭha

Paccanīkānuloma

14. 52 × Puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenāti? Āmantā. Sabbattha puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenāti? Na hevaṁ vattabbe.

53 Ājānāhi niggahaṁ. Hañci puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthena, tena vata re vattabbe— “sabbattha puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā”ti. Yaṁ tattha vadesi— “vattabbe kho— ‘puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthena’, no ca vattabbe— ‘sabbattha puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā’”ti micchā.

54 No ce pana vattabbe— “sabbattha puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā”ti, no ca vata re vattabbe— “puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā”ti. Yaṁ tattha vadesi— “vattabbe kho— ‘puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthena’, no ca vattabbe— ‘sabbattha puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā’”ti micchā…pe… .

55 Chaṭṭho niggaho.
