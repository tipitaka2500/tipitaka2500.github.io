Mahāpaṇṇāsaka

Paṭhamavagga

Puggalakathā

Avayavasaccikaṭṭha

Paccanīkānuloma

16. 60 × Puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenāti? Āmantā. Sabbesu puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenāti? Na hevaṁ vattabbe.

61 Ājānāhi niggahaṁ. Hañci puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthena, tena vata re vattabbe— “sabbesu puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā”ti. Yaṁ tattha vadesi— “vattabbe kho— ‘puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthena’, no ca vattabbe— ‘sabbesu puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā’”ti micchā.

62 No ce pana vattabbe— “sabbesu puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā”ti, no ca vata re vattabbe— “puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā”ti. Yaṁ tattha vadesi— “vattabbe kho— ‘puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthena’, no ca vattabbe— ‘sabbesu puggalo nupalabbhati saccikaṭṭhaparamatthenā’”ti micchā…pe… .

63 Aṭṭhakaniggaho.
