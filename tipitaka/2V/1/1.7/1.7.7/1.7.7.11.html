Pacchatogamanasikkhāpada

649. 2062 Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū pacchato gacchantā purato gacchantassa dhammaṁ desenti…pe… .

2063 “Na pacchato gacchanto purato gacchantassa agilānassa dhammaṁ desessāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti. (71:216)

2064 Na pacchato gacchantena purato gacchantassa agilānassa dhammo desetabbo. Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca pacchato gacchanto purato gacchantassa agilānassa dhammaṁ deseti, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

2065 Anāpatti— asañcicca…pe… ādikammikassāti.

2066 Ekādasamasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
