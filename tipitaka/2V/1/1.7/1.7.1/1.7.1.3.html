Suppaṭicchannasikkhāpada

578. 1695 Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū kāyaṁ vivaritvā antaraghare gacchanti…pe… .

1696 “Suppaṭicchanno antaraghare gamissāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti. (3:148) [299]

1697 Suppaṭicchannena antaraghare gantabbaṁ. Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca kāyaṁ vivaritvā antaraghare gacchati, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

1698 Anāpatti— asañcicca, assatiyā, ajānantassa, gilānassa, āpadāsu, ummattakassa, ādikammikassāti.

1699 Tatiyasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
