Ukkuṭikasikkhāpada

600. 1805 Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū ukkuṭikāya antaraghare gacchanti…pe… .

1806 “Na ukkuṭikāya antaraghare gamissāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti. (25:170)

1807 Na ukkuṭikāya antaraghare gantabbaṁ. Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca ukkuṭikāya antaraghare gacchati, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

1808 Anāpatti— asañcicca…pe… ādikammikassāti.

1809 Pañcamasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
