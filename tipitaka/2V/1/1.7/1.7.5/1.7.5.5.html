Kabaḷavagga

Kabaḷāvacchedakasikkhāpada

621. 1909 Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū kabaḷāvacchedakaṁ bhuñjanti…pe… .

1910 “Na kabaḷāvacchedakaṁ bhuñjissāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti. (45:190)

1911 Na kabaḷāvacchedakaṁ bhuñjitabbaṁ. Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca kabaḷāvacchedakaṁ bhuñjati, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

1912 Anāpatti— asañcicca, assatiyā, ajānantassa, gilānassa, khajjake phalāphale, uttaribhaṅge, āpadāsu, ummattakassa, ādikammikassāti.

1913 Pañcamasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
