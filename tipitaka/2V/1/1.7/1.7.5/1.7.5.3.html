1900 “Na sakabaḷena mukhena byāharissāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti. (43:188)

1901 Na sakabaḷena mukhena byāharitabbaṁ. Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca sakabaḷena mukhena byāharati, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

1902 Anāpatti— asañcicca…pe… ādikammikassāti.

1903 Tatiyasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
