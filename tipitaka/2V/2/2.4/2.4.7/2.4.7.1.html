2.4.7.1 Gabbhinisikkhāpada

2.4.7.1. คัพ์ภินิสิก์ขาปท

Bhikkhunivibhaṅga

Pācittiyakaṇḍa

Gabbhinivagga

Gabbhinisikkhāpada

1067. 3181 Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena bhikkhuniyo gabbhiniṁ vuṭṭhāpenti. Sā piṇḍāya carati. Manussā evamāhaṁsu— “dethāyyāya bhikkhaṁ, garubhārā[495] ayyā”ti.

3182 Manussā ujjhāyanti khiyyanti vipācenti— “kathañhi nāma bhikkhuniyo gabbhiniṁ vuṭṭhāpessantī”ti.

3183 Assosuṁ kho bhikkhuniyo tesaṁ manussānaṁ ujjhāyantānaṁ khiyyantānaṁ vipācentānaṁ. Yā tā bhikkhuniyo appicchā…pe… tā ujjhāyanti khiyyanti vipācenti— “kathañhi nāma bhikkhuniyo gabbhiniṁ vuṭṭhāpessantī”ti…pe… “saccaṁ kira, bhikkhave, bhikkhuniyo gabbhiniṁ vuṭṭhāpentī”ti? “Saccaṁ, bhagavā”ti. Vigarahi buddho bhagavā…pe… kathañhi nāma, bhikkhave, bhikkhuniyo gabbhiniṁ vuṭṭhāpessanti. Netaṁ, bhikkhave, appasannānaṁ vā pasādāya…pe… evañca pana, bhikkhave, bhikkhuniyo imaṁ sikkhāpadaṁ uddisantu—

1068. 3184 “Yā pana bhikkhunī gabbhiniṁ vuṭṭhāpeyya, pācittiyan”ti. (61:116)

1069. 3185 Yā panāti yā yādisā…pe… bhikkhunīti…pe… ayaṁ imasmiṁ atthe adhippetā bhikkhunīti.

3186 Gabbhinī nāma āpannasattā vuccati. Vuṭṭhāpeyyāti upasampādeyya.

3187 “Vuṭṭhāpessāmī”ti gaṇaṁ vā ācariniṁ vā pattaṁ vā cīvaraṁ vā pariyesati, sīmaṁ vā sammannati, āpatti dukkaṭassa. Ñattiyā dukkaṭaṁ. Dvīhi kammavācāhi dukkaṭā. Kammavācāpariyosāne upajjhāyāya āpatti pācittiyassa. Gaṇassa ca ācariniyā ca āpatti dukkaṭassa.

1070. 3188 Gabbhiniyā gabbhinisaññā vuṭṭhāpeti, āpatti pācittiyassa. Gabbhiniyā vematikā vuṭṭhāpeti, āpatti dukkaṭassa. Gabbhiniyā agabbhinisaññā vuṭṭhāpeti, anāpatti. Agabbhiniyā gabbhinisaññā, āpatti dukkaṭassa. Agabbhiniyā vematikā, āpatti dukkaṭassa. Agabbhiniyā agabbhinisaññā, anāpatti.

1071. 3189 Anāpatti— gabbhiniṁ agabbhinisaññā vuṭṭhāpeti, agabbhiniṁ agabbhinisaññā vuṭṭhāpeti, ummattikāya, ādikammikāyāti.

3190 Paṭhamasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
