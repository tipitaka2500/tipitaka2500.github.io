2.4.1.4. ชตุมัฏ์กสิก์ขาปท

Bhikkhunivibhaṅga

Pācittiyakaṇḍa

Lasuṇavagga

Jatumaṭṭhakasikkhāpada

806. 2592 Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena aññatarā purāṇarājorodhā bhikkhunīsu pabbajitā[429] hoti. Aññatarā bhikkhunī anabhiratiyā pīḷitā yena sā bhikkhunī tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā taṁ bhikkhuniṁ etadavoca— “rājā kho, ayye, tumhe cirāciraṁ gacchati. Kathaṁ tumhe dhārethā”ti? “Jatumaṭṭhakena, ayye”ti. “Kiṁ etaṁ, ayye, jatumaṭṭhakan”ti? Atha kho sā bhikkhunī tassā bhikkhuniyā jatumaṭṭhakaṁ ācikkhi. Atha kho sā bhikkhunī jatumaṭṭhakaṁ ādiyitvā dhovituṁ vissaritvā ekamantaṁ chaḍḍesi. Bhikkhuniyo makkhikāhi samparikiṇṇaṁ passitvā evamāhaṁsu— “kassidaṁ kamman”ti? Sā evamāha— “mayhidaṁ kamman”ti.

2593 Yā tā bhikkhuniyo appicchā…pe… tā ujjhāyanti khiyyanti vipācenti— “kathañhi nāma bhikkhunī jatumaṭṭhakaṁ ādiyissatī”ti…pe… “saccaṁ kira, bhikkhave, bhikkhunī jatumaṭṭhakaṁ ādiyatī”ti? “Saccaṁ, bhagavā”ti. Vigarahi buddho bhagavā…pe… kathañhi nāma, bhikkhave, bhikkhunī jatumaṭṭhakaṁ ādiyissati. Netaṁ, bhikkhave, appasannānaṁ vā pasādāya…pe… evañca pana, bhikkhave, bhikkhuniyo imaṁ sikkhāpadaṁ uddisantu— [430]

807. 2594 “Jatumaṭṭhake pācittiyan”ti. (4:59) [431]

808. 2595 Jatumaṭṭhakaṁ nāma jatumayaṁ kaṭṭhamayaṁ piṭṭhamayaṁ mattikāmayaṁ.

2596 Ādiyeyyāti samphassaṁ sādiyantī antamaso uppalapattampi muttakaraṇaṁ paveseti, āpatti pācittiyassa.

809. 2597 Anāpatti— ābādhapaccayā, ummattikāya, ādikammikāyāti.

2598 Catutthasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.

  2. ādiyatī"ti: ādiyīti (sī, pā1, cha1) (Word Variant) ↩
