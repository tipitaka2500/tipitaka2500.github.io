6.2.1 Kharaputtajātaka

6.2.1. ขรปุต์ตชาตก

Chakkanipāta

Kharaputtavagga

Kharaputtajātaka

77. 1406 “Saccaṁ kirevamāhaṁsu,
vastaṁ bāloti paṇḍitā;
Passa bālo rahokammaṁ,
āvikubbaṁ na bujjhati”. [813]

78. 1407 “Tvaṁ khopi[814] samma bālosi,
kharaputta vijānahi;
Rajjuyā hi[815] parikkhitto,
vaṅkoṭṭho ohitomukho.

79. 1408 Aparampi samma te bālyaṁ[816],
yo mutto na palāyasi;
So ca bālataro samma,
yaṁ tvaṁ vahasi senakaṁ”.

80. 1409 “Yannu samma ahaṁ bālo,
ajarāja vijānahi;
Atha kena senako bālo,
taṁ me akkhāhi pucchito”.

81. 1410 “Uttamatthaṁ labhitvāna,
bhariyāya yo padassati[817];
Tena jahissatattānaṁ,
sā cevassa na hessati”.

82. 1411 “Na ve piyammeti[818] janinda tādiso,
Attaṁ niraṁkatvā piyāni sevati[819];
Attāva seyyo paramā ca seyyo,
Labbhā piyā ocitatthena pacchā”ti.

1412 Kharaputtajātakaṁ[820] paṭhamaṁ.

  1. vastaṁ: bhastaṁ (sī, pā1), kalakaṁ (syā2, syā3), garaṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. khopi: tvaṁ nu kho (sī, syā2, syā3), tvaṁ ca kho (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. hi: rajjuyāhi (syā2, syā3), rajjuyāsi (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. bālyaṁ: ayampi samma te bālo (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. padassati: bhariyā yo padassati (pā1), bhariyā na bhavissati (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  6. piyammeti: na piyammeti (syā2, syā3), na piyameti (ka-ma), na piyamedanti (katthaci) (Word Variant) ↩
  7. sevati: sevaye (?) (Word Variant) ↩
  8. Kharaputtajātakaṁ: khuraputtajātakaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
