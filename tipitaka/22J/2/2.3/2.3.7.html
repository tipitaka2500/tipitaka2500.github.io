2.3.7 Tindukajātaka

2.3.7. ติน์ทุกชาตก

Dukanipāta

Kalyāṇavagga

Tindukajātaka

53. 424 “Dhanuhatthakalāpehi,
nettiṁ savaradhāribhi;
Samantā parikiṇṇamha,
kathaṁ mokkho bhavissati”.

54. 425 “Appeva bahukiccānaṁ,
attho jāyetha koci naṁ;
Atthi rukkhassa acchinnaṁ,
khajjathaññeva tindukan”ti.

426 Tindukajātakaṁ[208] sattamaṁ.
