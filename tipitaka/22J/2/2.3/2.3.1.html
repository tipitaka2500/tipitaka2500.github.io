2.3.1 Kalyāṇadhammajātaka

2.3.1. กัล๎ยาณธัม์มชาตก

Dukanipāta

Kalyāṇavagga

Kalyāṇadhammajātaka

41. 406 “Kalyāṇadhammoti yadā janinda,
Loke samaññaṁ anupāpuṇāti;
Tasmā na hiyyetha[201] naro sapañño,
Hiriyāpi santo dhuramādiyanti.

42. 407 Sāyaṁ samaññā idha majja pattā,
Kalyāṇadhammoti janinda loke;
Tāhaṁ samekkhaṁ idha pabbajissaṁ,
Na hi matthi chando idha kāmabhoge”ti.

408 Kalyāṇadhammajātakaṁ paṭhamaṁ.

  1. hiyyetha: hīyetha (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
