2.3.8 Kacchapajātaka

2.3.8. กัจ์ฉปชาตก

Dukanipāta

Kalyāṇavagga

Kacchapajātaka

55. 427 “Janittaṁ me[209] bhavittaṁ me,
iti paṅke avassayiṁ;
Taṁ maṁ paṅko ajjhabhavi,
yathā dubbalakaṁ tathā;
Taṁ taṁ vadāmi bhaggava,
suṇohi vacanaṁ mama”.

56. 428 “Gāme vā yadi vā raññe,
sukhaṁ yatrādhigacchati;
Taṁ janittaṁ bhavittañca,
purisassa pajānato;
Yamhi jīve tamhi gacche,
na niketahato siyā”ti.

429 Kacchapajātakaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.

  1. me: janitaṁ me bhavitaṁ me (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
