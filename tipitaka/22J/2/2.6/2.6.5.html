2.6.5 Gaṅgeyyajātaka

2.6.5. คํเคย์ยชาตก

Dukanipāta

Nataṃdaḷhavagga

Gaṅgeyyajātaka

109. 518 “Sobhati maccho gaṅgeyyo,
atho sobhati yāmuno[257];
Catuppadoyaṁ puriso,
nigrodhaparimaṇḍalo;
Īsakāyatagīvo ca,
sabbeva atirocati”. [258]

110. 519 “Yaṁ pucchito na taṁ akkhāsi[259],
Aññaṁ akkhāsi[260] pucchito;
Attappasaṁsako poso,
Nāyaṁ asmāka ruccatī”ti.

520 Gaṅgeyyajātakaṁ pañcamaṁ.

  1. yāmuno: sobhanti macchā gaṅgeyyā, atho sobhanti yāmunā (syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Īsakāyatagīvo: īsamāyata (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. akkhāsi: akkhā (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. akkhāsi: akkhāti (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
