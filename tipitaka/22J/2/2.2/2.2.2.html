2.2.2. สัน์ถวชาตก

Dukanipāta

Santhavavagga

Santhavajātaka

23. 377 “Na santhavasmā paramatthi pāpiyo,
Yo santhavo[187] kāpurisena hoti;
Santappito sappinā pāyasena[188],
Kicchākataṁ paṇṇakuṭiṁ adayhi[189].

24. 378 Na santhavasmā paramatthi seyyo,
Yo santhavo sappurisena hoti;
Sīhassa byagghassa ca dīpino ca,
Sāmā mukhaṁ lehati santhavenā”ti.

379 Santhavajātakaṁ dutiyaṁ.

  1. santhavo: sandhavo (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. pāyasena: pāyāsena (syā2, syā3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. adayhi: adaḍḍhahi (sī, syā2, syā3), adaṭṭhahi (pā1), adaddahi (?) (Word Variant) ↩
