2.2.6 Upasāḷakajātaka

2.2.6. อุปสาฬกชาตก

Dukanipāta

Santhavavagga

Upasāḷakajātaka

31. 389 “Upasāḷakanāmāni,
sahassāni catuddasa;
Asmiṁ padese daḍḍhāni,
natthi loke anāmataṁ. [195]

32. 390 Yamhi saccañca dhammo ca,
ahiṁsā saṁyamo damo;
Etaṁ ariyā[196] sevanti,
etaṁ loke anāmatan”ti.

391 Upasāḷakajātakaṁ[197] chaṭṭhaṁ.

  1. "Upasāḷakanāmāni: upasāḷhakanāmānaṁ (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. ariyā: etadariyā (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Upasāḷakajātakaṁ: upasāḷhajātakaṁ (sī, pā1), upasāḷhakajātakaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
