2.2.10 Kakaṇṭakajātaka

2.2.10. กกัณ์ฏกชาตก

Dukanipāta

Santhavavagga

Kakaṇṭakajātaka

39. 401 “Nāyaṁ pure uṇṇamati[200],
toraṇagge kakaṇṭako;
Mahosadha vijānāhi,
kena thaddho kakaṇṭako”.

40. 402 “Aladdhapubbaṁ laddhāna,
aḍḍhamāsaṁ kakaṇṭako;
Atimaññati rājānaṁ,
vedehaṁ mithilaggahan”ti.

403 Kakaṇṭakajātakaṁ dasamaṁ.
 Santhavavaggo dutiyo.

404

405 Atha indasamāna sapaṇṇakuṭi,
Susimuttama gijjha jalābujako;
Upasāḷaka bhikkhu salāpavaro,
Atha mettavaro dasapuṇṇamatīti.

  1. uṇṇamati: unnamati (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
