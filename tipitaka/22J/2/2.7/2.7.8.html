2.7.8 Kūṭavāṇijajātaka

2.7.8. กูฏวาณิชชาตก

Dukanipāta

Bīraṇathambhavagga

Kūṭavāṇijajātaka

135. 559 “Saṭhassa sāṭheyyamidaṁ sucintitaṁ,
Paccoḍḍitaṁ paṭikūṭassa kūṭaṁ;
Phālañce khādeyyuṁ[282] mūsikā,
Kasmā kumāraṁ kulalā na[283] hareyyuṁ.

136. 560 Kūṭassa hi santi[284] kūṭakūṭā,
Bhavati cāpi nikatino nikatyā;
Dehi puttanaṭṭha phālanaṭṭhassa phālaṁ,
Mā te puttamahāsi phālanaṭṭho”ti. [285]

561 Kūṭavāṇijajātakaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.

  1. khādeyyuṁ: adeyyuṁ (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. na: no (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. santi: santīdha (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. Bhavati: bhavanti (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
