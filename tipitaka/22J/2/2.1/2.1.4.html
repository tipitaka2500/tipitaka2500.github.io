2.1.4. อุรคชาตก

Dukanipāta

Daḷhavagga

Uragajātaka

7. 349 “Idhūragānaṁ pavaro paviṭṭho,
Selassa vaṇṇena pamokkhamicchaṁ;
Brahmañca vaṇṇaṁ[171] apacāyamāno,
Bubhukkhito no vitarāmi[172] bhottuṁ”.

8. 350 “So brahmagutto cirameva jīva,
Dibyā ca te pātubhavantu bhakkhā;
Yo brahmavaṇṇaṁ apacāyamāno,
Bubhukkhito no vitarāsi[173] bhottun”ti.

351 Uragajātakaṁ catutthaṁ.

  1. vaṇṇaṁ: vakkaṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. vitarāmi: visahāmi (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. vitarāsi: visahāsi (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
