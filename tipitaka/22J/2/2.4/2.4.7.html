2.4.7 Catumaṭṭhajātaka

2.4.7. จตุมัฏ์ฐชาตก

Dukanipāta

Asadisavagga

Catumaṭṭhajātaka

73. 456 “Ucce viṭabhimāruyha,
mantayavho rahogatā;
Nīce oruyha mantavho,
migarājāpi sossati.

74. 457 Yaṁ suvaṇṇo suvaṇṇena[225],
devo devena mantaye;
Kiṁ tettha catumaṭṭhassa,
bilaṁ pavisa jambukā”ti.

458 Catumaṭṭhajātakaṁ[226] sattamaṁ.

  1. suvaṇṇena: yaṁ supaṇṇo supaṇṇena (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Catumaṭṭhajātakaṁ: catumaṭṭajātakaṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
