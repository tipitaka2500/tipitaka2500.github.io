2.8.9 Palāyitajātaka

2.8.9. ปลายิตชาตก

Dukanipāta

Kāsāvavagga

Palāyitajātaka

157. 594 “Gajaggameghehi hayaggamālibhi,
Rathūmijātehi sarābhivassebhi[302];
Tharuggahāvaṭṭadaḷhappahāribhi,
Parivāritā takkasilā samantato. [303]

158. 595 [304] Abhidhāvatha cūpadhāvatha ca[305],
Vividhā vināditā[306] vadantibhi;
Vattatajja tumulo ghoso yathā,
Vijjulatā jaladharassa gajjato”ti. [307]

596 Palāyitajātakaṁ[308] navamaṁ.

  1. sarābhivassebhi: sarābhivassabhi (sī, syā2, syā3), sarābhivassehi (pā1), sarābhivassibhi (?) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Tharuggahāvaṭṭadaḷhappahāribhi: dhanuggahāvaṭṭa ... (syā2, syā3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. (Open) ↩
  4. ca: abhidhāvathā cuppatathā ca (syā2, syā3), abhidhāvathā ca patathā ca (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. vināditā: vividhavinaditā (syā2, syā3), vināditattha (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  6. gajjato"ti: abhidhāvathā ca patathā ca, vividhavinaditā ca dantibhi. vattatajja tumulo ghoso, yathā vijjutā jaladharassa gajjatoti. (sī, pā1, ka-ma) (Close) ↩
  7. Palāyitajātakaṁ: palāsijātakaṁ (sī), palāyijātakaṁ (syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
