7.1.7 Sattubhastajātaka

7.1.7. สัต์ตุภัส๎ตชาตก

Sattakanipāta

Kukkuvagga

Sattubhastajātaka

46. 1536 “Vibbhantacitto kupitindriyosi,
Nettehi te vārigaṇā savanti;
Kiṁ te naṭṭhaṁ kiṁ pana patthayāno,
Idhāgamā brahme tadiṅgha[895] brūhi”.

47. 1537 “Miyyetha bhariyā vajato mamajja,
Agacchato maraṇamāha yakkho;
Etena dukkhena pavedhitosmi,
Akkhāhi me senaka etamatthaṁ”.

48. 1538 “Bahūni ṭhānāni vicintayitvā,
Yamettha vakkhāmi tadeva saccaṁ;
Maññāmi te brāhmaṇa sattubhastaṁ,
Ajānato kaṇhasappo paviṭṭho.

49. 1539 Ādāya daṇḍaṁ parisumbha bhastaṁ,
Passeḷamūgaṁ uragaṁ dujivhaṁ[896];
Chindajja kaṅkhaṁ vicikicchitāni,
Bhujaṅgamaṁ passa pamuñca bhastaṁ”.

50. 1540 Saṁviggarūpo parisāya majjhe,
So brāhmaṇo sattubhastaṁ pamuñci;
Atha nikkhami urago uggatejo,
Āsīviso sappo phaṇaṁ karitvā.

51. 1541 “Suladdhalābhā janakassa rañño,
Yo passatī senakaṁ sādhupaññaṁ;
Vivaṭṭachaddo nusi sabbadassī,
Ñāṇaṁ nu te brāhmaṇa bhiṁsarūpaṁ”. [897]

52. 1542 “Imāni me sattasatāni atthi,
Gaṇhāhi sabbāni dadāmi tuyhaṁ;
Tayā hi me jīvitamajja laddhaṁ,
Athopi bhariyāya makāsi sotthiṁ”.

53. 1543 “Na paṇḍitā vetanamādiyanti,
Citrāhi gāthāhi subhāsitāhi;
Itopi te brahme dadantu vittaṁ,
Ādāya tvaṁ gaccha sakaṁ niketan”ti.

1544 Sattubhastajātakaṁ[898] sattamaṁ.

  1. tadiṅgha: brāhmaṇa iṅgha (sī, syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. dujivhaṁ: dvijivhaṁ (sī), dijivhaṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Vivaṭṭachaddo: vivattacchaddo (sī), vivaṭṭacchado (syā2, syā3), vivattacchaddā (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. Sattubhastajātakaṁ: senakajātakaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
