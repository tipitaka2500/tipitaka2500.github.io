4.1.9 Chavakajātaka

4.1.9. ฉวกชาตก

Catukkanipāta

Kāliṅgavagga

Chavakajātaka

33. 923 [508] “Sabbamidaṁ carimaṁ kataṁ[507],
Ubho dhammaṁ na passare;
Ubho pakatiyā cutā,
Yo cāyaṁ mantejjhāpeti[509];
Yo ca mantaṁ adhīyati”.

34. 924 “Sālīnaṁ odanaṁ bhuñje,
suciṁ maṁsūpasecanaṁ;
Tasmā etaṁ na sevāmi,
dhammaṁ isīhi sevitaṁ”.

35. 925 “Paribbaja mahā loko[510],
pacantaññepi pāṇino;
Mā taṁ adhammo ācarito,
asmā kumbhamivābhidā.

36. 926 Dhiratthu taṁ yasalābhaṁ,
dhanalābhañca brāhmaṇa;
Yā vutti vinipātena,
adhammacaraṇena vā”ti.

927 Chavakajātakaṁ navamaṁ. [511]

  1. kataṁ: carimavataṁ (sī, pā1), ca marikataṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. (gāthā 33--35) 2V:2047 (Cross Reference) ↩
  3. mantejjhāpeti: sajjhāpayati (sī, pā1), mantajjhāyati (ka-sī), mante jhāpeti (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. loko: mahābrahme (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. Dhavakajātakaṁ: chavajātakaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
