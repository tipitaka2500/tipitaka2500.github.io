4.3.4 Cammasāṭakajātaka

4.3.4. จัม์มสาฏกชาตก

Catukkanipāta

Kuṭidūsakavagga

Cammasāṭakajātaka

93. 1002 “Kalyāṇarūpo vatayaṁ catuppado,
Subhaddako ceva supesalo ca;
Yo brāhmaṇaṁ jātimantūpapannaṁ,
Apacāyati meṇḍavaro yasassī”.

94. 1003 “Mā brāhmaṇa ittaradassanena,
Vissāsamāpajji catuppadassa;
Daḷhappahāraṁ abhikaṅkhamāno[560],
Avasakkatī dassati suppahāraṁ”.

95. 1004 Ūruṭṭhi bhaggaṁ[562] pavaṭṭito[563] khāribhāro,
Sabbañca bhaṇḍaṁ brāhmaṇassa[564] bhinnaṁ;
Ubhopi bāhā paggayha[565] kandati,
“Abhidhāvatha haññate brahmacārī. [561]

96. 1005 Evaṁ so nihato seti,
yo apūjaṁ pasaṁsati[566];
Yathāhamajja pahato,
hato meṇḍena dummatī”ti.

1006 Cammasāṭakajātakaṁ catutthaṁ.

  1. abhikaṅkhamāno: abhikattukāmo (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Ūruṭṭhi: ūraṭṭhi (sī), uruṭṭhī (syā2, syā3), satthi (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. bhaggaṁ: bhaggā (syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. pavaṭṭito: patito (sī, syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. brāhmaṇassa: brāhmaṇassīdha (sī, pā1), brāhmaṇasseva (ka-sī, syā2, syā3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  6. paggayha: paggayheva (syā2, syā3), paggahīya (?) (Word Variant) ↩
  7. pasaṁsati: namassati (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
