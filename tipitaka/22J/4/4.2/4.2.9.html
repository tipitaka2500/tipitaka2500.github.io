4.2.9 Tittirajātaka

4.2.9. ติต์ติรชาตก

Catukkanipāta

Pucimandavagga

Tittirajātaka

73. 975 “Susukhaṁ vata jīvāmi,
labhāmi ceva bhuñjituṁ;
Paripanthe ca tiṭṭhāmi,
kā nu bhante gatī mama”.

74. 976 “Mano ce te nappaṇamati,
pakkhi pāpassa kammuno;
Abyāvaṭassa bhadrassa,
na pāpamupalimpati”.

75. 977 “Ñātako no nisinnoti,
bahu āgacchate jano;
Paṭicca kammaṁ phusati,
tasmiṁ me saṅkate mano”.

76. 978 “Na paṭicca kammaṁ phusati,
mano ce nappadussati;
Appossukkassa bhadrassa,
na pāpamupalimpatī”ti.

979 Tittirajātakaṁ navamaṁ.
