Catukkanipāta

Pucimandavagga

Pucimandajātaka

41. 935 “Uṭṭhehi cora kiṁ sesi,
ko attho supanena[516] te;
Mā taṁ gahesuṁ[517] rājāno,
gāme kibbisakārakaṁ”.

42. 936 “Yannu coraṁ gahessanti,
gāme kibbisakārakaṁ;
Kiṁ tattha pucimandassa,
vane jātassa tiṭṭhato”. [518]

43. 937 “Na tvaṁ assattha jānāsi,
mama corassa cantaraṁ;
Coraṁ gahetvā rājāno,
gāme kibbisakārakaṁ;
Appenti nimbasūlasmiṁ,
tasmiṁ me saṅkate mano”. [519]

44. 938 “Saṅkeyya saṅkitabbāni,
rakkheyyānāgataṁ bhayaṁ;
Anāgatabhayā dhīro,
ubho loke avekkhatī”ti.

939 Pucimandajātakaṁ paṭhamaṁ.

  1. supanena: supinena (sī, pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. gahesuṁ: gahesu (syā2, syā3), gaṇheyyuṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. "Yannu: nanu (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. Appenti: accenti (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
