9.1.11 Pūtimaṃsajātaka

9.1.11. ปูติมํสชาตก

Navakanipāta

Gijjhavagga

Pūtimaṃsajātaka

96. 1886 “Na kho me ruccati āḷi,
pūtimaṁsassa pekkhanā;
Etādisā sakhārasmā,
ārakā parivajjaye”.

97. 1887 “Ummattikā ayaṁ veṇī,
vaṇṇeti patino sakhiṁ;
Pajjhāyi paṭigacchantiṁ,
āgataṁ meṇḍamātaraṁ[1094]”. [1093]

98. 1888 “Tvaṁ khosi samma ummatto,
dummedho avicakkhaṇo;
Yo tvaṁ[1095] matālayaṁ katvā,
akālena vipekkhasi”.

99. 1889 “Na akāle vipekkheyya,
kāle pekkheyya paṇḍito;
Pūtimaṁsova pajjhāyi[1096],
yo akāle vipekkhati”.

100. 1890 “Piyaṁ kho āḷi me hotu,
puṇṇapattaṁ dadāhi me;
Pati sañjīvito mayhaṁ,
eyyāsi piyapucchikā[1097]”.

101. 1891 “Piyaṁ kho āḷi te hotu,
puṇṇapattaṁ dadāmi te;
Mahatā parivārena[1098],
essaṁ kayirāhi[1100] bhojanaṁ”. [1099]

102. 1892 “Kīdiso tuyhaṁ parivāro,
yesaṁ kāhāmi bhojanaṁ;
Kiṁnāmakā ca te sabbe,
taṁ me akkhāhi pucchitā”. [1101]

103. 1893 “Māliyo caturakkho ca,
piṅgiyo atha jambuko;
Ediso mayhaṁ parivāro,
tesaṁ kayirāhi[1102] bhojanaṁ”.

104. 1894 “Nikkhantāya agārasmā,
bhaṇḍakampi vinassati;
Ārogyaṁ āḷino vajjaṁ[1103],
idheva vasa māgamā”ti.

1895 Pūtimaṁsajātakaṁ ekādasamaṁ.

  1. Pajjhāyi: pajjhāti (sī, pā1), pajjhāyati (sī -nissya) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. meṇḍamātaraṁ: meḷamātaraṁ (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. tvaṁ: so tvaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. pajjhāyi: pajjhāti (sī, pā1), pajjhāyati (sī -nissya) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. piyapucchikā: pucchitā (syā2, syā3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  6. parivārena: parihārena (syā2) (Word Variant) ↩
  7. essaṁ: esaṁ (ka-sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  8. kayirāhi: kayirāsi (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  9. taṁ: te (ka-sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  10. kayirāhi: kayirāsi (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  11. vajjaṁ: vacchaṁ (?) (Word Variant) ↩
