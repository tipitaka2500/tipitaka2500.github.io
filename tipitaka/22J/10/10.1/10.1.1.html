10.1.1 Catudvārajātaka

10.1.1. จตุท๎วารชาตก

Dasakanipāta

Catudvāravagga

Catudvārajātaka

1. 1909 “Catudvāramidaṁ nagaraṁ,
āyasaṁ daḷhapākāraṁ;
Oruddhapaṭiruddhosmi,
kiṁ pāpaṁ pakataṁ mayā”.

2. 1910 “Sabbe apihitā dvārā,
oruddhosmi yathā dijo;
Kimādhikaraṇaṁ yakkha,
cakkābhinihato ahaṁ.

3. 1911 Laddhā satasahassāni,
atirekāni vīsati;
Anukampakānaṁ ñātīnaṁ,
vacanaṁ samma nākari.

4. 1912 Laṅghiṁ samuddaṁ pakkhandi,
sāgaraṁ appasiddhikaṁ;
Catubbhi aṭṭhajjhagamā,
aṭṭhāhipi ca soḷasa.

5. 1913 Soḷasāhi ca bāttiṁsa,
atricchaṁ cakkamāsado;
Icchāhatassa posassa,
cakkaṁ bhamati matthake.

6. 1914 Uparivisālā duppūrā,
icchā visaṭagāminī[1110];
Ye ca taṁ anugijjhanti,
te honti cakkadhārino.

7. 1915 Bahubhaṇḍaṁ avahāya,
maggaṁ appaṭivekkhiya;
Yesañcetaṁ asaṅkhātaṁ,
te honti cakkadhārino. [1111]

8. 1916 Kammaṁ samekkhe vipulañca bhogaṁ,
Icchaṁ na seveyya anatthasaṁhitaṁ;
Kareyya vākyaṁ anukampakānaṁ,
Taṁ tādisaṁ nātivatteyya cakkaṁ”.

9. 1917 “Kīvaciraṁ nu me yakkha,
cakkaṁ sirasi ṭhassati;
Kati vassasahassāni,
taṁ me akkhāhi pucchito”.

10. 1918 “Atisaro paccasaro[1112],
mittavinda suṇohi me;
Cakkaṁ te sirasi[1113] māviddhaṁ,
na taṁ jīvaṁ pamokkhasī”ti.

1919 Catudvārajātakaṁ paṭhamaṁ.

  1. visaṭagāminī: visatagāminaṁ (syā2, syā3), visaṭagāminiṁ (pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Bahubhaṇḍaṁ: bahuṁ bhaṇḍaṁ (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. paccasaro: accasaro (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. sirasi: sira (sī), sirasmi (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
