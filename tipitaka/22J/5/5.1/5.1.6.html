5.1.6 Koraṇḍiyajātaka

5.1.6. โกรัณ์ฑิยชาตก

Pañcakanipāta

Maṇikuṇḍalavagga

Koraṇḍiyajātaka

34. 1184 “Eko araññe girikandarāyaṁ,
Paggayha paggayha silaṁ pavecchasi[675];
Punappunaṁ santaramānarūpo,
Koraṇḍiya ko nu tava yidhattho”. [676]

35. 1185 “Ahañhimaṁ sāgara sevitantaṁ,
Samaṁ karissāmi yathāpi pāṇi;
Vikiriya sānūni ca pabbatāni ca,
Tasmā silaṁ dariyā pakkhipāmi”.

36. 1186 “Nayimaṁ mahiṁ arahati pāṇikappaṁ,
Samaṁ manusso karaṇāya meko;
Maññāmimaññeva dariṁ jigīsaṁ[677],
Koraṇḍiya hāhasi[678] jīvalokaṁ”.

37. 1187 “Sace ahaṁ[679] bhūtadharaṁ na sakkā[680],
Samaṁ manusso karaṇāya meko;
Evameva tvaṁ brahme ime manusse,
Nānādiṭṭhike nānayissasi te[681]”.

38. 1188 “Saṁkhittarūpena bhavaṁ mamatthaṁ,
Akkhāsi koraṇḍiya evametaṁ;
Yathā na sakkā pathavī samāyaṁ,
Kattuṁ manussena tathā manussā”ti.

1189 Koraṇḍiyajātakaṁ[682] chaṭṭhaṁ.

  1. pavecchasi: pavejjhasi (sī-a, syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Koraṇḍiya: kāraṇḍiya (sī, pā1), kārandiya (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. jigīsaṁ: jigiṁsaṁ (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. hāhasi: hāyasi (sī, syā2, syā3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. ahaṁ: ayaṁ (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  6. sakkā: sakko (sī, syā2, syā3, ka-ma), sattho (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  7. te: ne (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  8. Koraṇḍiyajātakaṁ: kāraṇḍiyajātakaṁ (sī, pā1), kārandiyajātakaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
