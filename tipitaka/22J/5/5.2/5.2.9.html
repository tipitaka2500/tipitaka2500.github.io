5.2.9 Mittavindakajātaka

5.2.9. มิต์ตวิน์ทกชาตก

Pañcakanipāta

Vaṇṇārohavagga

Mittavindakajātaka

100. 1265 “Kyāhaṁ devānamakaraṁ,
kiṁ pāpaṁ pakataṁ mayā;
Yaṁ me sirasmiṁ ohacca[723],
cakkaṁ bhamati matthake”.

101. 1266 “Atikkamma ramaṇakaṁ,
sadāmattañca dūbhakaṁ;
Brahmattarañca pāsādaṁ,
kenatthena idhāgato”.

102. 1267 “Ito bahutarā bhogā,
atra maññe bhavissare;
Iti etāya saññāya,
passa maṁ byasanaṁ gataṁ”.

103. 1268 “Catubbhi aṭṭhajjhagamā,
aṭṭhāhipi ca[724] soḷasa;
Soḷasāhi ca bāttiṁsa,
atricchaṁ cakkamāsado;
Icchāhatassa posassa,
cakkaṁ bhamati matthake.

104. 1269 Uparivisālā duppūrā[725],
icchā visaṭagāminī[726];
Ye ca taṁ anugijjhanti,
te honti cakkadhārino”ti.

1270 Mittavindakajātakaṁ[727] navamaṁ.

  1. ohacca: ūhacca (pā1), uhacca (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. ca: aṭṭhakābhi (sī), aṭṭhābhi cāpi (syā2, syā3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. duppūrā: uparivisālaṁ duppūraṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. visaṭagāminī: icchā visadagāminī (sī), icchāvisaṭagāminiṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. Mittavindakajātakaṁ: mittavindukajātakaṁ (syā2, syā3), mittavindajātakaṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
