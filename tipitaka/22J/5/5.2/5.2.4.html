5.2.4 Khajjopanakajātaka

5.2.4. ขัช์โชปนกชาตก

Pañcakanipāta

Vaṇṇārohavagga

Khajjopanakajātaka

75. 1235 “Ko nu santamhi pajjote,
aggipariyesanaṁ caraṁ;
Addakkhi ratti[701] khajjotaṁ,
jātavedaṁ amaññatha.

76. 1236 Svassa gomayacuṇṇāni,
abhimatthaṁ tiṇāni ca;
Viparītāya saññāya,
nāsakkhi pajjaletave.

77. 1237 Evampi anupāyena,
atthaṁ na labhate migo[702];
Visāṇato gavaṁ dohaṁ,
yattha khīraṁ na vindati.

78. 1238 Vividhehi upāyehi,
atthaṁ papponti māṇavā;
Niggahena amittānaṁ,
mittānaṁ paggahena ca.

79. 1239 Senāmokkhapalābhena,
vallabhānaṁ nayena ca;
Jagatiṁ jagatipālā,
āvasanti vasundharan”ti. [703]

1240 Khajjopanakajātakaṁ[704] catutthaṁ.

  1. ratti: rattiṁ (sī, syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. migo: mago (sī), mūgo (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Senāmokkhapalābhena: seṇimokkho palābhena (sī), senīmokkhūpalābhena (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. Khajjopanakajātakaṁ: khajjopaṇakapañho (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
