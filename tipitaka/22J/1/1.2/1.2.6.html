1.2.6. ติปัล์ลัต์ถมิคชาตก

Ekakanipāta

Sīlavagga

Tipallatthamigajātaka

16. 34 “Migaṁ tipallatthamanekamāyaṁ[27],
Aṭṭhakkhuraṁ aḍḍharattāpapāyiṁ[28];
Ekena sotena chamāssasanto,
Chahi kalāhitibhoti[29] bhāgineyyo”ti.

35 Tipallatthamigajātakaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.

  1. tipallatthamanekamāyaṁ: tipallatta ... (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. aḍḍharattāpapāyiṁ: aḍḍharattāvapāyiṁ (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. kalāhitibhoti: kalāhatibhoti (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
