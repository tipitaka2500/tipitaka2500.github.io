1.2.1. ลัก์ขณมิคชาตก

Ekakanipāta

Sīlavagga

Lakkhaṇamigajātaka

11. 24 “Hoti sīlavataṁ attho,
paṭisanthāravuttinaṁ;
Lakkhaṇaṁ passa āyantaṁ,
ñātisaṅghapurakkhataṁ;
Atha passasimaṁ kāḷaṁ,
suvihīnaṁva ñātibhī”ti. [18] [19]

25 Lakkhaṇamigajātakaṁ[20] paṭhamaṁ.

  1. paṭisanthāravuttinaṁ: paṭisandhāra ... (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. ñātisaṅghapurakkhataṁ: purakkhitaṁ (syā2, syā3), purekkhitaṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Lakkhaṇamigajātakaṁ: lakkhaṇajātakaṁ (syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
