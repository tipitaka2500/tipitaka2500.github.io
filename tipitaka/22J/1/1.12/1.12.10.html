1.12.10 Bandhanamokkhajātaka

1.12.10. พัน์ธนโมก์ขชาตก

Ekakanipāta

Haṃcivagga

Bandhanamokkhajātaka

120. 265 “Abaddhā tattha bajjhanti,
yattha bālā pabhāsare;
Baddhāpi tattha muccanti,
yattha dhīrā pabhāsare”ti.

266 Bandhanamokkhajātakaṁ dasamaṁ.
 Haṁcivaggo[136] dvādasamo.

267

268 Atha gadrabha sattuva kaṁsasataṁ,
Bahucinti sāsikāyātikara;
Ativela visesamanācariyova,
Dhīrāpabhāsaratena dasāti.

  1. Haṁcivaggo: haṁsavaggo (sī), haṁsivaggo (syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
