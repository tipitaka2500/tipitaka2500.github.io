1.14.9 Ubhatobhaṭṭhajātaka

1.14.9. อุภโตภัฏ์ฐชาตก

Ekakanipāta

Asampadānavagga

Ubhatobhaṭṭhajātaka

139. 307 “Akkhī bhinnā paṭo naṭṭho,
sakhigehe ca bhaṇḍanaṁ;
Ubhato paduṭṭhā kammantā[152],
udakamhi thalamhi cā”ti.

308 Ubhatobhaṭṭhajātakaṁ navamaṁ.

  1. kammantā: paduṭṭhakammanto (sī), paduṭṭhakammantā (syā2, syā3), paduṭṭho kammanto (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
