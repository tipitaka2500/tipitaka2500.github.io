1.15.10 Sañjīvajātaka

1.15.10. สัญ์ชีวชาตก

Ekakanipāta

Kakaṇṭakavagga

Sañjīvajātaka

150. 331 “Asantaṁ yo paggaṇhāti,
asantaṁ cūpasevati;
Tameva ghāsaṁ kurute,
byaggho sañjīvako yathā”ti.

332 Sañjīvajātakaṁ dasamaṁ.
 Kakaṇṭakavaggo[164] pannarasamo.

333

334 Sukhamedhati daṇḍavaro ca puna,
Lasi vāladhi pañcamarādhavaro;
Samahodadhi kattika bondi puna,
Caturaṅgulabyagghavarena dasāti.

335 Uparimapaṇṇāsako[165].

336

337 Apaṇṇakaṁ sīlavaggakuruṅga,
Kulāvakaṁ atthakāmena pañcamaṁ;
Āsīso itthivaruṇaṁ apāyi,
Littavaggena te dasa;
Parosataṁ haṁci kusanāḷi[166],
Asampadaṁ kakaṇṭakavaggo.

338 Ekanipātamhilaṅkatanti.

339 Ekakanipātaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.

  1. Kakaṇṭakavaggo: pāpasevanavaggo (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Uparimapaṇṇāsako: sī potthakeyeva (Word Variant) ↩
  3. kusanāḷi: haṁsi sarikkhaṁ (sabbattha) (Word Variant) ↩
