1.9.3 Kāḷakaṇṇijātaka

1.9.3. กาฬกัณ์ณิชาตก

Ekakanipāta

Apāyimhavagga

Kāḷakaṇṇijātaka

83. 184 “Mitto have sattapadena hoti,
Sahāyo pana dvādasakena hoti;
Māsaḍḍhamāsena ca ñāti hoti,
Tatuttariṁ attasamopi hoti;
Sohaṁ kathaṁ attasukhassa hetu,
Cirasanthutaṁ kāḷakaṇṇiṁ jaheyyan”ti. [95]

185 Kāḷakaṇṇijātakaṁ[96] tatiyaṁ

  1. Cirasanthutaṁ: cirasatthunaṁ (pā1), cirasandhavaṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Kāḷakaṇṇijātakaṁ: kālakaṇṇijātakaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
