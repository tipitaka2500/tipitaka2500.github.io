1.6.6. กั์จนัก์ขัน์ธชาตก

Ekakanipāta

Āsīsavagga

Kañcanakkhandhajātaka

56. 123 “Yo pahaṭṭhena cittena,
pahaṭṭhamanaso naro;
Bhāveti kusalaṁ dhammaṁ,
yogakkhemassa pattiyā;
Pāpuṇe anupubbena,
sabbasaṁyojanakkhayan”ti.

124 Kañcanakkhandhajātakaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.
