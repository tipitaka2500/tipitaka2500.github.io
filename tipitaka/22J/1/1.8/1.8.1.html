Varuṇajātaka

71. 157 “Yo pubbe karaṇīyāni,
pacchā so kātumicchati;
Varuṇakaṭṭhabhañjova,
sa pacchā manutappatī”ti. [81]

158 Varuṇajātakaṁ[82] paṭhamaṁ.

  1. Varuṇakaṭṭhabhañjova: varaṇakaṭṭhabhañjova (sī, pā1), varuṇakaṭṭhaṁ bhañjova (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Varuṇajātakaṁ: varaṇajātakaṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
