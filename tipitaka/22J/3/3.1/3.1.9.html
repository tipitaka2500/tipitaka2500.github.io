3.1.9 Tirīṭavacchajātaka

3.1.9. ติรีฏวัจ์ฉชาตก

Tikanipāta

Saṅkappavagga

Tirīṭavacchajātaka

25. 702 “Nayimassa vijjāmayamatthi kiñci,
Na bandhavo no pana te sahāyo;
Atha kena vaṇṇena tirīṭavaccho[370],
Tedaṇḍiko bhuñjati aggapiṇḍaṁ”.

26. 703 “Āpāsu me yuddhaparājitassa,
Ekassa katvā vivanasmi ghore;
Pasārayī kicchagatassa pāṇiṁ,
Tenūdatāriṁ dukhasampareto. [371]

27. 704 Etassa kiccena idhānupatto,
Vesāyino visayā jīvaloke;
Lābhāraho tāta tirīṭavaccho,
Dethassa bhogaṁ yajathañca[372] yaññan”ti.

705 Tirīṭavacchajātakaṁ[373] navamaṁ.

  2. "Ᾱpāsu: āvāsu (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. yajathañca: yajatañca (sī, pā1), yajitañca (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. Tirīṭavacchajātakaṁ: tiritivacchajātakaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
