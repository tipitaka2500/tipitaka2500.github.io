3.4.3 Vaḍḍhakīsūkarajātaka

3.4.3. วัฑ์ฒกีสูกรชาตก

Tikanipāta

Abbhantaravagga

Vaḍḍhakīsūkarajātaka

97. 804 “Varaṁ varaṁ tvaṁ nihanaṁ pure cari,
Asmiṁ padese abhibhuyya sūkare;
So dāni eko byapagamma jhāyasi,
Balaṁ nu te byaggha na cajja vijjati”.

98. 805 “Ime sudaṁ[436] yanti disodisaṁ pure,
Bhayaṭṭitā leṇagavesino puthū;
Te dāni saṅgamma vasanti ekato,
Yatthaṭṭhitā duppasahajjame[437] mayā”.

99. 806 “Namatthu saṁghāna samāgatānaṁ,
Disvā sayaṁ sakhya vadāmi abbhutaṁ;
Byagghaṁ migā yattha jiniṁsu dāṭhino,
Sāmaggiyā dāṭhabalesu muccare”ti.

807 Vaḍḍhakīsūkarajātakaṁ[438] tatiyaṁ.

  1. sudaṁ: imassutā (syā2, syā3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. duppasahajjame: duppasahajjime (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Vaḍḍhakīsūkarajātakaṁ: vaḍḍhakisūkarajātakaṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
