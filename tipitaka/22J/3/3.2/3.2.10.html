3.2.10 Ulūkajātaka

3.2.10. อุลูกชาตก

Tikanipāta

Padumavagga

Ulūkajātaka

58. 748 “Sabbehi kira ñātīhi,
kosiyo issaro kato;
Sace ñātīhi anuññāto[398],
bhaṇeyyāhaṁ ekavācikaṁ”.

59. 749 “Bhaṇa samma anuññāto,
atthaṁ dhammañca kevalaṁ;
Santi hi daharā pakkhī,
paññavanto jutindharā”.

60. 750 “Na me ruccati bhaddaṁ vo[399],
ulūkassābhisecanaṁ;
Akkuddhassa mukhaṁ passa,
kathaṁ kuddho karissatī”ti.

751 Ulūkajātakaṁ dasamaṁ.
 Padumavaggo[400] dutiyo.

752

753 Padumuttama nāgasirivhayano,
Sa-mahaṇṇava yūpa khurappavaro;
Atha bhaddalī kuñjara rukkha puna,
Kharavāca ulūkavarena dasāti.

  1. anuññāto: ñātīhanuññāto (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. vo: bhadante (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Padumavaggo: kosiyavaggo (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
