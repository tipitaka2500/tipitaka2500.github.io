2.4.3.3.4. ทิพ์พโสตญาณ

Sāmaññaphalasutta

Sāmaññaphala

Paṇītatarasāmaññaphala

Aṭṭhañāṇa

Dibbasotañāṇa

240. 331 So evaṁ samāhite citte parisuddhe pariyodāte anaṅgaṇe vigatūpakkilese mudubhūte kammaniye ṭhite āneñjappatte dibbāya sotadhātuyā cittaṁ abhinīharati abhininnāmeti. So dibbāya sotadhātuyā visuddhāya atikkantamānusikāya ubho sadde suṇāti dibbe ca mānuse ca ye dūre santike ca.

241. 332 Seyyathāpi, mahārāja, puriso addhānamaggappaṭipanno. So suṇeyya bherisaddampi mudiṅgasaddampi[123] saṅkhapaṇavadindimasaddampi[124]. Tassa evamassa— ‘bherisaddo’ itipi[125], ‘mudiṅgasaddo’ itipi, ‘saṅkhapaṇavadindimasaddo’ itipi. Evameva kho, mahārāja, bhikkhu evaṁ samāhite citte parisuddhe pariyodāte anaṅgaṇe vigatūpakkilese mudubhūte kammaniye ṭhite āneñjappatte dibbāya sotadhātuyā cittaṁ abhinīharati abhininnāmeti. So dibbāya sotadhātuyā visuddhāya atikkantamānusikāya ubho sadde suṇāti dibbe ca mānuse ca ye dūre santike ca. Idampi kho, mahārāja, sandiṭṭhikaṁ sāmaññaphalaṁ purimehi sandiṭṭhikehi sāmaññaphalehi abhikkantatarañca paṇītatarañca.

  1. mudiṅgasaddampi: mudiṅgasaddaṁpi (syā1-3), mutiṅgasaddampi (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. saṅkhapaṇavadindimasaddampi: saṅkhapaṇavadeṇḍimasaddampi (sī, pā1), saṁkhasaddaṁpi paṇavasaddaṁpi dendimasaddaṁpi (syā1, kaṃ), saṁkhasaddaṁpi paṇavasaddaṁpi deṇḍimasaddaṁpi (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. itipi: saṅkhasaddo itipi paṇavasaddo itipi dendimasaddo itipi (syā1, kaṃ), saṁkhasaddo itipi paṇavasaddo itipi deṇḍimasaddo itipi (syā2, syā3), saṅkhapaṇavadeṇḍimasaddo itipi (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
