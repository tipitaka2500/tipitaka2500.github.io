8.3. จตุฐานํ

Siṅgālasutta

Catuṭhānaṃ

246. 582 “Katamehi catūhi ṭhānehi pāpakammaṁ na karoti? Chandāgatiṁ gacchanto pāpakammaṁ karoti, dosāgatiṁ gacchanto pāpakammaṁ karoti, mohāgatiṁ gacchanto pāpakammaṁ karoti, bhayāgatiṁ gacchanto pāpakammaṁ karoti. Yato kho, gahapatiputta, ariyasāvako neva chandāgatiṁ gacchati, na dosāgatiṁ gacchati, na mohāgatiṁ gacchati, na bhayāgatiṁ gacchati; imehi catūhi ṭhānehi pāpakammaṁ na karotī”ti. Idamavoca bhagavā. Idaṁ vatvāna sugato athāparaṁ etadavoca satthā—

583 [335] “Chandā dosā bhayā mohā,
yo dhammaṁ ativattati;
Nihīyati yaso tassa[336],
kāḷapakkheva candimā.

584 Chandā dosā bhayā mohā,
yo dhammaṁ nātivattati;
Āpūrati yaso tassa[337],
sukkapakkheva candimā”ti. [338]
