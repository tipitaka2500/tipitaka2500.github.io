11 Dasuttarasutta

11. ทสุต์ตรสุต์ต

Dasuttarasutta

Dasuttarasutta

350. 1132 Evaṁ me sutaṁ— ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā campāyaṁ viharati gaggarāya pokkharaṇiyā tīre mahatā bhikkhusaṁghena saddhiṁ pañcamattehi bhikkhusatehi. Tatra kho āyasmā sāriputto bhikkhū āmantesi— “āvuso bhikkhave”ti. “Āvuso”ti kho te bhikkhū āyasmato sāriputtassa paccassosuṁ. Āyasmā sāriputto etadavoca—

1133 “Dasuttaraṁ pavakkhāmi,
dhammaṁ nibbānapattiyā;
Dukkhassantakiriyāya,
sabbaganthappamocanaṁ.
