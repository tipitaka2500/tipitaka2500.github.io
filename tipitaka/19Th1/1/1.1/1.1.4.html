Paṭhamavagga

Puṇṇattheragāthā

4. 11 “Sabbhireva samāsetha,
paṇḍitehatthadassibhi;
Atthaṁ mahantaṁ gambhīraṁ,
duddasaṁ nipuṇaṁ aṇuṁ;
Dhīrā samadhigacchanti,
appamattā vicakkhaṇā”ti.

12 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā puṇṇo mantāṇiputto[4] thero gāthaṁ abhāsitthāti.
