2.2.10. อชิตัต์เถรคาถา

Ekakanipāta

Dutiyavagga

Ajitattheragāthā

20. 46 “Maraṇe me bhayaṁ natthi,
nikanti natthi jīvite;
Sandehaṁ nikkhipissāmi,
sampajāno paṭissato”ti. [23]

47 … Ajito thero…
vaggo dutiyo.

48

49 Cūḷavaccho mahāvaccho,
vanavaccho ca sīvako;
Kuṇḍadhāno ca belaṭṭhi,
dāsako ca tatopari;
Siṅgālapitiko thero,
kulo ca ajito dasāti.

  1. paṭissato"ti: patissatoti (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
