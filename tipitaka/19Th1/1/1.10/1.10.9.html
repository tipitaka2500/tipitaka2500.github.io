Uttiyattheragāthā

99. 221 “Saddaṁ sutvā sati muṭṭhā,
Piyaṁ nimittaṁ[68] manasikaroto;
Sārattacitto vedeti,
Tañca ajjhosa tiṭṭhati;
Tassa vaḍḍhanti āsavā,
Saṁsāraṁ upagāmino”ti.
