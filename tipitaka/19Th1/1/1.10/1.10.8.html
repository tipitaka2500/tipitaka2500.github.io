Abhayattheragāthā

98. 219 “Rūpaṁ disvā sati muṭṭhā,
Piyaṁ nimittaṁ[66] manasikaroto;
Sārattacitto vedeti,
Tañca ajjhosa tiṭṭhati;
Tassa vaḍḍhanti āsavā,
Bhavamūlopagāmino”ti. [67]
