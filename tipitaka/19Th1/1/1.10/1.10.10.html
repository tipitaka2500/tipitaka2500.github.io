Dasamavagga

(Dutiya) Devasabhattheragāthā

100. 223 “Sammappadhānasampanno,
Satipaṭṭhānagocaro;
Vimuttikusumasañchanno,
Parinibbissatyanāsavo”ti. [69]

224 … Devasabho thero… .
 Vaggo dasamo.

225

226 Paripuṇṇako ca vijayo,
erako mettajī muni;
Cakkhupālo khaṇḍasumano,
tisso ca abhayo tathā;
Uttiyo ca mahāpañño,
thero devasabhopi cāti.
