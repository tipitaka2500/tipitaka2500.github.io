6.1.6 Sappadāsattheragāthā

7.1.6. สัป์ปทาสัต์เถรคาถา

Chakkanipāta

Paṭhamavagga

Sappadāsattheragāthā

405. 678 “Paṇṇavīsativassāni,
yato pabbajito ahaṁ;
Accharāsaṅghātamattampi,
cetosantimanajjhagaṁ.

406. 679 Aladdhā cittassekaggaṁ,
kāmarāgena aṭṭito[214];
Bāhā paggayha kandanto,
vihārā upanikkhamiṁ[215].

407. 680 Satthaṁ vā āharissāmi,
Ko attho jīvitena me;
Kathaṁ hi sikkhaṁ paccakkhaṁ,
Kālaṁ kubbetha mādiso.

408. 681 Tadāhaṁ khuramādāya,
mañcakamhi upāvisiṁ;
Parinīto khuro āsi,
dhamaniṁ chettumattano.

409. 682 Tato me manasīkāro,
yoniso udapajjatha;
Ādīnavo pāturahu,
nibbidā samatiṭṭhatha.

410. 683 Tato cittaṁ vimucci me,
passa dhammasudhammataṁ;
Tisso vijjā anuppattā,
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanan”ti.

  1. aṭṭito: addito (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1-2), aḍḍito (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. upanikkhamiṁ: nūpanikkhamiṁ (sī), nupanikkhamiṁ (syā2, syā3, pā1-2), dupanikkhamiṁ (?) (Word Variant) ↩
