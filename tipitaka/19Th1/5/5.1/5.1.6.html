6.1.6. นทีกัส์สปัต์เถรคาถา

Pañcakanipāta

Paṭhamavagga

Nadīkassapattheragāthā

340. 597 “Atthāya vata me buddho,
nadiṁ nerañjaraṁ agā;
Yassāhaṁ dhammaṁ sutvāna,
micchādiṭṭhiṁ vivajjayiṁ.

341. 598 Yajiṁ uccāvace yaññe,
aggihuttaṁ juhiṁ ahaṁ;
‘Esā suddhī’ti maññanto,
andhabhūto puthujjano. [190]

342. 599 Diṭṭhigahanapakkhando,
parāmāsena mohito;
Asuddhiṁ maññisaṁ suddhiṁ,
andhabhūto aviddasu. [191]

343. 600 Micchādiṭṭhi pahīnā me,
bhavā sabbe padālitā[192];
Juhāmi dakkhiṇeyyaggiṁ,
namassāmi tathāgataṁ.

344. 601 Mohā sabbe pahīnā me,
bhavataṇhā padālitā;
Vikkhīṇo jātisaṁsāro,
natthi dāni punabbhavo”ti.

602 … Nadīkassapo thero… .

  2. Diṭṭhigahanapakkhando: ... gahanapakkhanto (sī), ... gahaṇapakkhanno (syā2, syā3, pā1), ... gahanapakkhanno (pā2) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. padālitā: vidālitā (pā1-2, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
