Dukanipāta

Tatiyavagga

Bhaddajittheragāthā

163. 341 “Panādo nāma so rājā,
yassa yūpo suvaṇṇayo;
Tiriyaṁ soḷasubbedho[108],
ubbhamāhu sahassadhā. [109]

164. 342 Sahassakaṇḍo satageṇḍu[111],
dhajālu haritāmayo;
Anaccuṁ tattha gandhabbā,
chasahassāni sattadhā”ti. [110]

343 … Bhaddajitthero… .

  1. soḷasubbedho: soḷasapabbedho (sī, pā1-2), soḷasabbāṇo (?) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. ubbhamāhu: uddhamāhu (sī), uccamāhu (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Sahassakaṇḍo: sahassakaṇḍu (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. satageṇḍu: satabheṇḍu (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
