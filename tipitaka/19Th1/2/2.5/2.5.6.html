3.5.6. จูฬกัต์เถรคาถา

Dukanipāta

Pañcamavagga

Cūḷakattheragāthā

211. 417 “Nadanti morā susikhā supekhuṇā,
Sunīlagīvā sumukhā sugajjino;
Susaddalā cāpi mahāmahī ayaṁ,
Subyāpitambu suvalāhakaṁ nabhaṁ.

212. 418 Sukallarūpo sumanassa jhāyataṁ[142],
Sunikkamo sādhu subuddhasāsane;
Susukkasukkaṁ nipuṇaṁ sududdasaṁ,
Phusāhi taṁ uttamamaccutaṁ padan”ti. [143]
