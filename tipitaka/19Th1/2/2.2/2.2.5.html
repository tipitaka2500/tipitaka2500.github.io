3.2.5. สัพ์พมิต์ตัต์เถรคาถา

Dukanipāta

Dutiyavagga

Sabbamittattheragāthā

149. 318 “Jano janamhi sambaddho[96],
janamevassito jano;
Jano janena heṭhīyati,
heṭheti ca[97] jano janaṁ.

150. 319 Ko hi tassa janenattho,
janena janitena vā;
Janaṁ ohāya gacchaṁ taṁ[98],
heṭhayitvā bahuṁ janan”ti. [99]

320 … Sabbamitto thero… .

  1. sambaddho: sambandho (syā2, syā3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. ca: heṭhiyati, heṭheti ca (syā2, syā3, pā1-2), bādhiyati, bādheti ca (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. taṁ: gaccheyya (syā2, syā3), gaccha taṁ (pā2) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. heṭhayitvā: bādhayitvā (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
