Dukanipāta

Dutiyavagga

Jotidāsattheragāthā

143. 309 “Ye kho te veṭhamissena[89],
nānattena ca kammunā;
Manusse uparundhanti,
pharusūpakkamā janā;
Tepi tattheva[91] kīranti,
na hi kammaṁ panassati. [90]

144. 310 Yaṁ karoti naro kammaṁ,
Kalyāṇaṁ yadi pāpakaṁ;
Tassa tasseva dāyādo,
Yaṁ yaṁ kammaṁ pakubbatī”ti.
