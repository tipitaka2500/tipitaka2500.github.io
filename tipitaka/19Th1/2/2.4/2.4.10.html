2.4.10 Kappaṭakurattheragāthā

3.4.10. กัป์ปฏกุรัต์เถรคาถา

Dukanipāta

Catutthavagga

Kappaṭakurattheragāthā

199. 397 “Ayamiti kappaṭo kappaṭakuro,
Acchāya atibharitāya[132];
Amataghaṭikāyaṁ dhammakaṭamatto[133],
Katapadaṁ jhānāni ocetuṁ.

200. 398 Mā kho tvaṁ kappaṭa pacālesi,
Mā tvaṁ upakaṇṇamhi tāḷessaṁ;
Na hi[134] tvaṁ kappaṭa mattamaññāsi,
Saṁghamajjhamhi pacalāyamāno”ti.

399 … Kappaṭakuro thero… .
 Vaggo catuttho.

400

401 Migasiro sivako ca,
upavāṇo ca paṇḍito;
Isidinno ca kaccāno,
nitako ca mahāvasī;
Poṭiriyaputto nisabho,
usabho kappaṭakuroti.

  1. atibharitāya: atibhariyāya (ka-sī, ka-ma), accaṁbharāya (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. dhammakaṭamatto: dhammakatamatto (sī, pā1-2), dhammakaṭamaggo (sī-a), dhammakaṭapatto (syā2, syā3, [Ma-aka]) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. hi: naha (sī), na ha (pā1), na vā (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
