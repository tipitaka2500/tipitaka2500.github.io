Dukanipāta

Catutthavagga

Nisabhattheragāthā

195. 391 [129] “Pañca kāmaguṇe hitvā,
Piyarūpe manorame;
Saddhāya gharā nikkhamma[130],
Dukkhassantakaro bhave.

196. 392 [131] Nābhinandāmi maraṇaṁ,
nābhinandāmi jīvitaṁ;
Kālañca paṭikaṅkhāmi,
sampajāno patissato”ti.
