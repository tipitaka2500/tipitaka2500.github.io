8.1.1 Mahākaccāyanattheragāthā

9.1.1. มหากัจ์จายนัต์เถรคาถา

Aṭṭhakanipāta

Paṭhamavagga

Mahākaccāyanattheragāthā

494. 787 “Kammaṁ bahukaṁ na kāraye,
Parivajjeyya janaṁ na uyyame;
So ussukko rasānugiddho,
Atthaṁ riñcati yo sukhādhivāho[249].

495. 788 Paṅkoti hi naṁ avedayuṁ,
Yāyaṁ vandanapūjanā kulesu;
Sukhumaṁ sallaṁ durubbahaṁ,
Sakkāro kāpurisena dujjaho.

496. 789 Na parassupanidhāya,
kammaṁ maccassa pāpakaṁ;
Attanā taṁ na seveyya,
kammabandhū hi mātiyā.

497. 790 Na pare vacanā coro,
na pare vacanā muni;
Attā ca naṁ yathāvedi[250],
devāpi naṁ tathā vidū.

498. 791 Pare ca na vijānanti,
mayamettha yamāmase;
Ye ca tattha vijānanti,
tato sammanti medhagā.

499. 792 Jīvate vāpi sappañño,
api vittaparikkhayo;
Paññāya ca alābhena,
vittavāpi na jīvati.

500. 793 Sabbaṁ suṇāti sotena,
sabbaṁ passati cakkhunā;
Na ca diṭṭhaṁ sutaṁ dhīro,
sabbaṁ ujjhitumarahati.

501. 794 Cakkhumāssa yathā andho,
sotavā badhiro yathā;
Paññavāssa yathā mūgo,
balavā dubbaloriva;
Atha atthe[251] samuppanne,
sayetha matasāyikan”ti. [252]

795 … Mahākaccāyano thero… .

  1. sukhādhivāho: sukhāvāho (pā2) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. yathāvedi: attā ca yathā vetti (sī), attānañca yathā vedi (syā2, syā3), attānaṁ ca yathā veti (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. atthe: attaatthe (28Mi:1660) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. sayetha: passetha (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
