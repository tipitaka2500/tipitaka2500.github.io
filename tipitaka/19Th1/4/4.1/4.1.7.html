Catukkanipāta

Paṭhamavagga

Sambhūtattheragāthā

291. 533 “Yo dandhakāle tarati,
taraṇīye ca dandhaye;
Ayoni saṁvidhānena,
bālo dukkhaṁ nigacchati. [174]

292. 534 Tassatthā parihāyanti,
kāḷapakkheva candimā;
Āyasakyañca pappoti,
mittehi ca virujjhati. [175]

293. 535 Yo dandhakāle dandheti,
taraṇīye ca tāraye;
Yoniso saṁvidhānena,
sukhaṁ pappoti paṇḍito.

294. 536 Tassatthā paripūrenti,
sukkapakkheva candimā;
Yaso kittiñca pappoti,
mittehi na virujjhatī”ti.
