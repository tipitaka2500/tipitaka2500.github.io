7.1.2 Lakuṇḍakabhaddiyattheragāthā

8.1.2. ลกุณ์ฑกภัท์ทิยัต์เถรคาถา

Sattakanipāta

Paṭhamavagga

Lakuṇḍakabhaddiyattheragāthā

466. 752 [239] “Pare ambāṭakārāme,
vanasaṇḍamhi bhaddiyo;
Samūlaṁ taṇhamabbuyha,
tattha bhaddova jhāyati[240].

467. 753 Ramanteke mudiṅgehi[241],
vīṇāhi paṇavehi ca;
Ahañca rukkhamūlasmiṁ,
rato buddhassa sāsane.

468. 754 Buddho ce[242] me varaṁ dajjā,
so ca labbhetha me varo;
Gaṇhehaṁ sabbalokassa,
niccaṁ kāyagataṁ satiṁ.

469. 755 Ye maṁ rūpena pāmiṁsu,
ye ca ghosena anvagū;
Chandarāgavasūpetā,
na maṁ jānanti te janā.

470. 756 Ajjhattañca na jānāti,
bahiddhā ca na passati;
Samantāvaraṇo bālo,
sa ve ghosena vuyhati.

471. 757 Ajjhattañca na jānāti,
bahiddhā ca vipassati;
Bahiddhā phaladassāvī,
sopi ghosena vuyhati.

472. 758 Ajjhattañca pajānāti,
bahiddhā ca vipassati;
Anāvaraṇadassāvī,
na so ghosena vuyhatī”ti.
