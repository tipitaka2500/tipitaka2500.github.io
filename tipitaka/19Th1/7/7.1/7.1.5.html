7.1.5 Sarabhaṅgattheragāthā

8.1.5. สรภํคัต์เถรคาถา

Sattakanipāta

Paṭhamavagga

Sarabhaṅgattheragāthā

487. 776 “Sare hatthehi bhañjitvā,
katvāna kuṭimacchisaṁ;
Tena me sarabhaṅgoti,
nāmaṁ sammutiyā ahu.

488. 777 Na mayhaṁ kappate ajja,
sare hatthehi bhañjituṁ;
Sikkhāpadā no paññattā,
gotamena yasassinā.

489. 778 Sakalaṁ samattaṁ rogaṁ,
Sarabhaṅgo nāddasaṁ pubbe;
Soyaṁ rogo diṭṭho,
Vacanakarenātidevassa.

490. 779 Yeneva maggena gato vipassī,
Yeneva maggena sikhī ca vessabhū;
Kakusandhakoṇāgamano ca kassapo,
Tenañjasena agamāsi gotamo.

491. 780 Vītataṇhā anādānā,
satta buddhā khayogadhā;
Yehāyaṁ desito dhammo,
dhammabhūtehi tādibhi.

492. 781 Cattāri ariyasaccāni,
anukampāya pāṇinaṁ;
Dukkhaṁ samudayo maggo,
nirodho dukkhasaṅkhayo.

493. 782 Yasmiṁ nivattate[247] dukkhaṁ,
saṁsārasmiṁ anantakaṁ;
Bhedā imassa kāyassa,
jīvitassa ca saṅkhayā;
Añño punabbhavo natthi,
suvimuttomhi sabbadhī”ti.

783 … Sarabhaṅgo thero… .

784 Sattakanipāto niṭṭhito.

785

786 Sundarasamuddo thero,
thero lakuṇḍabhaddiyo[248];
Bhaddo thero ca sopāko,
sarabhaṅgo mahāisi;
Sattake pañcakā therā,
gāthāyo pañcatiṁsatīti.

  1. nivattate: yasmiṁ nibbattate (pā1), yasmiṁ na nibbattate (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. lakuṇḍabhaddiyo: lakuṇṭabhaddiyo (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
