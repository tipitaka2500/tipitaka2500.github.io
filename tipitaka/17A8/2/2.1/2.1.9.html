2.1.9. ปมปุค์คลสุต์ต

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Gotamīvagga

Paṭhamapuggalasutta

59. 610 [230] “Aṭṭhime, bhikkhave, puggalā āhuneyyā pāhuneyyā dakkhiṇeyyā añjalikaraṇīyā anuttaraṁ puññakkhettaṁ lokassa? Katame aṭṭha? Sotāpanno, sotāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipanno, sakadāgāmī, sakadāgāmiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipanno, anāgāmī, anāgāmiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipanno, arahā, arahattāya paṭipanno. Ime kho, bhikkhave, aṭṭha puggalā āhuneyyā…pe… anuttaraṁ puññakkhettaṁ lokassāti.

611 Cattāro ca paṭipannā,
cattāro ca phale ṭhitā;
Esa saṁgho ujubhūto,
paññāsīlasamāhito.

612 Yajamānānaṁ manussānaṁ,
Puññapekkhāna pāṇinaṁ;
Karotaṁ opadhikaṁ puññaṁ,
Saṁghe dinnaṁ mahapphalan”ti.

613 Navamaṁ.
