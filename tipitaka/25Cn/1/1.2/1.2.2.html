Tissametteyyamāṇavapucchā

65. 68 [37] “Kodha santusito loke, (iccāyasmā tissametteyyo)
Kassa no santi iñjitā;
Ko ubhantamabhiññāya,
Majjhe mantā na lippati[38];
Kaṁ brūsi mahāpurisoti,
Ko idha[39] sibbinimaccagā[40]”. (1)

66. 69 “Kāmesu brahmacariyavā, (metteyyāti bhagavā)
Vītataṇho sadā sato;
Saṅkhāya nibbuto bhikkhu,
Tassa no santi iñjitā. (2)

67. 70 So ubhantamabhiññāya,
Majjhe mantā na lippati;
Taṁ brūmi mahāpurisoti,
So idha sibbinimaccagā”ti. (3)

71 Tissametteyyamāṇavapucchā dutiyā.
