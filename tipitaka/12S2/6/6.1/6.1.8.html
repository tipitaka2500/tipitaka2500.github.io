6.1.8 Siṅgālasutta

6.1.8. สิงคาลสุต์ต

Lābhasakkārasaṃyutta

Paṭhamavagga

Siṅgālasutta

164. 1039 [262] Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. “Dāruṇo, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko…pe… adhigamāya. Assuttha no tumhe, bhikkhave, rattiyā paccūsasamayaṁ jarasiṅgālassa[263] vassamānassā”ti? “Evaṁ, bhante”. “Eso kho, bhikkhave, jarasiṅgālo ukkaṇḍakena[264] nāma rogajātena phuṭṭho neva bilagato ramati, na rukkhamūlagato ramati, na ajjhokāsagato ramati; yena yena gacchati, yattha yattha tiṭṭhati, yattha yattha nisīdati, yattha yattha nipajjati; tattha tattha anayabyasanaṁ āpajjati. Evameva kho, bhikkhave, idhekacco bhikkhu lābhasakkārasilokena abhibhūto pariyādiṇṇacitto neva suññāgāragato ramati, na rukkhamūlagato ramati, na ajjhokāsagato ramati; yena yena gacchati, yattha yattha tiṭṭhati, yattha yattha nisīdati, yattha yattha nipajjati; tattha tattha anayabyasanaṁ āpajjati. Evaṁ dāruṇo kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko…pe… evañhi vo, bhikkhave, sikkhitabban”ti.

1040 Aṭṭhamaṁ.

  1. sigālasuttaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. jarasiṅgālassa: jarasigālassa (sī, syā1-3, kaṃ), siṅgālassa (pā1-2, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. ukkaṇḍakena: ukkaṇṭakena (sī), ukkannakena (ka-sī), ukkaṇṇakena (syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
