6.1.7. ทิท์ธสุต์ต

Lābhasakkārasaṃyutta

Paṭhamavagga

Diddhasutta

163. 1036 [258] Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. “Dāruṇo, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko…pe… adhigamāya. Kaṁ, bhikkhave, diddhagatena visallena sallena[259] vijjhatu, sekhaṁ[260] appattamānasaṁ lābhasakkārasiloko anupāpuṇātu[261].

1037 Sallanti kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasilokassetaṁ adhivacanaṁ. Evaṁ dāruṇo kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko…pe… evañhi vo, bhikkhave, sikkhitabban”ti.

1038 Sattamaṁ.

  1. diddhavisallasuttaṁ (sī), diṭṭhaṁ (pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. sallena: diṭṭhigatena visallena (ka-sī), diṭṭhigatena sallena (syā1-3, kaṃ), diṭṭhagatena visallena sallena (pā1-2), diṭṭhigatena visallena sallena (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. sekhaṁ: bijjhatu sekhaṁ (sī), vijjhatu, sekkhaṁ (syā1-3), vijjhati, taṁ sekhaṁ (pā1-2, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. anupāpuṇātu: anupāpuṇāti (pā1-2, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
