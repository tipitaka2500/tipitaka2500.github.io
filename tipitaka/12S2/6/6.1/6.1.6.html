6.1.6. อสนิสุต์ต

Lābhasakkārasaṃyutta

Paṭhamavagga

Asanisutta

162. 1033 [255] [254] Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. “Dāruṇo, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko…pe… adhigamāya. Kaṁ, bhikkhave, asanivicakkaṁ āgacchatu, sekhaṁ[256] appattamānasaṁ lābhasakkārasiloko anupāpuṇātu[257].

1034 Asanivicakkanti kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasilokassetaṁ adhivacanaṁ. Evaṁ dāruṇo kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko…pe… evañhi vo, bhikkhave, sikkhitabban”ti.

1035 Chaṭṭhaṁ.

  1. asanivicakkasuttaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. "mā ca kho tvaṁ tāta sekhaṁ ... anupāpuṇātū"ti āgataṁ. tena nayena idhāpi attho gahetabbo. ettha hi kiṁsaddena paṭikkhepatthopi sakkā ñātuṁ, yathā "sayaṁ abhiññāya kamuddiseyyan"ti. tasmā kaṁ ... āgacchatūti ettha kamapi ... mā āgacchatūti ca, kaṁ sekhaṁ ... anupāpuṇātūti ettha kamapi sekhaṁ ... mā pāpuṇātūti ca attho veditabbo. aṭṭhakathāṭīkāsu ca ayamevattho ñāpito (12S2:1064, 12S2:1066) (Cross Reference) ↩
  3. sekhaṁ: asanivicakkaṁ, sekhaṁ (syā1-3, kaṃ), asanivicakkaṁ, taṁ sekhaṁ (pā1-2, ka-ma), asanivicakkaṁ āgacchatu, kaṁ sekhaṁ (?) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. anupāpuṇātu: anupāpuṇāti (pā1-2, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
