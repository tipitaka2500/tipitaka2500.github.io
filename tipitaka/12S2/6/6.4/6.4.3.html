Lābhasakkārasaṃyutta

Catutthavagga

Kusaladhammasutta

182. 1088 [287] Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. “Dāruṇo, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko. Lābhasakkārasilokena abhibhūtassa pariyādiṇṇacittassa, bhikkhave, devadattassa kusalo dhammo samucchedamagamā. Evaṁ dāruṇo kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko…pe… sikkhitabban”ti.
