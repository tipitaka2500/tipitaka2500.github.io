6.4.4. สุก์กธัม์มสุต์ต

Lābhasakkārasaṃyutta

Catutthavagga

Sukkadhammasutta

183. 1090 [288] Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. “Dāruṇo, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko. Lābhasakkārasilokena abhibhūtassa pariyādiṇṇacittassa, bhikkhave, devadattassa sukko dhammo samucchedamagamā. Evaṁ dāruṇo kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko…pe… sikkhitabban”ti.
