9.1.12. ทุติยสิงคาลสุต์ต

Opammasaṃyutta

Opammavagga

Dutiyasiṅgālasutta

234. 1245 Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. “Assuttha no[354] tumhe, bhikkhave, rattiyā paccūsasamayaṁ jarasiṅgālassa vassamānassā”ti? “Evaṁ, bhante”. “Siyā kho, bhikkhave, tasmiṁ jarasiṅgāle yā kāci kataññutā kataveditā, na tveva idhekacce sakyaputtiyapaṭiññe siyā yā kāci kataññutā kataveditā. Tasmātiha, bhikkhave, evaṁ sikkhitabbaṁ— ‘kataññuno bhavissāma katavedino; na ca no amhesu appakampi kataṁ nassissatī’ti. Evañhi vo, bhikkhave, sikkhitabban”ti. [355]
