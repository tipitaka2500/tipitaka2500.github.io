9.1.11 Siṅgālasutta

9.1.11. สิงคาลสุต์ต

Opammasaṃyutta

Opammavagga

Siṅgālasutta

233. 1243 [353] Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. “Assuttha no tumhe, bhikkhave, rattiyā paccūsasamayaṁ jarasiṅgālassa vassamānassā”ti? “Evaṁ, bhante”. “Eso kho, bhikkhave, jarasiṅgālo ukkaṇḍakena nāma rogajātena phuṭṭho. So yena yena icchati tena tena gacchati; yattha yattha icchati tattha tattha tiṭṭhati; yattha yattha icchati tattha tattha nisīdati; yattha yattha icchati tattha tattha nipajjati; sītakopi naṁ vāto upavāyati. Sādhu khvassa, bhikkhave, yaṁ idhekacco sakyaputtiyapaṭiñño evarūpampi attabhāvapaṭilābhaṁ paṭisaṁvediyetha. Tasmātiha, bhikkhave, evaṁ sikkhitabbaṁ— ‘appamattā viharissāmā’ti. Evañhi vo, bhikkhave, sikkhitabban”ti.
