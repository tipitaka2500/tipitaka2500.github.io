4.12 Garahadinnavatthu

4.12. ครหทิน์นวัต์ถุ

Pupphavagga

Garahadinnavatthu

58. 62 Yathā saṅkāradhānasmiṁ[40],
ujjhitasmiṁ mahāpathe;
Padumaṁ tattha jāyetha,
sucigandhaṁ manoramaṁ.

59. 63 Evaṁ saṅkārabhūtesu,
andhabhūte puthujjane;
Atirocati paññāya,
sammāsambuddhasāvako. [41]

64 Pupphavaggo catuttho.

  1. saṅkāradhānasmiṁ: saṅkāraṭhānasmiṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. andhabhūte: andhībhūte (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
