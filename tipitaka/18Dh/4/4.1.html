4.1 Pathavikathāpasutapañcasatabhikkhuvatthu

4.1. ปถวิกถาปสุตปัญ์จสตภิก์ขุวัต์ถุ

Pupphavagga

Pathavikathāpasutapañcasatabhikkhuvatthu

44. 48 Ko imaṁ[27] pathaviṁ vicessati[28],
Yamalokañca imaṁ sadevakaṁ;
Ko dhammapadaṁ sudesitaṁ,
Kusalo pupphamiva pacessati[29].

45. 49 Sekho pathaviṁ vicessati,
Yamalokañca imaṁ sadevakaṁ;
Sekho dhammapadaṁ sudesitaṁ,
Kusalo pupphamiva pacessati.

  1. imaṁ: komaṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. vicessati: vijessati (syā1-3, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. pacessati: pupphamivappacessati (pā2, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
