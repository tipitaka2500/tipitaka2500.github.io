12.8 Kālattheravatthu

12.8. กาลัต์เถรวัต์ถุ

Attavagga

Kālattheravatthu

164. 176 Yo sāsanaṁ arahataṁ,
ariyānaṁ dhammajīvinaṁ;
Paṭikkosati dummedho,
diṭṭhiṁ nissāya pāpikaṁ;
Phalāni kaṭṭhakasseva,
attaghātāya phallati. [116]

  1. attaghātāya: attaghaññāya (sī, syā1-3, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
