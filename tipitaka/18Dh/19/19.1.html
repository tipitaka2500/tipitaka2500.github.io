19.1 Vinicchayamahāmattavatthu

19.1. วินิจ์ฉยมหามัต์ตวัต์ถุ

Dhammaṭṭhavagga

Vinicchayamahāmattavatthu

256. 277 Na tena hoti dhammaṭṭho,
yenatthaṁ sāhasā[165] naye;
Yo ca atthaṁ anatthañca,
ubho niccheyya paṇḍito.

257. 278 Asāhasena dhammena,
samena nayatī pare;
Dhammassa gutto medhāvī,
“dhammaṭṭho”ti pavuccati.

  1. sāhasā: sahasā (sī, syā1-3, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
