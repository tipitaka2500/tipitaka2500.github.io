19.4 Lakuṇḍakabhaddiyattheravatthu

19.4. ลกุณ์ฑกภัท์ทิยัต์เถรวัต์ถุ

Dhammaṭṭhavagga

Lakuṇḍakabhaddiyattheravatthu

260. 281 Na tena thero so hoti[166],
yenassa palitaṁ siro;
Paripakko vayo tassa,
“moghajiṇṇo”ti vuccati.

261. 282 Yamhi saccañca dhammo ca,
ahiṁsā saṁyamo damo;
Sa ve vantamalo dhīro,
“thero” iti[167] pavuccati.

  1. hoti: thero hoti (sī, syā1-3, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. iti: so theroti (syā1-3, ka-ma), thero ti (pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
