19.2. ฉัพ์พัค์คิยวัต์ถุ

Dhammaṭṭhavagga

Chabbaggiyavatthu

258. 279 Na tena paṇḍito hoti,
yāvatā bahu bhāsati;
Khemī averī abhayo,
“paṇḍito”ti pavuccati.
