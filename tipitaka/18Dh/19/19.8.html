19.8 Titthiyavatthu

19.8. ติต์ถิยวัต์ถุ

Dhammaṭṭhavagga

Titthiyavatthu

268. 289 [169] Na monena munī hoti,
mūḷharūpo aviddasu;
Yo ca tulaṁva paggayha,
varamādāya paṇḍito.

269. 290 Pāpāni parivajjeti,
sa munī tena so muni;
Yo munāti ubho loke,
“muni” tena pavuccati.
