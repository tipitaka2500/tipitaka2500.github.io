19.6 Hatthakavatthu

19.6. หัต์ถกวัต์ถุ

Dhammaṭṭhavagga

Hatthakavatthu

264. 285 Na muṇḍakena samaṇo,
abbato alikaṁ bhaṇaṁ;
Icchālobhasamāpanno,
samaṇo kiṁ bhavissati.

265. 286 Yo ca sameti pāpāni,
aṇuṁthūlāni sabbaso;
Samitattā hi pāpānaṁ,
“samaṇo”ti pavuccati.
