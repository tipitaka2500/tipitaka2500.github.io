Appamādavagga

Bālanakkhattasaṃghuṭṭhavatthu

26. 28 Pamādamanuyuñjanti,
bālā dummedhino janā;
Appamādañca medhāvī,
dhanaṁ seṭṭhaṁva rakkhati.

27. 29 Mā pamādamanuyuñjetha,
mā kāmaratisanthavaṁ[19];
Appamatto hi jhāyanto,
pappoti vipulaṁ sukhaṁ.
