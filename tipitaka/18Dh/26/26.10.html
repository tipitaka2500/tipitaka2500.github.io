26.10. ชฏิลพ๎ราห๎มณวัต์ถุ

Brāhmaṇavagga

Jaṭilabrāhmaṇavatthu

393. 421 Na jaṭāhi na gottena,
na jaccā hoti brāhmaṇo;
Yamhi saccañca dhammo ca,
so sucī so ca brāhmaṇo.
