26.7. สาริปุต์ตัต์เถรวัต์ถุ

Brāhmaṇavagga

Sāriputtattheravatthu

389. 417 Na brāhmaṇassa pahareyya,
nāssa muñcetha brāhmaṇo;
Dhī brāhmaṇassa hantāraṁ,
tato dhī yassa[243] muñcati. [242]

390. 418 Na brāhmaṇassetadakiñci seyyo,
Yadā nisedho manaso piyehi;
Yato yato hiṁsamano nivattati,
Tato tato sammatimeva dukkhaṁ.

  1. Dhī: dhi (syā1-3 byākaraṇesu) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. yassa: yo + assa = (Word Variant) ↩
