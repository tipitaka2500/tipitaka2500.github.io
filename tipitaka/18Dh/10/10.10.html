10.10 Pilotikatissattheravatthu

10.10. ปิโลติกติส์สัต์เถรวัต์ถุ

Daṇḍavagga

Pilotikatissattheravatthu

143. 153 Hirīnisedho puriso,
koci lokasmi vijjati;
Yo niddaṁ[100] apabodheti[101],
asso bhadro kasāmiva.

144. 154 Asso yathā bhadro kasāniviṭṭho,
Ātāpino saṁvegino bhavātha;
Saddhāya sīlena ca vīriyena ca,
Samādhinā dhammavinicchayena ca;
Sampannavijjācaraṇā patissatā,
Jahissatha dukkhamidaṁ anappakaṁ. [102]

  1. niddaṁ: nindaṁ (sī, pā1-2 12S1:58) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. apabodheti: apabodhati (sī 12S1:58), appabodheti (syā1), appabodhati (pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Jahissatha: pahassatha (sī, syā1-3, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
