10.7 Mahāmoggallānattheravatthu

10.7. มหาโมค์คัล์ลานัต์เถรวัต์ถุ

Daṇḍavagga

Mahāmoggallānattheravatthu

137. 147 Yo daṇḍena adaṇḍesu,
appaduṭṭhesu dussati;
Dasannamaññataraṁ ṭhānaṁ,
khippameva nigacchati.

138. 148 Vedanaṁ pharusaṁ jāniṁ,
sarīrassa va bhedanaṁ[91];
Garukaṁ vāpi ābādhaṁ,
cittakkhepaṁ va[92] pāpuṇe.

139. 149 Rājato vā upasaggaṁ[93],
Abbhakkhānaṁ va[94] dāruṇaṁ;
Parikkhayaṁ va[95] ñātīnaṁ,
Bhogānaṁ va[96] pabhaṅguraṁ[97].

140. 150 Atha vāssa agārāni,
aggi ḍahati[98] pāvako;
Kāyassa bhedā duppañño,
nirayaṁ sopapajjati[99].

  1. bhedanaṁ: sarīrassa pabhedanaṁ (syā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. va: cittakkhepañca (pā1-2, cha1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. upasaggaṁ: upassaggaṁ (sī, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. va: abbhakkhānañca (cha1) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. va: parikkhayañca (cha1) (Word Variant) ↩
  6. va: bhogānañca (cha1) (Word Variant) ↩
  7. pabhaṅguraṁ: pabhaṅguṇaṁ (syā1-3, pā1-2), pabhaṅgunaṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  8. ḍahati: ḍayhati (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  9. sopapajjati: so upapajjati (sī, syā1-3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
