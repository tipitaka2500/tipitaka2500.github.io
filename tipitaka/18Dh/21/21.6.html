21.6 Vajjiputtakabhikkhuvatthu

21.6. วัช์ชิปุต์ตกภิก์ขุวัต์ถุ

Pakiṇṇakavagga

Vajjiputtakabhikkhuvatthu

302. 325 [189] Duppabbajjaṁ durabhiramaṁ,
Durāvāsā gharā dukhā;
Dukkhosamānasaṁvāso,
Dukkhānupatitaddhagū;
Tasmā na ca[190]ddhagū siyā,
Na ca dukkhānupatito[191] siyā.

  1. (Open) ↩
  2. ca: tasmā na caddhagū siyā (sī), tasmā na caddhagū na ca (ka-ma) (Close) ↩
  3. dukkhānupatito: dukkhānupātito (?) (Word Variant) ↩
