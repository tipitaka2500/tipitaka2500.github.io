21.7. จิต์ตคหปติวัต์ถุ

Pakiṇṇakavagga

Cittagahapativatthu

303. 326 Saddho sīlena sampanno,
yasobhogasamappito;
Yaṁ yaṁ padesaṁ bhajati,
tattha tattheva pūjito.
