5.14 Cittagahapativatthu

5.14. จิต์ตคหปติวัต์ถุ

Bālavagga

Cittagahapativatthu

73. 78 Asantaṁ bhāvanamiccheyya[52],
Purekkhārañca bhikkhusu;
Āvāsesu ca issariyaṁ,
Pūjaṁ parakulesu ca.

74. 79 Mameva kata maññantu,
gihī pabbajitā ubho;
Mamevātivasā assu,
kiccākiccesu kismici;
Iti bālassa saṅkappo,
icchā māno ca vaḍḍhati.

  1. bhāvanamiccheyya: asantaṁ bhāvamiccheyya (syā1-3), asantabhāvanamiccheyya (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
