Sahassavagga

Sāriputtattherassa mātulabrāhmaṇavatthu

106. 114 Māse māse sahassena,
yo yajetha sataṁ samaṁ;
Ekañca bhāvitattānaṁ,
muhuttamapi pūjaye;
Sāyeva pūjanā seyyo,
yañce vassasataṁ hutaṁ.
