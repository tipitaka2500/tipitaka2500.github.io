11.9. มหาธนเสฏ์ิปุต์ตวัต์ถุ

Jarāvagga

Mahādhanaseṭṭhiputtavatthu

155. 166 [111] Acaritvā brahmacariyaṁ,
aladdhā yobbane dhanaṁ;
Jiṇṇakoñcāva jhāyanti,
khīṇamaccheva pallale.

156. 167 Acaritvā brahmacariyaṁ,
aladdhā yobbane dhanaṁ;
Senti cāpātikhīṇāva,
purāṇāni anutthunaṁ.

168 Jarāvaggo ekādasamo.
