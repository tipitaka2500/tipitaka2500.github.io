14.6 Aggidattabrāhmaṇavatthu

14.6. อัค์คิทัต์ตพ๎ราห๎มณวัต์ถุ

Buddhavagga

Aggidattabrāhmaṇavatthu

188. 202 Bahuṁ ve saraṇaṁ yanti,
pabbatāni vanāni ca;
Ārāmarukkhacetyāni,
manussā bhayatajjitā.

189. 203 Netaṁ kho saraṇaṁ khemaṁ,
netaṁ saraṇamuttamaṁ;
Netaṁ saraṇamāgamma,
sabbadukkhā pamuccati.

190. 204 Yo ca buddhañca dhammañca,
saṁghañca saraṇaṁ gato;
Cattāri ariyasaccāni,
sammappaññāya passati.

191. 205 Dukkhaṁ dukkhasamuppādaṁ,
dukkhassa ca atikkamaṁ;
Ariyaṁ caṭṭhaṅgikaṁ maggaṁ,
dukkhūpasamagāminaṁ.

192. 206 Etaṁ kho saraṇaṁ khemaṁ,
etaṁ saraṇamuttamaṁ;
Etaṁ saraṇamāgamma,
sabbadukkhā pamuccati.
