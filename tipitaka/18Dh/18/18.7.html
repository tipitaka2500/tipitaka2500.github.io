18.7 Pañcaupāsakavatthu

18.7. ปั์จอุปาสกวัต์ถุ

Malavagga

Pañcaupāsakavatthu

246. 266 [160] Yo pāṇamatipāteti,
musāvādañca bhāsati;
Loke adinnamādiyati,
paradārañca gacchati.

247. 267 Surāmerayapānañca,
yo naro anuyuñjati;
Idheva meso lokasmiṁ,
mūlaṁ khaṇati attano.

248. 268 Evaṁ bho purisa jānāhi,
pāpadhammā asaññatā;
Mā taṁ lobho adhammo ca,
ciraṁ dukkhāya randhayuṁ.
