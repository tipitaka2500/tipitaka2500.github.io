18.8 Tissadaharavatthu

18.8. ติส์สทหรวัต์ถุ

Malavagga

Tissadaharavatthu

249. 269 Dadāti ve yathāsaddhaṁ,
yathāpasādanaṁ jano;
Tattha yo ca maṅku bhavati[162],
paresaṁ pānabhojane;
Na so divā vā rattiṁ vā,
samādhimadhigacchati. [161]

250. 270 Yassa cetaṁ samucchinnaṁ,
mūlaghaccaṁ samūhataṁ;
Sa ve divā vā rattiṁ vā,
samādhimadhigacchati. [163]

  1. yathāpasādanaṁ: yattha pasādanaṁ (katthaci) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. bhavati: tattha ce maṁku yo hoti (sī), tattha yo maṅkuto hoti (syā1-3), tattha yo maṅku hoti (pā1), tattha yo maṅku bhavati (pā2) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. mūlaghaccaṁ: mulaghaccaṁ (syā1-3), mūlaghacchaṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
