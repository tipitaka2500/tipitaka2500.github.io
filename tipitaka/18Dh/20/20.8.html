20.8 Pañcamahallakabhikkhuvatthu

20.8. ปัญ์จมหัล์ลกภิก์ขุวัต์ถุ

Maggavagga

Pañcamahallakabhikkhuvatthu

283. 305 Vanaṁ chindatha mā rukkhaṁ,
vanato jāyate bhayaṁ;
Chetvā vanañca vanathañca,
nibbanā hotha bhikkhavo.

284. 306 Yāva hi vanatho na chijjati,
Aṇumattopi narassa nārisu;
Paṭibaddhamanova tāva so,
Vaccho khīrapakova[182] mātari. [181]

  1. Paṭibaddhamanova: paṭibandhamanova (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. khīrapakova: khīrapānova (ka-sī) (Word Variant) ↩
