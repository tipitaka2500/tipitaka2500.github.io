20.1 Pañcasatabhikkhuvatthu

20.1. ปัญ์จสตภิก์ขุวัต์ถุ

Maggavagga

Pañcasatabhikkhuvatthu

273. 295 [170] Maggānaṭṭhaṅgiko seṭṭho,
saccānaṁ caturo padā;
Virāgo seṭṭho dhammānaṁ,
dvipadānañca cakkhumā.

274. 296 [171] Eseva maggo natthañño,
Dassanassa visuddhiyā;
Etañhi tumhe paṭipajjatha,
Mārassetaṁ pamohanaṁ. [172]

275. 297 Etañhi tumhe paṭipannā,
dukkhassantaṁ karissatha;
Akkhāto vo[173] mayā maggo,
aññāya sallakantanaṁ[174].

276. 298 Tumhehi kiccamātappaṁ,
akkhātāro tathāgatā;
Paṭipannā pamokkhanti,
jhāyino mārabandhanā.
