25.1 Pañcabhikkhuvatthu

25.1. ปัญ์จภิก์ขุวัต์ถุ

Bhikkhuvagga

Pañcabhikkhuvatthu

360. 387 Cakkhunā saṁvaro sādhu,
sādhu sotena saṁvaro;
Ghānena saṁvaro sādhu,
sādhu jivhāya saṁvaro.

361. 388 [226] Kāyena saṁvaro sādhu,
sādhu vācāya saṁvaro;
Manasā saṁvaro sādhu,
sādhu sabbattha saṁvaro;
Sabbattha saṁvuto bhikkhu,
sabbadukkhā pamuccati.
