25.7 Sambahulabhikkhuvatthu

25.7. สัม์พหุลภิก์ขุวัต์ถุ

Bhikkhuvagga

Sambahulabhikkhuvatthu

368. 395 Mettāvihārī yo bhikkhu,
pasanno buddhasāsane;
Adhigacche padaṁ santaṁ,
saṅkhārūpasamaṁ sukhaṁ.

369. 396 Siñca bhikkhu imaṁ nāvaṁ,
sittā te lahumessati;
Chetvā rāgañca dosañca,
tato nibbānamehisi.

370. 397 [227] Pañca chinde pañca jahe,
pañca cuttari bhāvaye;
Pañca saṅgātigo bhikkhu,
“oghatiṇṇo”ti vuccati.

371. 398 Jhāya bhikkhu[228] mā pamādo[229],
Mā te kāmaguṇe ramessu[230] cittaṁ;
Mā lohaguḷaṁ gilī pamatto,
Mā kandi “dukkhamidan”ti dayhamāno.

372. 399 Natthi jhānaṁ apaññassa,
paññā natthi ajhāyato[231];
Yamhi jhānañca paññā ca,
sa ve nibbānasantike.

373. 400 Suññāgāraṁ paviṭṭhassa,
santacittassa bhikkhuno;
Amānusī rati hoti,
sammā dhammaṁ vipassato.

374. 401 Yato yato sammasati,
khandhānaṁ udayabbayaṁ;
Labhatī pītipāmojjaṁ,
amataṁ taṁ vijānataṁ. [232]

375. 402 Tatrāyamādi bhavati,
idha paññassa bhikkhuno;
Indriyagutti santuṭṭhi,
pātimokkhe ca saṁvaro.

376. 403 Mitte bhajassu kalyāṇe,
suddhājīve atandite;
Paṭisanthāravutyassa,
ācārakusalo siyā;
Tato pāmojjabahulo,
dukkhassantaṁ karissati. [233]
