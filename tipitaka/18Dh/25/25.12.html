25.12. สุมนสามเณรวัต์ถุ

Bhikkhuvagga

Sumanasāmaṇeravatthu

382. 409 Yo have daharo bhikkhu,
yuñjati buddhasāsane;
Somaṁ lokaṁ pabhāseti,
abbhā muttova candimā. [240]

410 Bhikkhuvaggo pañcavīsatimo.
