25.8. ปัญ์จสตภิก์ขุวัต์ถุ

Bhikkhuvagga

Pañcasatabhikkhuvatthu

377. 404 Vassikā viya pupphāni,
maddavāni pamuñcati;
Evaṁ rāgañca dosañca,
vippamuñcetha bhikkhavo. [234]
