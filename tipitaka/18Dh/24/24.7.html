24.7 Cūḷadhanuggahapaṇḍitavatthu

24.7. จูฬธนุค์คหปัณ์ฑิตวัต์ถุ

Taṇhāvagga

Cūḷadhanuggahapaṇḍitavatthu

349. 375 [217] Vitakkamathitassa jantuno,
Tibbarāgassa subhānupassino;
Bhiyyo taṇhā pavaḍḍhati,
Esa kho daḷhaṁ[218] karoti bandhanaṁ.

350. 376 [219] Vitakkūpasame ca[220] yo rato,
Asubhaṁ bhāvayate sadā sato;
Esa kho byanti kāhiti,
Esa checchati mārabandhanaṁ. [221] [222]
