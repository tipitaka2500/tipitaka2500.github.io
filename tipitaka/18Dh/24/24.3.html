24.3 Vibbhantabhikkhuvatthu

24.3. วิพ์ภัน์ตภิก์ขุวัต์ถุ

Taṇhāvagga

Vibbhantabhikkhuvatthu

344. 370 Yo nibbanatho vanādhimutto,
Vanamutto vanameva dhāvati;
Taṁ puggalametha passatha,
Mutto bandhanameva dhāvati.
