9.12. สุป์ปพุท์ธสัก๎ยวัต์ถุ

Pāpavagga

Suppabuddhasakyavatthu

128. 137 Na antalikkhe na samuddamajjhe,
Na pabbatānaṁ vivaraṁ pavissa;
Na vijjatī so jagatippadeso,
Yatthaṭṭhitaṁ nappasaheyya maccu. [86]

138 Pāpavaggo navamo.

  1. Yatthaṭṭhitaṁ: yatraṭṭhitaṁ (syā1-3) (Word Variant) ↩
