3.3 Rathakārapetivatthu

3.3. รถการเปติวัต์ถุ

Cūḷavagga

Rathakārapetivatthu

439. 474 “Veḷuriyathambhaṁ ruciraṁ pabhassaraṁ,
Vimānamāruyha anekacittaṁ;
Tatthacchasi devi mahānubhāve,
Pathaddhani pannaraseva cando. [148]

440. 475 Vaṇṇo ca te kanakassa sannibho,
Uttattarūpo bhusa dassaneyyo;
Pallaṅkaseṭṭhe atule nisinnā,
Ekā tuvaṁ natthi ca tuyha sāmiko.

441. 476 Imā ca te pokkharaṇī samantā,
Pahūtamalyā bahupuṇḍarīkā;
Suvaṇṇacuṇṇehi samantamotthatā,
Na tattha paṅko paṇako ca vijjati. [149]

442. 477 Haṁsā cime dassanīyā manoramā,
Udakasmimanupariyanti sabbadā;
Samayya vaggūpanadanti sabbe,
Bindussarā dundubhīnaṁva ghoso.

443. 478 Daddallamānā yasasā yasassinī,
Nāvāya ca tvaṁ avalamba tiṭṭhasi;
Āḷārapamhe hasite piyaṁvade,
Sabbaṅgakalyāṇi bhusaṁ virocasi.

444. 479 Idaṁ vimānaṁ virajaṁ same ṭhitaṁ,
Uyyānavantaṁ ratinandivaḍḍhanaṁ;
Icchāmahaṁ nāri anomadassane,
Tayā saha nandane idha moditun”ti. [150]

445. 480 “Karohi kammaṁ idha vedanīyaṁ,
Cittañca te idha nihitaṁ bhavatu[151];
Katvāna kammaṁ idha vedanīyaṁ,
Evaṁ mamaṁ lacchasi kāmakāminin”ti.

446. 481 “Sādhū”ti so tassā paṭissuṇitvā,
Akāsi kammaṁ tahiṁ vedanīyaṁ;
Katvāna kammaṁ tahiṁ vedanīyaṁ,
Upapajji so māṇavo tassā sahabyatanti.

482 Rathakārapetivatthu[152] tatiyaṁ.
 Bhāṇavāraṁ dutiyaṁ.

  1. Pathaddhani: samantato (ka-sī, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Pahūtamalyā: pahūtamālā (sī), pahūtamāsā (syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Uyyānavantaṁ: uyyānavanaṁ (syā2, syā3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. bhavatu: nitañca hotu (syā2, syā3), natañca hotu (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. Rathakārapetivatthu: rathakārīpetavatthu (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
