Hetvādi

334. 697 Hetu sekkho dhammo hetussa sekkhassa dhammassa hetupaccayena paccayo… tīṇi.

698 Hetu sekkho dhammo hetussa sekkhassa dhammassa adhipatipaccayena paccayo— sahajātādhipati… tīṇi.

699 Nahetu sekkho dhammo nahetussa sekkhassa dhammassa adhipatipaccayena paccayo— sahajātādhipati… tīṇi.

700 Hetu sekkho dhammo hetussa sekkhassa dhammassa anantarapaccayena paccayo. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

335. 701 Hetuyā tīṇi, adhipatiyā cha, anantare nava, samanantare nava, sahajāte nava, aññamaññe nava, nissaye nava, upanissaye nava, kamme tīṇi, vipāke nava, āhāre tīṇi, indriye nava, jhāne tīṇi, magge nava, sampayutte nava, atthiyā nava, natthiyā nava, vigate nava, avigate nava. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)
