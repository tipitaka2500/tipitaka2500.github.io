Hetvādi

128. 226 Hetu vipākadhammadhammo hetussa vipākadhammadhammassa hetupaccayena paccayo… tīṇi.

129. 227 Hetu vipākadhammadhammo hetussa vipākadhammadhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo… nava.

130. 228 Hetu vipākadhammadhammo hetussa vipākadhammadhammassa adhipatipaccayena paccayo— ārammaṇādhipati, sahajātādhipati… nava.

131. 229 Hetu vipākadhammadhammo hetussa vipākadhammadhammassa anantarapaccayena paccayo…pe… upanissayapaccayena paccayo— ārammaṇūpanissayo, anantarūpanissayo, pakatūpanissayo… nava… āsevanapaccayena paccayo… nava.

132. 230 Nahetu vipākadhammadhammo nahetussa vipākadhammadhammassa kammapaccayena paccayo… tīṇi.

231 Nahetu vipākadhammadhammo nahetussa vipākadhammadhammassa āhārapaccayena paccayo. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

133. 232 Hetuyā tīṇi, ārammaṇe nava, adhipatiyā nava, anantare nava, samanantare nava, sahajāte nava, aññamaññe nava, nissaye nava, upanissaye nava, āsevane nava, kamme tīṇi, āhāre tīṇi, indriye nava, jhāne tīṇi, magge nava, sampayutte nava, atthiyā nava, natthiyā nava, vigate nava, avigate nava. (Saṁkhittaṁ, anulomaṁ.)
