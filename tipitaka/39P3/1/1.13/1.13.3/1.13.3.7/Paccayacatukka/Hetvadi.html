Paccayacatukka

Hetvādi

401. 865 Hetu appamāṇārammaṇo dhammo hetussa appamāṇārammaṇassa dhammassa hetupaccayena paccayo… tīṇi.

866 Hetu appamāṇārammaṇo dhammo hetussa appamāṇārammaṇassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo… nava.

867 Hetu appamāṇārammaṇo dhammo hetussa appamāṇārammaṇassa dhammassa adhipatipaccayena paccayo— ārammaṇādhipati, sahajātādhipati… tīṇi.

868 Nahetu appamāṇārammaṇo dhammo nahetussa appamāṇārammaṇassa dhammassa adhipatipaccayena paccayo— ārammaṇādhipati, sahajātādhipati… tīṇi.

869 Hetu appamāṇārammaṇo ca nahetu appamāṇārammaṇo ca dhammā hetussa appamāṇārammaṇassa dhammassa adhipatipaccayena paccayo— ārammaṇādhipati… tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

402. 870 Hetuyā tīṇi, ārammaṇe nava, adhipatiyā nava, anantare nava, samanantare nava, sahajāte nava, aññamaññe nava, nissaye nava, upanissaye nava, āsevane nava, kamme tīṇi, vipāke nava, āhāre tīṇi, indriye nava, jhāne tīṇi, magge nava, sampayutte nava, atthiyā nava…pe… avigate nava. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)
