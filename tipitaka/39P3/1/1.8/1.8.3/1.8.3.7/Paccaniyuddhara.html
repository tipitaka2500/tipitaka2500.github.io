Paccanīyuddhāra

280. 569 Hetu nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbo dhammo hetussa nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo… sahajātapaccayena paccayo… upanissayapaccayena paccayo. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

281. 570 Nahetuyā nava, naārammaṇe nava. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

571 Hetupaccayā naārammaṇe tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

572 Nahetupaccayā ārammaṇe nava. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

573 (Yathā kusalattike pañhāvārassa anulomampi paccanīyampi anulomapaccanīyampi paccanīyānulomampi gaṇitaṁ, evaṁ gaṇetabbaṁ.)
