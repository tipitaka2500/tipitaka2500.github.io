Hetvādi

246. 488 Hetu sukhasahagato dhammo hetussa sukhasahagatassa dhammassa hetupaccayena paccayo… tīṇi.

489 Hetu sukhasahagato dhammo hetussa sukhasahagatassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo… nava.

490 Hetu sukhasahagato dhammo hetussa sukhasahagatassa dhammassa adhipatipaccayena paccayo… nava. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

247. 491 Hetuyā tīṇi, ārammaṇe nava, adhipatiyā nava, anantare nava, samanantare nava, sahajāte nava, aññamaññe nava, nissaye nava, upanissaye nava, āsevane nava, kamme tīṇi, vipāke nava, āhāre tīṇi, indriye nava, jhāne tīṇi, magge nava, sampayutte nava, atthiyā nava, natthiyā nava, vigate nava, avigate nava. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)
