2303 Hetuyā ekaṁ, ārammaṇe ekaṁ…pe… avigate ekaṁ. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

2. 2305 Kilesaṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca kileso akusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi.

2306 Nokilesaṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nokileso akusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi.

2307 Kilesaṁ akusalañca nokilesaṁ akusalañca dhammaṁ paṭicca kileso akusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

3. 2308 Hetuyā nava, ārammaṇe nava (sabbattha nava), avigate nava. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

4. 2309 Kilesaṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca kileso akusalo dhammo uppajjati nahetupaccayā— vicikicchaṁ paṭicca vicikicchāsahagato moho, uddhaccaṁ paṭicca uddhaccasahagato moho. (1)

2310 Nokilesaṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca kileso akusalo dhammo uppajjati nahetupaccayā— vicikicchāsahagate uddhaccasahagate khandhe paṭicca vicikicchāsahagato uddhaccasahagato moho. (1)

2311 Kilesaṁ akusalañca nokilesaṁ akusalañca dhammaṁ paṭicca kileso akusalo dhammo uppajjati nahetupaccayā— vicikicchāsahagate khandhe ca vicikicchañca paṭicca vicikicchāsahagato moho, uddhaccasahagate khandhe ca uddhaccañca paṭicca uddhaccasahagato moho. (1) (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

5. 2312 Nahetuyā tīṇi, naadhipatiyā nava, napurejāte nava, napacchājāte nava, naāsevane nava, nakamme tīṇi, navipāke nava, navippayutte nava. (Saṁkhittaṁ, sahajātavārādi vitthāretabbo.)

6. 2313 Kileso akusalo dhammo kilesassa akusalassa dhammassa hetupaccayena paccayo. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

7. 2314 Hetuyā tīṇi, ārammaṇe nava, adhipatiyā nava (majjhe tīṇi sahajātādhipati), anantare nava, samanantare nava…pe… upanissaye nava, āsevane nava, kamme tīṇi, āhāre tīṇi, indriye tīṇi, jhāne tīṇi, magge nava, sampayutte nava…pe… avigate nava. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

2315 Nokilesaṁ abyākataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nokileso abyākato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)
