84. 2185 Ajjhattikaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca bāhiro kusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (1)

2186 Bāhiraṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca bāhiro kusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Bāhiraṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca ajjhattiko kusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Bāhiraṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca ajjhattiko kusalo ca bāhiro kusalo ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā. (3)

2187 Ajjhattikaṁ kusalañca bāhiraṁ kusalañca dhammaṁ paṭicca bāhiro kusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (1) (Saṁkhittaṁ.)
