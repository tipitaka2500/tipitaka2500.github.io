26. 157 » Yattha kāyasaṅkhāro nuppajjati tattha vacīsaṅkhāro nuppajjatīti?

158 Rūpāvacare arūpāvacare tattha kāyasaṅkhāro nuppajjati, no ca tattha vacīsaṅkhāro nuppajjati. Catutthajjhāne asaññasatte tattha kāyasaṅkhāro ca nuppajjati vacīsaṅkhāro ca nuppajjati.

159 « Yattha vā pana vacīsaṅkhāro nuppajjati tattha kāyasaṅkhāro nuppajjatīti?

160 Dutiyajjhāne tatiyajjhāne tattha vacīsaṅkhāro nuppajjati, no ca tattha kāyasaṅkhāro nuppajjati. Catutthajjhāne asaññasatte tattha vacīsaṅkhāro ca nuppajjati kāyasaṅkhāro ca nuppajjati.

161 » Yattha kāyasaṅkhāro nuppajjati tattha cittasaṅkhāro nuppajjatīti?

162 Catutthajjhāne rūpāvacare arūpāvacare tattha kāyasaṅkhāro nuppajjati, no ca tattha cittasaṅkhāro nuppajjati. Asaññasatte tattha kāyasaṅkhāro ca nuppajjati cittasaṅkhāro ca nuppajjati.
