53. สนิทัส์สนัต์ติก, สนิทัส์สนทุก

Sanidassanattika, Sanidassanaduka

96. 237 Sanidassanasappaṭigho nasanidassano dhammo nasanidassanasappaṭighassa nasanidassanassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Ārammaṇe tīṇi, adhipatiyā tīṇi, upanissaye purejāte atthiyā avigate tīṇi.

238 Anidassanasappaṭighaṁ anidassanaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naanidassanasappaṭigho naanidassano dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā nava, adhipatiyā nava…pe… avigate nava.
