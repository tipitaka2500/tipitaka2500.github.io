93. 231 Anidassanaappaṭighaṁ hetuñceva hetusampayuttañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nasanidassanasappaṭigho nahetu ceva nahetuvippayutto ca dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.
