74. สนิทัส์สนัต์ติก, อัช์ฌัต์ตทุก

Sanidassanattika, Ajjhattaduka

122. 293 Anidassanaappaṭighaṁ ajjhattikaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naanidassanaappaṭigho naajjhattiko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Anidassanaappaṭighaṁ ajjhattikaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasanidassanasappaṭigho naajjhattiko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā…pe… anidassanaappaṭighaṁ ajjhattikaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasanidassanasappaṭigho naajjhattiko ca naanidassanasappaṭigho naajjhattiko ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā cha. Ārammaṇe tīṇi…pe… avigate cha.

294 Anidassanasappaṭighaṁ bāhiraṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasanidassanasappaṭigho nabāhiro dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā ekādasa, ārammaṇe tīṇi…pe… avigate ekādasa.
