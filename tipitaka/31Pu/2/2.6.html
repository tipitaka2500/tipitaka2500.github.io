2.6 Chakkapuggalapaññatti

2.6. ฉัก์กปุค์คลปั์ัต์ติ

Niddesa

Chakkapuggalapaññatti

202. 512 Tatra yvāyaṁ puggalo pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu sāmaṁ saccāni abhisambujjhati, tattha ca sabbaññutaṁ pāpuṇāti balesu ca vasībhāvaṁ, sammāsambuddho tena daṭṭhabbo. (1)

513 Tatra yvāyaṁ puggalo pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu sāmaṁ saccāni abhisambujjhati, na ca tattha sabbaññutaṁ pāpuṇāti na ca balesu vasībhāvaṁ, paccekasambuddho tena daṭṭhabbo. (2)

514 Tatra yvāyaṁ puggalo pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu sāmaṁ saccāni anabhisambujjhati, diṭṭheva dhamme dukkhassantakaro hoti[170], sāvakapāramiñca pāpuṇāti, sāriputtamoggallānā tena daṭṭhabbā. (3)

515 Tatra yvāyaṁ puggalo pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu sāmaṁ saccāni anabhisambujjhati, diṭṭheva dhamme dukkhassantakaro hoti, na ca sāvakapāramiṁ pāpuṇāti, avasesā arahantā tena daṭṭhabbā. (4)

516 Tatra yvāyaṁ puggalo pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu sāmaṁ saccāni anabhisambujjhati, na ca diṭṭheva dhamme dukkhassantakaro hoti, anāgāmī hoti anāgantā itthattaṁ, anāgāmī tena daṭṭhabbo. (5)

517 Tatra yvāyaṁ puggalo pubbe ananussutesu dhammesu sāmaṁ saccāni anabhisambujjhati, na ca diṭṭheva dhamme dukkhassantakaro hoti, āgantā itthattaṁ, sotāpannasakadāgāmino tena daṭṭhabbā. (6)

518 Chakkaniddeso.

  1. hoti: dukkhassantaṁ karoti (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
