9.12. ปฏิสารณียกัม์มกถา

Campeyyakkhandhaka

Paṭisāraṇīyakammakathā

414. 1850 Idha pana, bhikkhave, bhikkhu gihī akkosati paribhāsati. Tatra ce bhikkhūnaṁ evaṁ hoti— ‘ayaṁ kho, āvuso, bhikkhu gihī akkosati paribhāsati. Handassa mayaṁ paṭisāraṇīyakammaṁ karomā’ti. Te tassa paṭisāraṇīyakammaṁ karonti— adhammena vaggā. So tamhā āvāsā aññaṁ āvāsaṁ gacchati. Tatthapi bhikkhūnaṁ evaṁ hoti— ‘ayaṁ kho, āvuso, bhikkhu saṁghena paṭisāraṇīyakammakato adhammena vaggehi. Handassa mayaṁ paṭisāraṇīyakammaṁ karomā’ti. Te tassa paṭisāraṇīyakammaṁ karonti— adhammena samaggā…pe… dhammena vaggā… dhammapatirūpakena vaggā… dhammapatirūpakena samaggā…pe… .

1851 Cakkaṁ kātabbaṁ.
