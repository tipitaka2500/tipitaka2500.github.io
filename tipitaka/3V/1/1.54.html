1.54 Ubhatobyañjanakavatthu

1.54. อุภโตพ๎ยัญ์ชนกวัต์ถุ

Mahākhandhaka

Ubhatobyañjanakavatthu

116. 490 Tena kho pana samayena aññataro ubhatobyañjanako bhikkhūsu pabbajito hoti. So karotipi kārāpetipi. Bhagavato etamatthaṁ ārocesuṁ. “Ubhatobyañjanako, bhikkhave, anupasampanno na upasampādetabbo, upasampanno nāsetabbo”ti. (11)
