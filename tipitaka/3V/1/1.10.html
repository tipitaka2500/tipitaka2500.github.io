1.10 Dutiyamārakathā

1.10. ทุติยมารกถา

Mahākhandhaka

Dutiyamārakathā

35. 147 Atha kho bhagavā vassaṁvuṭṭho[74] bhikkhū āmantesi— “mayhaṁ kho, bhikkhave, yoniso manasikārā yoniso sammappadhānā anuttarā vimutti anuppattā, anuttarā vimutti sacchikatā. Tumhepi, bhikkhave, yoniso manasikārā yoniso sammappadhānā anuttaraṁ vimuttiṁ anupāpuṇātha, anuttaraṁ vimuttiṁ sacchikarothā”ti. Atha kho māro pāpimā yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami, upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ gāthāya ajjhabhāsi—

148 “Baddhosi mārapāsehi,
ye dibbā ye ca mānusā;
Mahābandhanabaddhosi,
na me samaṇa mokkhasī”ti. [75]

149 “Muttāhaṁ mārapāsehi,
Ye dibbā ye ca mānusā;
Mahābandhanamuttomhi,
Nihato tvamasi antakā”ti. [76]

150 Atha kho māro pāpimā— “jānāti maṁ bhagavā, jānāti maṁ sugato”ti dukkhī dummano tatthevantaradhāyi.

151 Dutiyamārakathā niṭṭhitā.

  1. vassaṁvuṭṭho: vassaṁ vuttho (sī, syā1, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Mahābandhanabaddhosi: mārabandhanabaddhosi (sī, syā1-3) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Mahābandhanamuttomhi: mārabandhanamuttomhi (sī, syā1-3) (Word Variant) ↩
