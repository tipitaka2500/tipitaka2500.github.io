1.8.4 Uparibhāgapariyāya

1.8.4. อุปริภาคปริยาย

Mūlapariyāyavagga

Sallekhasutta

Uparibhāgapariyāya

86. 251 Seyyathāpi, cunda, ye keci akusalā dhammā sabbe te adhobhāgaṅgamanīyā[76], ye keci kusalā dhammā sabbe te uparibhāgaṅgamanīyā[77]; evameva kho, cunda, vihiṁsakassa purisapuggalassa avihiṁsā hoti uparibhāgāya[78], pāṇātipātissa purisapuggalassa pāṇātipātā veramaṇī hoti uparibhāgāya…pe… sandiṭṭhiparāmāsiādhānaggāhiduppaṭinissaggissa purisapuggalassa asandiṭṭhiparāmāsianādhānaggāhisuppaṭinissaggitā hoti uparibhāgāya. (44)

  1. adhobhāgaṅgamanīyā: adhobhāvaṅgamanīyā (sī, syā1-3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. uparibhāgaṅgamanīyā: uparibhāvaṅgamanīyā (sī, syā1-3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. uparibhāgāya: uparibhāvāya (sī, syā1-3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
