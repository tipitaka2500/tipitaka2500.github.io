Vīriyādhipatighaṭanā

315. 1026 Adhipati sahajāta nissaya indriya magga atthi avigatanti satta. Adhipati sahajāta aññamañña nissaya indriya magga sampayutta atthi avigatanti tīṇi. Adhipati sahajāta nissaya indriya magga vippayutta atthi vigatanti tīṇi. [Avipāka— 3]

1027 Adhipati sahajāta nissaya vipāka indriya magga atthi avigatanti ekaṁ. Adhipati sahajāta aññamañña nissaya vipāka indriya magga sampayutta atthi avigatanti ekaṁ. Adhipati sahajāta nissaya vipāka indriya magga vippayutta atthi avigatanti ekaṁ. [Savipāka— 3]
