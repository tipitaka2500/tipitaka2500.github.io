370. 1123 Maggādhipati sahajāta nissaya indriya atthi avigatanti satta. Maggādhipati sahajāta aññamañña nissaya indriya sampayutta atthi avigatanti tīṇi. Maggādhipati sahajāta nissaya indriya vippayutta atthi avigatanti tīṇi. [Avipāka— 3]

1124 Maggādhipati sahajāta nissaya vipāka indriya atthi avigatanti ekaṁ. Maggādhipati sahajāta aññamañña nissaya vipāka indriya sampayutta atthi avigatanti ekaṁ. Maggādhipati sahajāta nissaya vipāka indriya vippayutta atthi avigatanti ekaṁ. [Savipāka— 3]
