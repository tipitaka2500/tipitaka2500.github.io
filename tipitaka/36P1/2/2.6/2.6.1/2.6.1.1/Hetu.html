256. 818 Kusalaṁ dhammaṁ sampayutto kusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— kusalaṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ sampayuttā tayo khandhā, tayo khandhe sampayutto eko khandho, dve khandhe sampayuttā dve khandhā. (1)

257. 819 Akusalaṁ dhammaṁ sampayutto akusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— akusalaṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ sampayuttā tayo khandhā, tayo khandhe sampayutto eko khandho, dve khandhe sampayuttā dve khandhā. (1)
