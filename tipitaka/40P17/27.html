44. 123 Anidassanaappaṭighaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naanidassanaappaṭigho nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Anidassanaappaṭighaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasanidassanasappaṭigho nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā…pe… anidassanaappaṭighaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasanidassanasappaṭigho nakusalo ca naanidassanasappaṭigho nakusalo ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā cha, adhipatiyā cha…pe… avigate cha.
