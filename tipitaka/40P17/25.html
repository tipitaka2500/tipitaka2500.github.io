Vitakkattika, Kusalattika

42. 116 Savitakkasavicāraṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasavitakkasavicāro nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi.

117 Avitakkavicāramattaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naavitakkavicāramatto nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā pannarasa, adhipatiyā pannarasa…pe… avigate pannarasa.

118 Savitakkasavicāraṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasavitakkasavicāro naakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā nava, adhipatiyā nava…pe… avigate nava.
