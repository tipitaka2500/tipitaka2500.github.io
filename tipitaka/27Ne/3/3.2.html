3.2 Nayasaṅkhepa

3.2. นยสํเขป

Niddesavāra

Nayasaṅkhepa

41 Taṇhañca avijjampi ca,
Samathena vipassanāya yo neti;
Saccehi yojayitvā,
Ayaṁ nayo nandiyāvaṭṭo.

42 Yo akusale samūlehi,
Neti kusale ca kusalamūlehi;
Bhūtaṁ tathaṁ avitathaṁ,
Tipukkhalaṁ taṁ nayaṁ āhu.

43 Yo neti vipallāsehi,
Kilese indriyehi saddhamme;
Etaṁ nayaṁ nayavidū,
Sīhavikkīḷitaṁ āhu.

44 Veyyākaraṇesu hi ye,
Kusalākusalā tahiṁ tahiṁ vuttā;
Manasā volokayate,
Taṁ khu disālocanaṁ āhu.

45 [13] Oloketvā disalocanena,
Ukkhipiya yaṁ samāneti;
Sabbe kusalākusale,
Ayaṁ nayo aṅkuso nāma.

46 Soḷasa hārā paṭhamaṁ,
Disalocanato disā viloketvā;
Saṅkhipiya aṅkusena hi,
Nayehi tīhi niddise suttaṁ. [14]
