3.2.1. อาปายิกสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Āpāyikavagga

Āpāyikasutta

114. 908 “Tayome, bhikkhave, āpāyikā nerayikā idamappahāya. Katame tayo? Yo ca abrahmacārī brahmacāripaṭiñño, yo ca suddhaṁ brahmacariyaṁ carantaṁ amūlakena[407] abrahmacariyena anuddhaṁseti, yo cāyaṁ evaṁvādī evaṁdiṭṭhi— ‘natthi kāmesu doso’ti, so tāya kāmesu pātabyataṁ āpajjati. Ime kho, bhikkhave, tayo āpāyikā nerayikā idamappahāyā”ti.

909 Paṭhamaṁ.

  1. amūlakena: abhūtena (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
