3.4.12 Dutiyamoranivāpasutta

3.4.12. ทุติยโมรนิวาปสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Yodhājīvavagga

Dutiyamoranivāpasutta

145. 1056 “Tīhi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu accantaniṭṭho hoti accantayogakkhemī accantabrahmacārī accantapariyosāno seṭṭho devamanussānaṁ. Katamehi tīhi? Iddhipāṭihāriyena, ādesanāpāṭihāriyena, anusāsanīpāṭihāriyena— imehi kho, bhikkhave, tīhi dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu accantaniṭṭho hoti accantayogakkhemī accantabrahmacārī accantapariyosāno seṭṭho devamanussānan”ti.

1057 Dvādasamaṁ.
