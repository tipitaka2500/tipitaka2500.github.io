3.5.10 Pubbaṇhasutta

3.5.10. ปุพ์พัณ๎หสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Maṅgalavagga

Pubbaṇhasutta

156. 1089 “Ye, bhikkhave, sattā pubbaṇhasamayaṁ kāyena sucaritaṁ caranti, vācāya sucaritaṁ caranti, manasā sucaritaṁ caranti, supubbaṇho, bhikkhave, tesaṁ sattānaṁ. [454]

1090 Ye, bhikkhave, sattā majjhanhikasamayaṁ kāyena sucaritaṁ caranti, vācāya sucaritaṁ caranti, manasā sucaritaṁ caranti, sumajjhanhiko, bhikkhave, tesaṁ sattānaṁ.

1091 Ye, bhikkhave, sattā sāyanhasamayaṁ kāyena sucaritaṁ caranti, vācāya sucaritaṁ caranti, manasā sucaritaṁ caranti, susāyanho, bhikkhave, tesaṁ sattānanti.

1092 Sunakkhattaṁ sumaṅgalaṁ,
suppabhātaṁ suhuṭṭhitaṁ[455];
Sukhaṇo sumuhutto ca,
suyiṭṭhaṁ brahmacārisu.

1093 Padakkhiṇaṁ kāyakammaṁ,
vācākammaṁ padakkhiṇaṁ;
Padakkhiṇaṁ manokammaṁ,
paṇīdhi te padakkhiṇe[456];
Padakkhiṇāni katvāna,
labhantatthe padakkhiṇe. [457]

1094 Te atthaladdhā sukhitā,
viruḷhā buddhasāsane;
Arogā sukhitā hotha,
saha sabbehi ñātibhī”ti.

1095 Dasamaṁ.

1096 Maṅgalavaggo pañcamo.

1097

1098 Akusalañca sāvajjaṁ,
visamāsucinā saha;
Caturo khatā vandanā,
pubbaṇhena ca te dasāti.

1099 Tatiyo paṇṇāsako samatto.

  1. "Ye: ye hi (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. suhuṭṭhitaṁ: suvuṭṭhitaṁ (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. padakkhiṇe: paṇidhiyo padakkhiṇā (sī), paṇidhi te padakkhiṇā (syā1-3, kaṃ), paṇidhīyo padakkhiṇā (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. labhantatthe: labhatatthe (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
