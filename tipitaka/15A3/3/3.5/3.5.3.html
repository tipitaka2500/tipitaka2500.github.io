3.5.3. วิสมสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Maṅgalavagga

Visamasutta

149. 1069 “Tīhi, bhikkhave…pe… visamena kāyakammena, visamena vacīkammena, visamena manokammena— imehi kho…pe… evaṁ niraye.

1070 Tīhi, bhikkhave, dhammehi…pe… samena kāyakammena, samena vacīkammena, samena manokammena— imehi kho…pe… evaṁ sagge”ti.

1071 Tatiyaṁ.
