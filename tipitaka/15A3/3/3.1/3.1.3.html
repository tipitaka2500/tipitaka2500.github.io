3.1.3. ทุติยอัส์สาทสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Sambodhavagga

Dutiyaassādasutta

106. 876 “No cedaṁ[394], bhikkhave, loke assādo abhavissa, nayidaṁ sattā loke sārajjeyyuṁ. Yasmā ca kho, bhikkhave, atthi loke assādo, tasmā sattā loke sārajjanti. No cedaṁ, bhikkhave, loke ādīnavo abhavissa, nayidaṁ sattā loke nibbindeyyuṁ. Yasmā ca kho, bhikkhave, atthi loke ādīnavo, tasmā sattā loke nibbindanti. No cedaṁ, bhikkhave, loke nissaraṇaṁ abhavissa, nayidaṁ sattā lokamhā[395] nissareyyuṁ. Yasmā ca kho, bhikkhave, atthi loke nissaraṇaṁ, tasmā sattā lokamhā nissaranti. Yāvakīvañca, bhikkhave, sattā lokassa assādañca assādato ādīnavañca ādīnavato nissaraṇañca nissaraṇato yathābhūtaṁ nābbhaññāsuṁ[396], neva tāva, bhikkhave, sattā sadevakā lokā samārakā sabrahmakā sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiyā pajāya sadevamanussāya nissaṭā visaṁyuttā vippamuttā[397] vimariyādīkatena[398] cetasā vihariṁsu. Yato ca kho, bhikkhave, sattā lokassa assādañca assādato ādīnavañca ādīnavato nissaraṇañca nissaraṇato yathābhūtaṁ abbhaññāsuṁ, atha, bhikkhave, sattā sadevakā lokā samārakā sabrahmakā sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiyā pajāya sadevamanussāya nissaṭā visaṁyuttā vippamuttā vimariyādīkatena cetasā viharantī”ti.

877 Tatiyaṁ.

  1. cedaṁ: no cetaṁ (syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1, ka-ma) evamuparipi 13S3:140 (Word Variant) ↩
  2. lokamhā: loke (pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. nābbhaññāsuṁ: na abbhaññāsuṁ (sī, pā1), nābbhaññaṁsu (13S3:140) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. vippamuttā: vippayuttā (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. vimariyādīkatena: vimariyādikatena (pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
