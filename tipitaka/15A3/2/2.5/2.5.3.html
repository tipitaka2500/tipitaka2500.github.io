2.5.3 Saradasutta

2.5.3. สรทสุต์ต

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Loṇakapallavagga

Saradasutta

95. 801 “Seyyathāpi, bhikkhave, saradasamaye viddhe vigatavalāhake deve ādicco nabhaṁ abbhussakkamāno sabbaṁ ākāsagataṁ tamagataṁ abhivihacca bhāsate ca tapate ca virocati ca.

802 Evamevaṁ kho, bhikkhave, yato ariyasāvakassa virajaṁ vītamalaṁ dhammacakkhuṁ uppajjati[341], saha dassanuppādā, bhikkhave, ariyasāvakassa tīṇi saṁyojanāni pahīyanti— sakkāyadiṭṭhi, vicikicchā, sīlabbataparāmāso.

803 Athāparaṁ dvīhi dhammehi niyyāti abhijjhāya ca byāpādena ca. So vivicceva kāmehi vivicca akusalehi dhammehi savitakkaṁ savicāraṁ vivekajaṁ pītisukhaṁ paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharati. Tasmiñce, bhikkhave, samaye ariyasāvako kālaṁ kareyya, natthi taṁ[342] saṁyojanaṁ yena saṁyojanena saṁyutto ariyasāvako puna imaṁ[343] lokaṁ āgaccheyyā”ti.

804 Tatiyaṁ.

  1. uppajjati: udapādi (sabbattha) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. taṁ: tassa (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. imaṁ: punayimaṁ (syā1-3, kaṃ, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
