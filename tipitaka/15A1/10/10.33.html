Dutiyapamādādivagga

130. 236 “Ye te, bhikkhave, bhikkhū adhammaṁ dhammoti dīpenti te, bhikkhave, bhikkhū bahujanaahitāya[23] paṭipannā bahujanaasukhāya[24], bahuno janassa anatthāya ahitāya dukkhāya devamanussānaṁ. Bahuñca te, bhikkhave, bhikkhū apuññaṁ pasavanti, te cimaṁ[25] saddhammaṁ antaradhāpentī”ti.

237 Tettiṁsatimaṁ.

  1. bahujanaahitāya: bahujanāhitāya (sī, syā1-3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. bahujanaasukhāya: bahujanāsukhāya (sī, syā1-3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. cimaṁ: tepimaṁ (ka-sī) (Word Variant) ↩
