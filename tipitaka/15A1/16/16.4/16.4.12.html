16.4.12.

Ekadhammapāḷi

Catutthavagga

333. 424 … Evamevaṁ kho, bhikkhave, appakā te sattā ye vavassaggārammaṇaṁ karitvā labhanti samādhiṁ[62] labhanti cittassekaggataṁ[63]; atha kho eteva sattā bahutarā ye vavassaggārammaṇaṁ karitvā na labhanti samādhiṁ na labhanti cittassekaggataṁ.

  1. samādhiṁ: cittassa samādhiṁ (ka-sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. cittassekaggataṁ: cittassa ekaggaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
