16.3.4

16.3.4.

Ekadhammapāḷi

Tatiyavagga

311. 400 “Nāhaṁ, bhikkhave, aññaṁ ekapuggalampi samanupassāmi yo evaṁ bahujanaahitāya paṭipanno bahujanaasukhāya, bahuno janassa anatthāya ahitāya dukkhāya devamanussānaṁ yathayidaṁ, bhikkhave, makkhali moghapuriso. Seyyathāpi, bhikkhave, nadīmukhe khippaṁ[55] uḍḍeyya[56] bahūnaṁ macchānaṁ ahitāya dukkhāya anayāya byasanāya; evamevaṁ kho, bhikkhave, makkhali moghapuriso manussakhippaṁ maññe loke uppanno bahūnaṁ sattānaṁ ahitāya dukkhāya anayāya byasanāyā”ti.

  1. khippaṁ: khipaṁ (sī, syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. uḍḍeyya: oḍḍeyya (sī), ujjheyya (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
