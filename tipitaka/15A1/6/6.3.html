Accharāsaṅghātavagga

53. 112 “Accharāsaṅghātamattampi ce, bhikkhave, bhikkhu mettācittaṁ[19] āsevati; ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave— ‘bhikkhu arittajjhāno viharati satthusāsanakaro ovādapatikaro, amoghaṁ raṭṭhapiṇḍaṁ bhuñjati’. Ko pana vādo ye naṁ bahulīkarontī”ti.
