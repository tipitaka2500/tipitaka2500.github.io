6.3.8 Appamattakavissajjakasammuti

6.3.8. อัป์ปมัต์ตกวิส์สัช์ชกสัม์มุติ

Senāsanakkhandhaka

Tatiyabhāṇavāra

Appamattakavissajjakasammuti

328. 1440 Tena kho pana samayena saṁghassa bhaṇḍāgāre appamattako parikkhāro uppanno[300] hoti. Bhagavato etamatthaṁ ārocesuṁ. “Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, pañcahaṅgehi samannāgataṁ bhikkhuṁ appamattakavissajjakaṁ sammannituṁ— yo na chandāgatiṁ gaccheyya, na dosāgatiṁ gaccheyya, na mohāgatiṁ gaccheyya, na bhayāgatiṁ gaccheyya, vissajjitāvissajjitañca jāneyya. Evañca pana, bhikkhave, sammannitabbo. Paṭhamaṁ bhikkhu yācitabbo, yācitvā byattena bhikkhunā paṭibalena saṁgho ñāpetabbo—

1441 ‘Suṇātu me, bhante, saṁgho. Yadi saṁghassa pattakallaṁ, saṁgho itthannāmaṁ bhikkhuṁ appamattakavissajjakaṁ sammanneyya. Esā ñatti.

1442 Suṇātu me, bhante, saṁgho. Saṁgho itthannāmaṁ bhikkhuṁ appamattakavissajjakaṁ sammannati. Yassāyasmato khamati itthannāmassa bhikkhuno appamattakavissajjakassa sammuti, so tuṇhassa; yassa nakkhamati, so bhāseyya.

1443 Sammato saṁghena itthannāmo bhikkhu appamattakavissajjako. Khamati saṁghassa, tasmā tuṇhī, evametaṁ dhārayāmī’”ti.

1444 Tena appamattakavissajjakena bhikkhunā ekā[301] sūci dātabbā, satthakaṁ dātabbaṁ, upāhanā dātabbā, kāyabandhanaṁ dātabbaṁ, aṁsabaddhako dātabbo, parissāvanaṁ dātabbaṁ, dhammakaraṇo dātabbo, kusi dātabbā, aḍḍhakusi dātabbā, maṇḍalaṁ dātabbaṁ, aḍḍhamaṇḍalaṁ dātabbaṁ, anuvāto dātabbo, paribhaṇḍaṁ dātabbaṁ. Sace hoti saṁghassa sappi vā telaṁ vā madhu vā phāṇitaṁ vā, sakiṁ paṭisāyituṁ dātabbaṁ. Sace punapi attho hoti, punapi dātabbaṁ.

  1. uppanno: ussanno (syā1-3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. ekā: ekekā (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
