6.3.6 Bhattuddesakasammuti

6.3.6. ภัต์ตุท์เทสกสัม์มุติ

Senāsanakkhandhaka

Tatiyabhāṇavāra

Bhattuddesakasammuti

326. 1431 Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū attano varabhattāni gahetvā lāmakāni bhattāni bhikkhūnaṁ denti. Bhagavato etamatthaṁ ārocesuṁ. “Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, pañcahaṅgehi samannāgataṁ bhikkhuṁ bhattuddesakaṁ sammannituṁ— yo na chandāgatiṁ gaccheyya, na dosāgatiṁ gaccheyya, na mohāgatiṁ gaccheyya, na bhayāgatiṁ gaccheyya, uddiṭṭhānuddiṭṭhañca jāneyya. Evañca pana, bhikkhave, sammannitabbo. Paṭhamaṁ bhikkhu yācitabbo, yācitvā byattena bhikkhunā paṭibalena saṁgho ñāpetabbo—

1432 ‘Suṇātu me, bhante, saṁgho. Yadi saṁghassa pattakallaṁ, saṁgho itthannāmaṁ bhikkhuṁ bhattuddesakaṁ sammanneyya. Esā ñatti.

1433 Suṇātu me, bhante, saṁgho. Saṁgho itthannāmaṁ bhikkhuṁ bhattuddesakaṁ sammannati. Yassāyasmato khamati itthannāmassa bhikkhuno bhattuddesakassa sammuti, so tuṇhassa; yassa nakkhamati, so bhāseyya.

1434 Sammato saṁghena itthannāmo bhikkhu bhattuddesako. Khamati saṁghassa, tasmā tuṇhī, evametaṁ dhārayāmī’”ti.

1435 Atha kho bhattuddesakānaṁ bhikkhūnaṁ etadahosi— “kathaṁ nu kho bhattaṁ uddisitabban”ti? Bhagavato etamatthaṁ ārocesuṁ. “Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, salākāya vā[299] paṭṭikāya vā upanibandhitvā opuñjitvā bhattaṁ uddisitun”ti.
