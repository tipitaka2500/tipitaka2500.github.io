4.1.2. สุก์กปัก์ขนวก

Samathakkhandhaka

Sammukhāvinaya

Sukkapakkhanavaka

188. 797 Dhammavādī puggalo adhammavādiṁ puggalaṁ saññāpeti nijjhāpeti pekkheti anupekkheti dasseti anudasseti— ‘ayaṁ dhammo, ayaṁ vinayo, idaṁ satthusāsanaṁ, imaṁ gaṇhāhi, imaṁ rocehī’ti. Evañcetaṁ adhikaraṇaṁ vūpasammati, dhammena vūpasammati sammukhāvinayena. (1)

798 Dhammavādī puggalo adhammavādī sambahule saññāpeti nijjhāpeti pekkheti anupekkheti dasseti anudasseti— ‘ayaṁ dhammo, ayaṁ vinayo, idaṁ satthusāsanaṁ, imaṁ gaṇhatha, imaṁ rocethā’ti. Evañcetaṁ adhikaraṇaṁ vūpasammati, dhammena vūpasammati sammukhāvinayena. (2)

799 Dhammavādī puggalo adhammavādiṁ saṁghaṁ saññāpeti nijjhāpeti pekkheti anupekkheti dasseti anudasseti— ‘ayaṁ dhammo, ayaṁ vinayo, idaṁ satthusāsanaṁ, imaṁ gaṇhāhi, imaṁ rocehī’ti. Evañcetaṁ adhikaraṇaṁ vūpasammati, dhammena vūpasammati sammukhāvinayena. (3)

800 Dhammavādī sambahulā adhammavādiṁ puggalaṁ saññāpenti nijjhāpenti pekkhenti anupekkhenti dassenti anudassenti— ‘ayaṁ dhammo, ayaṁ vinayo, idaṁ satthusāsanaṁ, imaṁ gaṇhāhi, imaṁ rocehī’ti. Evañcetaṁ adhikaraṇaṁ vūpasammati, dhammena vūpasammati sammukhāvinayena. (4)

801 Dhammavādī sambahulā adhammavādī sambahule saññāpenti nijjhāpenti pekkhenti anupekkhenti dassenti anudassenti— ‘ayaṁ dhammo, ayaṁ vinayo, idaṁ satthusāsanaṁ, imaṁ gaṇhatha, imaṁ rocethā’ti. Evañcetaṁ adhikaraṇaṁ vūpasammati, dhammena vūpasammati sammukhāvinayena. (5)

802 Dhammavādī sambahulā adhammavādiṁ saṁghaṁ saññāpenti nijjhāpenti pekkhenti anupekkhenti dassenti anudassenti— ‘ayaṁ dhammo, ayaṁ vinayo, idaṁ satthusāsanaṁ, imaṁ gaṇhāhi, imaṁ rocehī’ti. Evañcetaṁ adhikaraṇaṁ vūpasammati, dhammena vūpasammati sammukhāvinayena. (6)

803 Dhammavādī saṁgho adhammavādiṁ puggalaṁ saññāpeti nijjhāpeti pekkheti anupekkheti dasseti anudasseti— ‘ayaṁ dhammo, ayaṁ vinayo, idaṁ satthusāsanaṁ, imaṁ gaṇhāhi, imaṁ rocehī’ti. Evañcetaṁ adhikaraṇaṁ vūpasammati, dhammena vūpasammati sammukhāvinayena. (7)

804 Dhammavādī saṁgho adhammavādī sambahule saññāpeti nijjhāpeti pekkheti anupekkheti dasseti anudasseti— ‘ayaṁ dhammo, ayaṁ vinayo, idaṁ satthusāsanaṁ, imaṁ gaṇhatha, imaṁ rocethā’ti. Evañcetaṁ adhikaraṇaṁ vūpasammati, dhammena vūpasammati sammukhāvinayena. (8)

805 Dhammavādī saṁgho adhammavādiṁ saṁghaṁ saññāpeti nijjhāpeti pekkheti anupekkheti dasseti anudasseti— ‘ayaṁ dhammo, ayaṁ vinayo, idaṁ satthusāsanaṁ, imaṁ gaṇhāhi, imaṁ rocehī’ti. Evañcetaṁ adhikaraṇaṁ vūpasammati, dhammena vūpasammati sammukhāvinayenā”ti. (9)

806 Sukkapakkhanavakaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
