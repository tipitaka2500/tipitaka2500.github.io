1.5.1. อธัม์มกัม์มท๎วาทสก

Kammakkhandhaka

Āpattiyāadassaneukkhepanīyakamma

Adhammakammadvādasaka

48. 220 Tīhi, bhikkhave, aṅgehi samannāgataṁ, āpattiyā adassane, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ adhammakammañca hoti, avinayakammañca, duvūpasantañca. Asammukhā kataṁ hoti, appaṭipucchā kataṁ hoti, appaṭiññāya kataṁ hoti— imehi kho, bhikkhave, tīhaṅgehi samannāgataṁ, āpattiyā adassane, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ adhammakammañca hoti, avinayakammañca, duvūpasantañca.

221 Aparehipi, bhikkhave, tīhaṅgehi samannāgataṁ, āpattiyā adassane, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ adhammakammañca hoti, avinayakammañca, duvūpasantañca. Anāpattiyā kataṁ hoti, adesanāgāminiyā āpattiyā kataṁ hoti, desitāya āpattiyā kataṁ hoti…pe… acodetvā kataṁ hoti, asāretvā kataṁ hoti, āpattiṁ anāropetvā kataṁ hoti…pe… asammukhā kataṁ hoti, adhammena kataṁ hoti, vaggena kataṁ hoti…pe… appaṭipucchā kataṁ hoti, adhammena kataṁ hoti, vaggena kataṁ hoti…pe… appaṭiññāya kataṁ hoti, adhammena kataṁ hoti, vaggena kataṁ hoti…pe… anāpattiyā kataṁ hoti, adhammena kataṁ hoti, vaggena kataṁ hoti…pe… adesanāgāminiyā āpattiyā kataṁ hoti, adhammena kataṁ hoti, vaggena kataṁ hoti…pe… desitāya āpattiyā kataṁ hoti, adhammena kataṁ hoti, vaggena kataṁ hoti…pe… acodetvā kataṁ hoti, adhammena kataṁ hoti, vaggena kataṁ hoti…pe… asāretvā kataṁ hoti, adhammena kataṁ hoti, vaggena kataṁ hoti…pe… āpattiṁ anāropetvā kataṁ hoti, adhammena kataṁ hoti, vaggena kataṁ hoti— imehi kho, bhikkhave, tīhaṅgehi samannāgataṁ, āpattiyā adassane, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ adhammakammañca hoti, avinayakammañca, duvūpasantañca.

222 Āpattiyā adassane ukkhepanīyakamme
 adhammakammadvādasakaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
