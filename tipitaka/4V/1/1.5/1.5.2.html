Āpattiyāadassaneukkhepanīyakamma

Dhammakammadvādasaka

49. 223 Tīhi, bhikkhave, aṅgehi samannāgataṁ, āpattiyā adassane, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ dhammakammañca hoti, vinayakammañca, suvūpasantañca. Sammukhā kataṁ hoti, paṭipucchā kataṁ hoti, paṭiññāya kataṁ hoti— imehi kho, bhikkhave, tīhaṅgehi samannāgataṁ, āpattiyā adassane, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ dhammakammañca hoti, vinayakammañca, suvūpasantañca.
