9.9 Codakenaupaṭṭhāpetabbadhamma

9.9. โจทเกนอุปัฏ์ฐาเปตัพ์พธัม์ม

Pātimokkhaṭṭhapanakkhandhaka

Codakenaupaṭṭhāpetabbadhamma

400. 1935 [430] “Codakena, bhante, bhikkhunā paraṁ codetukāmena kati dhamme ajjhattaṁ upaṭṭhāpetvā paro codetabbo”ti? “Codakenupāli, bhikkhunā paraṁ codetukāmena pañca dhamme ajjhattaṁ upaṭṭhāpetvā paro codetabbo— kālena vakkhāmi, no akālena; bhūtena vakkhāmi, no abhūtena; saṇhena vakkhāmi, no pharusena; atthasaṁhitena vakkhāmi, no anatthasaṁhitena; mettacitto vakkhāmi, no dosantaroti. Codakenupāli, bhikkhunā paraṁ codetukāmena ime pañca dhamme ajjhattaṁ upaṭṭhāpetvā paro codetabbo”ti.
