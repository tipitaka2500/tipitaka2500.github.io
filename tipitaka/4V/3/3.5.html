166. 697 Idha pana, bhikkhave, bhikkhu sambahulā saṁghādisesā āpattiyo āpajjitvā appaṭicchādetvā vibbhamati. So puna[108] upasampanno tā āpattiyo nacchādeti. Tassa, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno mānattaṁ dātabbaṁ. (1)
