141. 1016 » Na cakkhu na indriyanti?

1017 Cakkhuṁ ṭhapetvā avasesā indriyā na cakkhu, indriyā. Cakkhuñca indriye ca ṭhapetvā avasesā na ceva cakkhu na ca indriyā.
