242. 2144 » Yattha cakkhundriyaṁ uppajjittha tattha sotindriyaṁ uppajjitthāti? Āmantā.

2145 « Yattha vā pana sotindriyaṁ uppajjittha tattha cakkhundriyaṁ uppajjitthāti? Āmantā.

2146 » Yattha cakkhundriyaṁ uppajjittha tattha ghānindriyaṁ uppajjitthāti?

2147 Rūpāvacare tattha cakkhundriyaṁ uppajjittha, no ca tattha ghānindriyaṁ uppajjittha. Kāmāvacare tattha cakkhundriyañca uppajjittha ghānindriyañca uppajjittha.
