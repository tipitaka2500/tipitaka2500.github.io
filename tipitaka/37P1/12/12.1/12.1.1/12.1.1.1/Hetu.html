Paccayānuloma

Vibhaṅgavāra

Hetu

1. 1655 Sekkhaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sekkho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— sekkhaṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ paṭicca tayo khandhā…pe… dve khandhe…pe… . (1)

1656 Sekkhaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nevasekkhanāsekkho[5] dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— sekkhe khandhe paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ. (2)

1657 Sekkhaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sekkho ca nevasekkhanāsekkho ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā— sekkhaṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ paṭicca tayo khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañca rūpaṁ…pe… dve khandhe…pe… . (3)

2. 1658 Asekkhaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca asekkho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— asekkhaṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ paṭicca tayo khandhā…pe… . (1)

1659 Asekkhaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nevasekkhanāsekkho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— asekkhe khandhe paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ. (2)

1660 Asekkhaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca asekkho ca nevasekkhanāsekkho ca dhammā…pe… asekkhaṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ paṭicca tayo khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañca rūpaṁ…pe… dve khandhe…pe… . (3)

3. 1661 Nevasekkhanāsekkhaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nevasekkhanāsekkho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— nevasekkhanāsekkhaṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ paṭicca tayo khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañca rūpaṁ…pe… dve khandhe…pe… paṭisandhikkhaṇe…pe… khandhe paṭicca vatthu, vatthuṁ paṭicca khandhā, ekaṁ mahābhūtaṁ paṭicca tayo mahābhūtā…pe… dve mahābhūte paṭicca dve mahābhūtā, mahābhūte paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ kaṭattārūpaṁ upādārūpaṁ. (1)

1662 Sekkhañca nevasekkhanāsekkhañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nevasekkhanāsekkho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— sekkhe khandhe ca mahābhūte ca paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ. (1)

1663 Asekkhañca nevasekkhanāsekkhañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nevasekkhanāsekkho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— asekkhe khandhe ca mahābhūte ca paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ. (1)

  1. nevasekkhanāsekkho: nevasekhānāsekho (sī), nevasekkhānāsekkho (syā2, syā3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
