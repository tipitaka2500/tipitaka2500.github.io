43. 2841 Ajjhatto dhammo ajjhattassa dhammassa āhārapaccayena paccayo— ajjhattā āhārā sampayuttakānaṁ khandhānaṁ cittasamuṭṭhānānañca rūpānaṁ āhārapaccayena paccayo. Paṭisandhikkhaṇe…pe… ajjhatto kabaḷīkāro āhāro ajjhattassa kāyassa āhārapaccayena paccayo. (1)

2842 Ajjhatto dhammo bahiddhā dhammassa āhārapaccayena paccayo— ajjhatto kabaḷīkāro āhāro bahiddhā kāyassa āhārapaccayena paccayo. (2)

2843 Bahiddhā dhammo bahiddhā dhammassa āhārapaccayena paccayo (pavattipaṭisandhi) bahiddhā kabaḷīkāro āhāro bahiddhā kāyassa āhārapaccayena paccayo. (1)

2844 Bahiddhā dhammo ajjhattassa dhammassa āhārapaccayena paccayo— bahiddhā kabaḷīkāro āhāro ajjhattassa kāyassa āhārapaccayena paccayo. (2)

44. 2845 Ajjhatto dhammo ca bahiddhā dhammo ca ajjhattassa dhammassa āhārapaccayena paccayo— ajjhatto kabaḷīkāro āhāro ca bahiddhā kabaḷīkāro āhāro ca ajjhattassa kāyassa āhārapaccayena paccayo. (1)
