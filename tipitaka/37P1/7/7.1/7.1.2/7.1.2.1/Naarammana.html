29. 135 Savitakkasavicāraṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca avitakkaavicāro dhammo uppajjati naārammaṇapaccayā— savitakkasavicāre khandhe paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ. Paṭisandhikkhaṇe savitakkasavicāre khandhe paṭicca kaṭattārūpaṁ. (1)
