77. 2101 Paritto dhammo parittassa dhammassa āsevanapaccayena paccayo— purimā purimā parittā khandhā pacchimānaṁ pacchimānaṁ parittānaṁ khandhānaṁ…pe… anulomaṁ gotrabhussa, anulomaṁ vodānassa āsevanapaccayena paccayo. (1)

2102 Paritto dhammo mahaggatassa dhammassa āsevanapaccayena paccayo— paṭhamassa jhānassa parikammaṁ tasseva[6] āsevanapaccayena paccayo…pe… nevasaññānāsaññāyatanassa parikammaṁ tasseva[7] āsevanapaccayena paccayo. Dibbassa cakkhussa parikammaṁ…pe… anāgataṁsañāṇassa parikammaṁ anāgataṁsañāṇassa āsevanapaccayena paccayo. (2)

2103 Paritto dhammo appamāṇassa dhammassa āsevanapaccayena paccayo— gotrabhu maggassa… vodānaṁ maggassa āsevanapaccayena paccayo. (3)

2104 Mahaggato dhammo mahaggatassa dhammassa āsevanapaccayena paccayo— purimā purimā mahaggatā khandhā pacchimānaṁ pacchimānaṁ mahaggatānaṁ khandhānaṁ…pe… āsevanapaccayena paccayo. (1)
