2.2.7 Macchariyasutta

2.2.7. มัจ์ฉริยสุต์ต

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Satipaṭṭhānavagga

Macchariyasutta

69. 454 “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, macchariyāni. Katamāni pañca? Āvāsamacchariyaṁ, kulamacchariyaṁ, lābhamacchariyaṁ, vaṇṇamacchariyaṁ, dhammamacchariyaṁ— imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañca macchariyāni.

455 Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, pañcannaṁ macchariyānaṁ pahānāya…pe… ime cattāro satipaṭṭhānā bhāvetabbā”ti.

456 Sattamaṁ.
