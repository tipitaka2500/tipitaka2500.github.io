Gatisutta

68. 451 “Pañcimā, bhikkhave, gatiyo. Katamā pañca? Nirayo, tiracchānayoni, pettivisayo, manussā, devā— imā kho, bhikkhave, pañca gatiyo.

452 Imāsaṁ kho, bhikkhave, pañcannaṁ gatīnaṁ pahānāya…pe… ime cattāro satipaṭṭhānā bhāvetabbā”ti.

453 Chaṭṭhaṁ.
