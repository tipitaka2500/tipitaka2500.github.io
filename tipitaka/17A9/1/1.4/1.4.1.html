1.4.1 Anupubbavihārasutta

1.4.1. อนุปุพ์พวิหารสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Mahāvagga

Anupubbavihārasutta

32. 246 [104] “Navayime, bhikkhave, anupubbavihārā. Katame nava[105]? Paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ, dutiyaṁ jhānaṁ, tatiyaṁ jhānaṁ, catutthaṁ jhānaṁ, ākāsānañcāyatanaṁ, viññāṇañcāyatanaṁ, ākiñcaññāyatanaṁ, nevasaññānāsaññāyatanaṁ, saññāvedayitanirodho— ime kho, bhikkhave, nava anupubbavihārā”ti.
