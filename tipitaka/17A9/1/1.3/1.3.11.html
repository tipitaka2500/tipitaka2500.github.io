1.3.11 Anupubbanirodhasutta

1.3.11. อนุปุพ์พนิโรธสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Sattāvāsavagga

Anupubbanirodhasutta

31. 241 [102] “Navayime, bhikkhave, anupubbanirodhā. Katame nava? Paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ samāpannassa kāmasaññā[103] niruddhā hoti; dutiyaṁ jhānaṁ samāpannassa vitakkavicārā niruddhā honti; tatiyaṁ jhānaṁ samāpannassa pīti niruddhā hoti; catutthaṁ jhānaṁ samāpannassa assāsapassāsā niruddhā honti; ākāsānañcāyatanaṁ samāpannassa rūpasaññā niruddhā hoti; viññāṇañcāyatanaṁ samāpannassa ākāsānañcāyatanasaññā niruddhā hoti; ākiñcaññāyatanaṁ samāpannassa viññāṇañcāyatanasaññā niruddhā hoti; nevasaññānāsaññāyatanaṁ samāpannassa ākiñcaññāyatanasaññā niruddhā hoti; saññāvedayitanirodhaṁ samāpannassa saññā ca vedanā ca niruddhā honti. Ime kho, bhikkhave, nava anupubbanirodhā”ti.
