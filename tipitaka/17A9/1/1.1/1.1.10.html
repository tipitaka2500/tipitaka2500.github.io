1.1.10 Āhuneyyasutta

1.1.10. อาหุเนย์ยสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Sambodhivagga

Āhuneyyasutta

10. 82 [23] “Navayime, bhikkhave, puggalā āhuneyyā pāhuneyyā dakkhiṇeyyā añjalikaraṇīyā anuttaraṁ puññakkhettaṁ lokassa. Katame nava? Arahā, arahattāya paṭipanno, anāgāmī, anāgāmiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipanno, sakadāgāmī, sakadāgāmiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipanno, sotāpanno, sotāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya paṭipanno, gotrabhū— ime kho, bhikkhave, nava puggalā āhuneyyā…pe… anuttaraṁ puññakkhettaṁ lokassā”ti.

83 Dasamaṁ.

84 Sambodhivaggo paṭhamo.

85

86 Sambodhi nissayo ceva,
Meghiya nandakaṁ balaṁ;
Sevanā sutavā sajjho,
Puggalo āhuneyyena cāti.
