5.1.6. สัญ์ญาสุต์ต

Uppādasaṃyutta

Uppādavagga

Saññāsutta

317. 1079 [293] Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Yo kho, bhikkhave, rūpasaññāya uppādo ṭhiti…pe… jarāmaraṇassa pātubhāvo…pe… yo dhammasaññāya uppādo ṭhiti abhinibbatti pātubhāvo, dukkhasseso uppādo, rogānaṁ ṭhiti, jarāmaraṇassa pātubhāvo. Yo ca kho, bhikkhave, rūpasaññāya nirodho…pe… jarāmaraṇassa atthaṅgamo…pe… yo dhammasaññāya nirodho vūpasamo atthaṅgamo, dukkhasseso nirodho, rogānaṁ vūpasamo, jarāmaraṇassa atthaṅgamo”ti.

1080 Chaṭṭhaṁ.
