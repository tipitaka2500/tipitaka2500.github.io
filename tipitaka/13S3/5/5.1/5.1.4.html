5.1.4. สัม์ผัส์สสุต์ต

Uppādasaṃyutta

Uppādavagga

Samphassasutta

315. 1074 [291] Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Yo kho, bhikkhave, cakkhusamphassassa uppādo ṭhiti…pe… jarāmaraṇassa pātubhāvo…pe… yo manosamphassassa uppādo ṭhiti…pe… jarāmaraṇassa pātubhāvo. Yo ca kho, bhikkhave, cakkhusamphassassa nirodho…pe… jarāmaraṇassa atthaṅgamo…pe… yo manosamphassassa nirodho…pe… jarāmaraṇassa atthaṅgamo”ti.
