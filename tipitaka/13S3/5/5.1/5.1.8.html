5.1.8. ตัณ๎หาสุต์ต

Uppādasaṃyutta

Uppādavagga

Taṇhāsutta

319. 1083 Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Yo kho, bhikkhave, rūpataṇhāya uppādo ṭhiti…pe… jarāmaraṇassa pātubhāvo…pe… yo dhammataṇhāya uppādo ṭhiti abhinibbatti pātubhāvo, dukkhasseso uppādo, rogānaṁ ṭhiti, jarāmaraṇassa pātubhāvo. Yo ca kho, bhikkhave, rūpataṇhāya nirodho…pe… jarāmaraṇassa atthaṅgamo…pe… yo dhammataṇhāya nirodho vūpasamo atthaṅgamo, dukkhasseso nirodho, rogānaṁ vūpasamo, jarāmaraṇassa atthaṅgamo”ti.

1084 Aṭṭhamaṁ.
