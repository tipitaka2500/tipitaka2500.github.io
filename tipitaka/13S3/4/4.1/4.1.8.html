4.1.8. รูปตัณ๎หาสุต์ต

Okkantasaṃyutta

Cakkhuvagga

Rūpataṇhāsutta

309. 1057 [288] Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Rūpataṇhā, bhikkhave, aniccā vipariṇāmī aññathābhāvī; saddataṇhā… gandhataṇhā… rasataṇhā… phoṭṭhabbataṇhā… dhammataṇhā aniccā vipariṇāmī aññathābhāvī. Yo, bhikkhave, ime dhamme evaṁ saddahati adhimuccati, ayaṁ vuccati ‘saddhānusārī…pe… sambodhiparāyano’”ti.

1058 Aṭṭhamaṁ.
