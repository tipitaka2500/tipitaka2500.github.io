4.1.7. รูปสัญ์เจตนาสุต์ต

Okkantasaṃyutta

Cakkhuvagga

Rūpasañcetanāsutta

308. 1055 [287] Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Rūpasañcetanā, bhikkhave, aniccā vipariṇāmī aññathābhāvī; saddasañcetanā… gandhasañcetanā… rasasañcetanā… phoṭṭhabbasañcetanā… dhammasañcetanā aniccā vipariṇāmī aññathābhāvī. Yo, bhikkhave, ime dhamme evaṁ saddahati adhimuccati, ayaṁ vuccati ‘saddhānusārī…pe… sambodhiparāyano’”ti.

1056 Sattamaṁ.
