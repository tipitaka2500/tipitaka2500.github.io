4.1.6. รูปสัญ์ญาสุต์ต

Okkantasaṃyutta

Cakkhuvagga

Rūpasaññāsutta

307. 1053 [286] Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Rūpasaññā, bhikkhave, aniccā vipariṇāmī aññathābhāvī; saddasaññā… gandhasaññā… rasasaññā… phoṭṭhabbasaññā… dhammasaññā aniccā vipariṇāmī aññathābhāvī. Yo, bhikkhave, ime dhamme evaṁ saddahati adhimuccati, ayaṁ vuccati ‘saddhānusārī…pe… sambodhiparāyano’”ti.

1054 Chaṭṭhaṁ.
