6.1.10. ขัน์ธสุต์ต

Kilesasaṃyutta

Kilesavagga

Khandhasutta

331. 1111 Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Yo, bhikkhave, rūpasmiṁ chandarāgo, cittasseso upakkileso…pe… yo viññāṇasmiṁ chandarāgo, cittasseso upakkileso. Yato kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno imesu pañcasu ṭhānesu cetaso upakkileso pahīno hoti, nekkhammaninnañcassa cittaṁ hoti. Nekkhammaparibhāvitaṁ cittaṁ kammaniyaṁ khāyati, abhiññā sacchikaraṇīyesu dhammesū”ti.
