6.1.4 Samphassasutta

6.1.4. สัม์ผัส์สสุต์ต

Kilesasaṃyutta

Kilesavagga

Samphassasutta

325. 1099 [295] Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Yo, bhikkhave, cakkhusamphassasmiṁ chandarāgo, cittasseso upakkileso. Yo sotasamphassasmiṁ… yo ghānasamphassasmiṁ… yo jivhāsamphassasmiṁ… yo kāyasamphassasmiṁ… yo manosamphassasmiṁ chandarāgo, cittasseso upakkileso. Yato kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno…pe… abhiññā sacchikaraṇīyesu dhammesū”ti.

1100 Catutthaṁ.
