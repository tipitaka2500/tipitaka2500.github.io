11.1.1 Suddhikasutta

11.1.1. สุท์ธิกสุต์ต

Valāhakasaṃyutta

Valāhakavagga

Suddhikasutta

550. 1216 [322] [321] Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Valāhakakāyike vo, bhikkhave, deve desessāmi. Taṁ suṇātha. Katame ca, bhikkhave, valāhakakāyikā devā? Santi, bhikkhave, sītavalāhakā devā; santi uṇhavalāhakā devā; santi abbhavalāhakā devā; santi vātavalāhakā devā; santi vassavalāhakā devā— ime vuccanti, bhikkhave, ‘valāhakakāyikā devā’”ti.

1217 Paṭhamaṁ.
