Sotāpattivagga

Nahotitathāgatosutta

221. 967 Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Kismiṁ nu kho, bhikkhave, sati, kiṁ upādāya, kiṁ abhinivissa evaṁ diṭṭhi uppajjati— ‘na hoti tathāgato paraṁ maraṇā’”ti? “Bhagavaṁmūlakā no, bhante, dhammā…pe… niyato sambodhiparāyano”ti.

968 Soḷasamaṁ.
