3.1.15. โหติตถาคโตสุต์ต

Diṭṭhisaṃyutta

Sotāpattivagga

Hotitathāgatosutta

220. 965 [267] Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Kismiṁ nu kho, bhikkhave, sati, kiṁ upādāya, kiṁ abhinivissa evaṁ diṭṭhi uppajjati— ‘hoti tathāgato paraṁ maraṇā’”ti? “Bhagavaṁmūlakā no, bhante, dhammā…pe… niyato sambodhiparāyano”ti.

966 Pannarasamaṁ.
