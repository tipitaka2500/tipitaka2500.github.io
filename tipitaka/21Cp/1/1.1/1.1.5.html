1.1.5 Mahāgovindacariya

1.1.5. มหาโควิน์ทจริย

Akittivagga

Dānapāramī

Mahāgovindacariya

37. 42 [10] “Punāparaṁ yadā homi,
sattarājapurohito;
Pūjito naradevehi,
mahāgovindabrāhmaṇo.

38. 43 Tadāhaṁ sattarajjesu,
Yaṁ me āsi upāyanaṁ;
Tena demi mahādānaṁ,
Akkhobbhaṁ sāgarūpamaṁ. [11]

39. 44 Na me dessaṁ dhanaṁ dhaññaṁ,
Napi natthi nicayo mayi;
Sabbaññutaṁ piyaṁ mayhaṁ,
Tasmā demi varaṁ dhanan”ti.

45 Mahāgovindacariyaṁ pañcamaṁ.
