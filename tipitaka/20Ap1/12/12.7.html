12.7 Buddhasaññakattheraapadāna

12.7. พุท์ธสั์กัต์เถรอปทาน

Mahāparivāravagga

Buddhasaññakattheraapadāna

57. 2009 “Yadā vipassī lokaggo,
āyusaṅkhāramossaji;
Pathavī sampakampittha,
medanī jalamekhalā.

58. 2010 Otataṁ vitthataṁ[536] mayhaṁ,
suvicittavaṭaṁsakaṁ;
Bhavanampi pakampittha,
buddhassa āyusaṅkhaye. [537]

59. 2011 Tāso mayhaṁ samuppanno,
bhavane sampakampite;
Uppādo nu kimatthāya,
āloko vipulo ahu. [538]

60. 2012 Vessavaṇo idhāgamma,
nibbāpesi mahājanaṁ;
Pāṇabhūte bhayaṁ natthi,
ekaggā hotha saṁvutā[540]. [539]

61. 2013 Aho buddhā aho dhammā,
aho no satthu sampadā;
Yasmiṁ uppajjamānamhi,
pathavī sampakampati. [541]

62. 2014 Buddhānubhāvaṁ kittetvā,
kappaṁ saggamhi modahaṁ;
Avasesesu kappesu,
kusalaṁ caritaṁ[542] mayā.

63. 2015 Ekanavutito kappe,
yaṁ saññamalabhiṁ tadā;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhasaññāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

64. 2016 Ito cuddasakappamhi,
rājā āsiṁ patāpavā;
Samito nāma nāmena,
cakkavattī mahabbalo.

65. 2017 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

2018 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā buddhasaññako thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2019 Buddhasaññakattherassāpadānaṁ sattamaṁ.

  1. vitthataṁ: ogataṁ vitataṁ (syā2, syā3), otataṁ vitataṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. suvicittavaṭaṁsakaṁ: suci cittaṁ papañcakaṁ (ka-sī, syā2, syā3), suvicittaṁ vaṭaṁsakaṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Uppādo: uppāto (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. Pāṇabhūte: pāṇabhūnaṁ (syā2, syā3, ka-ma), pāṇabhūtaṁ (ka-sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. saṁvutā: sagāravā (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  6. pathavī: paṭhavī (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  7. caritaṁ: karitaṁ (ka-sī, syā2, syā3), kāritaṁ (ka-sī, pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
