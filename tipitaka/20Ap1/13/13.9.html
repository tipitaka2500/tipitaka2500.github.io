13.9 Gaṇṭhipupphiyattheraapadāna

13.9. คัณ์ิปุป์ผิยัต์เถรอปทาน

Sereyyavagga

Gaṇṭhipupphiyattheraapadāna

91. 2160 [571] “Suvaṇṇavaṇṇo sambuddho,
vipassī dakkhiṇāraho;
Purakkhato sāvakehi,
ārāmā abhinikkhami.

92. 2161 Disvānahaṁ buddhaseṭṭhaṁ,
sabbaññuṁ tamanāsakaṁ;
Pasannacitto sumano,
gaṇṭhipupphaṁ apūjayiṁ. [572]

93. 2162 Tena cittappasādena,
dvipadindassa tādino;
Haṭṭho haṭṭhena cittena,
puna vandiṁ tathāgataṁ.

94. 2163 Ekanavutito kappe,
yaṁ pupphamabhiropayiṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

95. 2164 Ekatālīsito kappe,
caraṇo nāma khattiyo;
Sattaratanasampanno,
cakkavattī mahabbalo.

96. 2165 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

2166 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā gaṇṭhipupphiyo[573] thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2167 Gaṇṭhipupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ navamaṁ.

  2. gaṇṭhipupphaṁ: gandhapupphaṁ (sī), gatamaggaṁ (syā2, syā3, ka-ma), ka-sī, cha1-2 (Word Variant) ↩
  3. gaṇṭhipupphiyo: gandhapupphiyo (sī, syā2, syā3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
