19.4 Pañcahatthiyattheraapadāna

19.4. ปั์จหัต์ถิยัต์เถรอปทาน

Kuṭajapupphiyavagga

Pañcahatthiyattheraapadāna

18. 2604 “Tisso nāmāsi bhagavā,
lokajeṭṭho narāsabho;
Purakkhato sāvakehi,
rathiyaṁ paṭipajjatha.

19. 2605 Pañca uppalahatthā ca,
cāturā ṭhapitā mayā;
Āhutiṁ dātukāmohaṁ,
paggaṇhiṁ vatasiddhiyā[663].

20. 2606 Suvaṇṇavaṇṇaṁ sambuddhaṁ,
Gacchantaṁ antarāpaṇe;
Buddharaṁsīhi phuṭṭhosmi[664],
Pūjesiṁ dvipaduttamaṁ.

21. 2607 Dvenavute ito kappe,
yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

22. 2608 Ito terasakappamhi,
pañca susabhasammatā;
Sattaratanasampannā,
cakkavattī mahabbalā.

23. 2609 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

2610 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā pañcahatthiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2611 Pañcahatthiyattherassāpadānaṁ catutthaṁ.

  1. vatasiddhiyā: puttomhi hitasiddhiyā (syā2, syā3), mūgomhi vata siddhiyā (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. phuṭṭhosmi: buddharaṁsyābhiphuṭṭhomhi (sī), buddharaṁsīhi phuṭṭhomhi (syā2, syā3), buddharaṁsābhighuṭṭhomhi (pā1), buddharaṁsābhighuṭṭhosmi (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
