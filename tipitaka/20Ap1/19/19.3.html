19.3 Koṭumbariyattheraapadāna

19.3. โกฏุม์พริยัต์เถรอปทาน

Kuṭajapupphiyavagga

Koṭumbariyattheraapadāna

12. 2596 [660] “Kaṇikāraṁva jotantaṁ,
nisinnaṁ pabbatantare;
Appameyyaṁva udadhiṁ,
vitthataṁ dharaṇiṁ yathā.

13. 2597 Pūjitaṁ devasaṅghena,
nisabhājāniyaṁ yathā;
Haṭṭho haṭṭhena cittena,
upāgacchiṁ naruttamaṁ. [661]

14. 2598 Sattapupphāni paggayha,
koṭumbarasamākulaṁ;
Buddhassa abhiropesiṁ,
sikhino lokabandhuno.

15. 2599 Ekattiṁse ito kappe,
yaṁ pupphamabhiropayiṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

16. 2600 Ito vīsatikappamhi,
mahānelasanāmako;
Eko āsi mahātejo,
cakkavattī mahabbalo.

17. 2601 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

2602 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā koṭumbariyo[662] thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2603 Koṭumbariyattherassāpadānaṁ tatiyaṁ.

  1. kotumbariyattherāpadānaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Pūjitaṁ: puretaṁ (syā2, syā3), paretaṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. koṭumbariyo: kotumbariyo (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
