30.4. สัท์ทสัญ์ญกัต์เถรอปทาน

Citakapūjakavagga

Saddasaññakattheraapadāna

17. 3380 “Anuggatamhi ādicce,
panādo vipulo ahu;
Buddhaseṭṭhassa lokamhi,
pātubhāvo mahesino. [847]

18. 3381 Ghosamassosahaṁ tattha,
na ca passāmi taṁ jinaṁ;
Maraṇañca anuppatto,
buddhasaññamanussariṁ. [848]

19. 3382 Catunnavutito kappe,
yaṁ saññamalabhiṁ tadā;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

20. 3383 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

3384 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā saddasaññako thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

3385 Saddasaññakattherassāpadānaṁ catutthaṁ.

  1. panādo: pasādo (syā2, syā3, pā1) aṭṭha. (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Ghosamassosahaṁ: sadda (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
