26.4 Ticampakapupphiyattheraapadāna

26.4. ติจัม์ปกปุป์ผิยัต์เถรอปทาน

Thomakavagga

Ticampakapupphiyattheraapadāna

13. 3112 [788] “Himavantassāvidūre,
vikato nāma pabbato;
Tassa vemajjhe vasati,
samaṇo bhāvitindriyo. [789]

14. 3113 Disvāna tassopasamaṁ,
vippasannena cetasā;
Tīṇi campakapupphāni,
gahetvāna samokiriṁ.

15. 3114 Ekanavutito kappe,
yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

16. 3115 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

3116 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā ticampakapupphiyo[790] thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

3117 Ticampakapupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ catutthaṁ.

  1. campakapupphiyattherāpadānaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. vikato: vikano (syā2, syā3), vikaṭo (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. ticampakapupphiyo: campakapupphiyo (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
