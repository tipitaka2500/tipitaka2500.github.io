25.2 Nāgakesariyattheraapadāna

25.2. นาคเกสริยัต์เถรอปทาน

Tuvaradāyakavagga

Nāgakesariyattheraapadāna

4. 3039 “Dhanuṁ advejjhaṁ katvāna,
vanamajjhogahiṁ ahaṁ;
Kesaraṁ ogataṁ[769] disvā,
patapattaṁ samuṭṭhitaṁ.

5. 3040 Ubho hatthehi paggayha,
sire katvāna añjaliṁ;
Buddhassa abhiropesiṁ,
tissassa lokabandhuno.

6. 3041 Dvenavute ito kappe,
yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

7. 3042 Tesattatimhi kappamhi[770],
satta kesaranāmakā;
Sattaratanasampannā,
cakkavattī mahabbalā.

8. 3043 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

3044 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā nāgakesariyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

3045 Nāgakesariyattherassāpadānaṁ dutiyaṁ.

  1. ogataṁ: osaṭaṁ (sī), osaraṁ (syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. kappamhi: sattasattatime kappe (syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
