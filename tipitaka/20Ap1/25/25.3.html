25.3 Naḷinakesariyattheraapadāna

25.3. นฬินเกสริยัต์เถรอปทาน

Tuvaradāyakavagga

Naḷinakesariyattheraapadāna

9. 3046 “Jātassarassa vemajjhe,
vasāmi jalakukkuṭo;
Addasāhaṁ devadevaṁ,
gacchantaṁ anilañjase. [771]

10. 3047 Tuṇḍena kesariṁ[772] gayha,
vippasannena cetasā;
Buddhassa abhiropesiṁ,
tissassa lokabandhuno.

11. 3048 Dvenavute ito kappe,
yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

12. 3049 Tesattatimhi kappamhi,
satta kesaranāmakā[773];
Sattaratanasampannā,
cakkavattī mahabbalā.

13. 3050 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

3051 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā naḷinakesariyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

3052 Naḷinakesariyattherassāpadānaṁ tatiyaṁ.

  1. Addasāhaṁ: athaddasaṁ (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. kesariṁ: kesaraṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. kesaranāmakā: satapattasanāmako (sī, pā1), sattapattasanāmako (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
