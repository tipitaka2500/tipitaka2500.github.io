25.8 Sattalipupphapūjakattheraapadāna

25.8. สัต์ตลิปุป์ผปูชกัต์เถรอปทาน

Tuvaradāyakavagga

Sattalipupphapūjakattheraapadāna

27. 3074 [778] “Satta sattalipupphāni,
sīse katvānahaṁ tadā;
Buddhassa abhiropesiṁ,
vessabhumhi naruttame[779].

28. 3075 Ekattiṁse ito kappe,
yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

29. 3076 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

3077 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā sattalipupphapūjako[780] thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

3078 Sattalipupphapūjakattherassāpadānaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.

  1. sattasattalipupphapūjakattherāpadānaṁ (syā2, syā3), pāṭalipūjakattherāpadānaṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. naruttame: vibhattivipallāso (Word Variant) ↩
  3. sattalipupphapūjako: sattasattalipupphapūjako (syā2, syā3), pāṭalipūjako (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
