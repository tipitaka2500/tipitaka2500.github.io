25.4 Viravapupphiyattheraapadāna

25.4. วิรวปุป์ผิยัต์เถรอปทาน

Tuvaradāyakavagga

Viravapupphiyattheraapadāna

14. 3053 [774] “Khīṇāsavasahassehi,
niyyāti lokanāyako;
Viravapupphamādāya,
buddhassa abhiropayiṁ. [775]

15. 3054 Ekanavutito kappe,
yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

16. 3055 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

3056 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā viravapupphiyo[776] thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

3057 Viravapupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ catutthaṁ.

  1. viravipupphiyattherāpadānaṁ (sī, syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Viravapupphamādāya: viravipupphaṁ paggayha (sī, syā2, syā3), viravapupphaṁ paggayha (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. viravapupphiyo: viravipupphiyo (sī, syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
