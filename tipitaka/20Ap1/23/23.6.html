23.6 Aṅkolapupphiyattheraapadāna

23.6. อังโกลปุป์ผิยัต์เถรอปทาน

Ālambaṇadāyakavagga

Aṅkolapupphiyattheraapadāna

29. 2937 [742] “Nārado iti me nāmaṁ,
kassapo iti maṁ vidū;
Addasaṁ samaṇānaggaṁ,
vipassiṁ devasakkataṁ. [743]

30. 2938 Anubyañjanadharaṁ buddhaṁ,
āhutīnaṁ paṭiggahaṁ;
Aṅkolapupphaṁ paggayha,
buddhassa abhiropayiṁ. [744]

31. 2939 Ekanavutito kappe,
yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

32. 2940 Catusattatito kappe,
romaso nāma khattiyo;
Āmukkamālābharaṇo,
sayoggabalavāhano.

33. 2941 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

2942 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā aṅkolapupphiyo[745] thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2943 Aṅkolapupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.

  1. vakulapupphiyattherāpadānaṁ (syā2, syā3), caṅkolapupphiyattherāpadānaṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. "Nārado: varado (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Aṅkolapupphaṁ: vakulapupphaṁ (syā2, syā3), caṅkolapupphaṁ (pā1), bakolapupphaṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. aṅkolapupphiyo: vakulapupphiyo (syā2, syā3), caṅkolapupphiyo (pā1), bakolapupphiyo (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
