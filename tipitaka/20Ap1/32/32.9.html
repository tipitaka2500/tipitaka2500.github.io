32.9 Kandalipupphiyattheraapadāna

32.9. กัน์ทลิปุป์ผิยัต์เถรอปทาน

Ārakkhadāyakavagga

Kandalipupphiyattheraapadāna

47. 3568 [883] “Sindhuyā nadiyā tīre,
ahosiṁ kassako tadā;
Parakammāyane yutto,
parabhattaṁ apassito.

48. 3569 Sindhuṁ anucarantohaṁ,
siddhatthaṁ jinamaddasaṁ;
Samādhinā nisinnaṁva,
satapattaṁva pupphitaṁ.

49. 3570 Satta kandalipupphāni[884],
vaṇṭe chetvānahaṁ tadā;
Matthake abhiropesiṁ,
buddhassādiccabandhuno.

50. 3571 Suvaṇṇavaṇṇaṁ sambuddhaṁ,
Anukūle samāhitaṁ;
Tidhāpabhinnamātaṅgaṁ,
Kuñjaraṁva durāsadaṁ.

51. 3572 Tamahaṁ upagantvāna,
nipakaṁ bhāvitindriyaṁ;
Añjaliṁ paggahetvāna,
avandiṁ satthuno ahaṁ.

52. 3573 Catunnavutito kappe,
yaṁ pupphamabhiropayiṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

53. 3574 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

3575 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā kandalipupphiyo[885] thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

3576 Kandalipupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ navamaṁ.

  1. nālipupphiyattherāpadānaṁ (syā2, syā3), kandalīpupphiyattherāpadānaṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. kandalipupphāni: nālipupphāni (syā2, syā3), sattakadalipupphāni (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. kandalipupphiyo: nālipupphiyo (syā2, syā3), kandalīpupphiyo (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
