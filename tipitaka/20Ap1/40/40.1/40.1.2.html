40.1.2 Dānavatthusampādana

40.1.2. ทานวัต์ถุสัม์ปาทน

Pilindavacchavagga

Pilindavacchattheraapadāna

Dānavatthusampādana

9. 4306 Naḷakāre upāgamma,
chattaṁ kāresi tāvade;
Chattasatasahassāni,
ekato sannipātayiṁ.

10. 4307 Dussasatasahassāni,
ekato sannipātayiṁ;
Pattasatasahassāni,
ekato sannipātayiṁ.

11. 4308 Vāsiyo satthake cāpi,
sūciyo nakhachedane;
Heṭṭhāchatte ṭhapāpesiṁ,
kāretvā tadanucchave.

12. 4309 Vidhūpane tālavaṇṭe,
morahatthe ca cāmare;
Parissāvane teladhāre[1046],
kārayiṁ tadanucchave.

13. 4310 Sūcighare aṁsabaddhe,
athopi kāyabandhane;
Ādhārake ca sukate,
kārayiṁ tadanucchave.

14. 4311 Paribhogabhājane ca,
athopi lohathālake;
Bhesajje pūrayitvāna,
heṭṭhāchatte ṭhapesahaṁ.

15. 4312 Vacaṁ usīraṁ laṭṭhimadhuṁ,
pipphalī maricāni ca;
Harītakiṁ siṅgīveraṁ,
sabbaṁ pūresi bhājane.

16. 4313 Upāhanā pādukāyo,
atho udakapuñchane;
Kattaradaṇḍe sukate,
kārayiṁ tadanucchave.

17. 4314 Osadhañjananāḷī ca[1047],
salākā dhammakuttarā;
Kuñcikā pañcavaṇṇehi,
sibbite kuñcikāghare.

18. 4315 Āyoge dhūmanette ca,
athopi dīpadhārake;
Tumbake ca karaṇḍe ca,
kārayiṁ tadanucchave.

19. 4316 Saṇḍāse pipphale ceva,
athopi malahārake;
Bhesajjathavike ceva,
kārayiṁ tadanucchave.

20. 4317 Āsandiyo pīṭhake ca,
pallaṅke caturomaye;
Tadanucchave kārayitvā,
heṭṭhāchatte ṭhapesahaṁ.

21. 4318 Uṇṇābhisī tūlabhisī,
athopi pīṭhikābhisī[1048];
Bimbohane ca sukate,
kārayiṁ tadanucchave. [1049]

22. 4319 Kuruvinde madhusitthe,
telaṁ hatthappatāpakaṁ;
Sipāṭiphalake sucī,
mañcaṁ attharaṇena ca.

23. 4320 Senāsane pādapuñche,
sayanāsanadaṇḍake;
Dantapoṇe ca āṭalī[1050],
sīsālepanagandhake.

24. 4321 Araṇī phalapīṭhe[1051] ca,
pattapidhānathālake;
Udakassa kaṭacchū ca,
cuṇṇakaṁ rajanambaṇaṁ[1052].

25. 4322 Sammajjanaṁ udapattaṁ,
tathā vassikasāṭikaṁ;
Nisīdanaṁ kaṇḍucchādi,
atha antaravāsakaṁ. [1053]

26. 4323 Uttarāsaṅgasaṅghāṭī,
natthukaṁ mukhasodhanaṁ;
Biḷaṅgaloṇaṁ pahūtañca[1054],
madhuñca dadhipānakaṁ.

27. 4324 Dhūpaṁ sitthaṁ pilotiñca,
mukhapuñchanasuttakaṁ;
Dātabbaṁ nāma yaṁ atthi,
yañca kappati satthuno. [1055]

28. 4325 Sabbametaṁ samānetvā,
ānandaṁ upasaṅkamiṁ;
Upasaṅkamma rājānaṁ,
janetāraṁ mahesino[1056];
Sirasā abhivādetvā,
idaṁ vacanamabraviṁ.

  1. teladhāre: teladhare (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. ca: osathaṁ añjanāpica (syā2, syā3), osadhaṁ jananāliṁ ca (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. pīṭhikābhisī: pīṭhakābhisiṁ (syā2, syā3), pīṭhakābhisī (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. Bimbohane: bibbohane (syā2, syā3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. āṭalī: kathaliṁ (syā2, syā3), pāṭalī (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  6. phalapīṭhe: palālapīṭhe (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  7. rajanambaṇaṁ: rajanammaṇaṁ (sī), rajanammanaṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  8. Sammajjanaṁ: sammuñjanaṁ (syā2, syā3), sammajjaniṁ, sammuñjaniṁ (?) (Word Variant) ↩
  9. pahūtañca: bilaṅgaloṇabhūtañca (syā2, syā3, ka-ma), bilaṅgaloṇaṁ bhūtaṁ ca (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  10. Dhūpaṁ: puppha (syā2, syā3), pupphaṁ (pā1), dhūmaṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  11. mahesino: mahesinaṁ (ka-sī), mahāyasaṁ (syā2, syā3, pā1), mahissaraṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
