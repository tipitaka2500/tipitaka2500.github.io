40.1.8.5 Satthakānisaṃsa

40.1.8.5. สัต์ถกานิสํส

Pilindavacchavagga

Pilindavacchattheraapadāna

Dānānisaṃsakathā

Satthakānisaṃsa

106. 4403 Akakkase apharuse,
sudhote satthake bahū;
Pasannacitto datvāna,
buddhe saṁghe tatheva ca.

107. 4404 Pañcānisaṁse anubhomi,
kammānucchavike mama;
Kalyāṇamittaṁ vīriyaṁ,
khantiñca mettasatthakaṁ. [1085]

108. 4405 Taṇhāsallassa chinnattā,
paññāsatthaṁ anuttaraṁ;
Vajirena samaṁ ñāṇaṁ,
tesaṁ nissandato labhe.

  1. Kalyāṇamittaṁ: kalyāṇacittaṁ (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
