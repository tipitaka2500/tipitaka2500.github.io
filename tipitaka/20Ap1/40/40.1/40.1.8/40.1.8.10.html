40.1.8.10 Parissāvana-dhammakara

40.1.8.10. ปริส์สาวน-ธัม์มกร

Pilindavacchavagga

Pilindavacchattheraapadāna

Dānānisaṃsakathā

Parissāvana-dhammakara

118. 4415 Parissāvane sugate,
datvā dhammakaruttame[1088];
Pañcānisaṁse anubhomi,
kammānucchavike mama.

119. 4416 Sabbesaṁ samatikkamma,
dibbaṁ āyuṁ labhāmahaṁ;
Appasayho sadā homi,
corapaccatthikehi vā.

120. 4417 Satthena vā visena vā,
vihesampi na kubbate;
Antarāmaraṇaṁ natthi,
tesaṁ nissandato mama.

  1. dhammakaruttame: datvā sugate dhammakuttare (syā2, syā3, ka-ma), datvā saṅghe gaṇuttame (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
