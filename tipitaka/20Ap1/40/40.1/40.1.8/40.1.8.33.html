40.1.8.33. นัต์ถุกานิสํส

Pilindavacchavagga

Pilindavacchattheraapadāna

Dānānisaṃsakathā

Natthukānisaṃsa

170. 4467 Natthuke sugate datvā,
saṁghe gaṇavaruttame;
Aṭṭhānisaṁse anubhomi,
kammānucchavike mama. [1106]

171. 4468 Saddhaṁ sīlaṁ hiriñcāpi,
atha ottappiyaṁ guṇaṁ;
Sutaṁ cāgañca khantiñca,
paññaṁ me aṭṭhamaṁ guṇaṁ.

  1. Natthuke: thavike (?) bhesajjathaviketi hi pubbe (Word Variant) ↩
