40.1.8.19 Upāhanānisaṃsa

40.1.8.19. อุปาหนานิสํส

Pilindavacchavagga

Pilindavacchattheraapadāna

Dānānisaṃsakathā

Upāhanānisaṃsa

142. 4439 Upāhane jine datvā,
saṁghe gaṇavaruttame;
Tīṇānisaṁse anubhomi,
kammānucchavike mama.

143. 4440 Hatthiyānaṁ assayānaṁ,
sivikā sandamānikā;
Saṭṭhisatasahassāni,
parivārenti maṁ sadā.

144. 4441 Maṇimayā tambamayā[1095],
soṇṇarajatapādukā;
Nibbattanti paduddhāre,
bhave saṁsarato mama.

145. 4442 Niyāmaṁ sati dhāvanti[1096],
āguācārasodhanaṁ;
Ime guṇe paṭilabhe,
tassa nissandato ahaṁ. [1097]

  1. tambamayā: kambalikā (sī, ka-ma), maṇḍalakā (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. dhāvanti: niyāmaṁ patidhāvanti (sī), niyamaṁ paṭidhāvantī (ka-sī), niyāmaṁ paṭidhāvanti (syā2, syā3), niyamaṁ paṭidhāvanti (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. āguācārasodhanaṁ: ācāraguṇasodhanaṁ (ka-sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
