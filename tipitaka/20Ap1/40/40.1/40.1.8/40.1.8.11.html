Dānānisaṃsakathā

Teladhārānisaṃsa

121. 4418 Teladhāre sugate datvā,
saṁghe gaṇavaruttame;
Pañcānisaṁse anubhomi,
kammānucchavike mama.

122. 4419 Sucārurūpo subhaddo[1089],
susamuggatamānaso;
Avikkhittamano homi,
sabbārakkhehi rakkhito.

  1. subhaddo: sugado (sī), sugoto (pā1), suvāco (?) (Word Variant) ↩
