Malaharaṇānisaṃsa

166. 4463 Malaharaṇiyo datvā,
buddhe saṁghe gaṇuttame;
Pañcānisaṁse anubhomi,
kammānucchavike mama. [1104]

167. 4464 Sabbalakkhaṇasampanno,
āyupaññāsamāhito;
Sabbāyāsavinimutto,
kāyo me hoti sabbadā.

  1. Malaharaṇiyo: añjananāḷiyo (sī), hatthalīlaṅgake (syā2, syā3), hatthī līlaṅgake (pā1), hatthalilaṅgate (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
