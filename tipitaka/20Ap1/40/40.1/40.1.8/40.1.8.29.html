40.1.8.29 Tumbaka-karaṇḍa

40.1.8.29. ตุม์พก-กรัณ์ฑ

Pilindavacchavagga

Pilindavacchattheraapadāna

Dānānisaṃsakathā

Tumbaka-karaṇḍa

162. 4459 Tumbake ca karaṇḍe ca,
datvā buddhe gaṇuttame;
Dasānisaṁse anubhomi,
kammānucchavike mama.

163. 4460 Sugutto sukhasamaṅgī,
Mahāyaso tathāgati;
Vipattivigato sukhumālo,
Sabbītiparivajjito. [1102] [1103]

164. 4461 Vipule ca guṇe lābhī,
samāva calanā mama;
Suvivajjitaubbego,
tumbake ca karaṇḍake.

165. 4462 Labhāmi caturo vaṇṇe,
hatthissaratanāni ca;
Tāni me na vinassanti,
tumbadāne idaṁ phalaṁ.

  1. Sugutto: sadā gutto (sī, pā1), tadā gutto (syā2, syā3), saṁgutto (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Vipattivigato: vibhattigatto (syā2, syā3), bhattikato (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
