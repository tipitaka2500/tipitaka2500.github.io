40.1.8.13. อํสพัท์ธานิสํส

Pilindavacchattheraapadāna

Dānānisaṃsakathā

Aṃsabaddhānisaṃsa

125. 4422 Aṁsabaddhe jine datvā,
saṁghe gaṇavaruttame;
Tīṇānisaṁse anubhomi,
kammānucchavike mama.

126. 4423 Saddhamme gādhaṁ[1090] vindāmi,
Sarāmi dutiyaṁ bhavaṁ;
Sabbattha succhavī homi,
Tassa nissandato ahaṁ.

  1. gādhaṁ: cetoñāṇañca (sī), saddhamme gāḷhaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
