40.1.8.17 Thālakānisaṃsa

40.1.8.17. ถาลกานิสํส

Pilindavacchavagga

Pilindavacchattheraapadāna

Dānānisaṃsakathā

Thālakānisaṃsa

136. 4433 Thālake sugate datvā,
saṁghe gaṇavaruttame;
Tīṇānisaṁse anubhomi,
kammānucchavike mama.

137. 4434 Soṇṇamaye maṇimaye,
athopi phalikāmaye;
Lohitaṅgamaye ceva,
labhāmi thālake ahaṁ.

138. 4435 Asatthake phalamaye,
atho pokkharapattake;
Madhupānakasaṅkhe ca,
labhāmi thālake ahaṁ. [1094]

139. 4436 Vatte guṇe paṭipatti,
ācārakiriyāsu ca;
Ime guṇe paṭilabhe,
tassa nissandato ahaṁ.

  1. Asatthake: assatthake (sī, pā1), assaṭṭhake (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
