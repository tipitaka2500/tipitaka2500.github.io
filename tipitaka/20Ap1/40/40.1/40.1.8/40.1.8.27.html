Dhūmanettānisaṃsa

158. 4455 Dhūmanette jine datvā,
saṁghe gaṇavaruttame;
Tīṇānisaṁse anubhomi,
kammānucchavike mama.

159. 4456 Sati me ujukā hoti,
susambandhā ca nhāravo;
Labhāmi dibbanayanaṁ[1099],
tassa nissandato ahaṁ.

  1. dibbanayanaṁ: dibbasayanaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
