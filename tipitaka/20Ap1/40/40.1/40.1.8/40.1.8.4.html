Pilindavacchattheraapadāna

Dānānisaṃsakathā

Vāsiānisaṃsa

103. 4400 Cittabandhanasambaddhā,
sukatā vāsiyo bahū;
Datvāna buddhaseṭṭhassa,
saṁghassa ca tathevahaṁ. [1084]

104. 4401 Aṭṭhānisaṁse anubhomi,
kammānucchavike mama;
Sūro homavisārī ca,
vesārajjesu pāramī.

105. 4402 Dhitivīriyavā homi,
paggahītamano sadā;
Kilesacchedanaṁ ñāṇaṁ,
sukhumaṁ atulaṁ suciṁ;
Sabbattha paṭilabhāmi,
tassa nissandato ahaṁ.

  1. Cittabandhanasambaddhā: cittabandhanasampannā (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
