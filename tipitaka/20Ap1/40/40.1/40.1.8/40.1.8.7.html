40.1.8.7 Nakhacchedanānisaṃsa

40.1.8.7. นขัจ์เฉทนานิสํส

Pilindavacchavagga

Pilindavacchattheraapadāna

Dānānisaṃsakathā

Nakhacchedanānisaṃsa

112. 4409 Nakhacchedane sugate,
datvā saṁghe gaṇuttame;
Pañcānisaṁse anubhomi,
kammānucchavike mama.

113. 4410 Dāsidāse gavasse ca,
bhatake nāṭake[1087] bahū;
Nhāpite bhattake sūde,
sabbattheva labhāmahaṁ. [1086]

  1. Dāsidāse: dāsadāsī (syā2, syā3), dāsidāsa (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. nāṭake: ārakkhake (sī), ārakkhe (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
