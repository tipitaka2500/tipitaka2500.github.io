Phalapīṭhānisaṃsa

187. 4484 Phalapīṭhe jine datvā,
saṁghe gaṇavaruttame;
Dvānisaṁse anubhomi,
kammānucchavike mama. [1114]

188. 4485 Soṇṇamaye maṇimaye,
dantasāramaye bahū;
Pallaṅkaseṭṭhe vindāmi,
phalapīṭhassidaṁ phalaṁ.

  1. Phalapīṭhe: palālapīṭhe (ka-sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
