40.1.6 Dānakathā

40.1.6. ทานกถา

Pilindavacchavagga

Pilindavacchattheraapadāna

Dānakathā

66. 4363 Bhikkhuno ekamekassa,
Paccekaṁ pattamadāsahaṁ;
Jahiṁsu sumbhakaṁ[1074] pattaṁ,
Lohapattaṁ adhārayuṁ.

67. 4364 Sattarattindivaṁ buddho,
nisīdi pupphamaṇḍape;
Bodhayanto bahū satte,
dhammacakkaṁ pavattayi.

68. 4365 Dhammacakkaṁ pavattento,
heṭṭhato pupphamaṇḍape;
Cullāsītisahassānaṁ,
dhammābhisamayo ahu.

69. 4366 Sattame divase patte,
padumuttaro mahāmuni;
Chattacchāyāyamāsīno,
imā gāthā abhāsatha.

  1. sumbhakaṁ: pubbakaṁ (sī), sambhataṁ (syā2, syā3), pupphakaṁ (pā1), mattikaṁ (?) (Word Variant) ↩
