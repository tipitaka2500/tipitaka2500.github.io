10.3 Aḍḍhaceḷakattheraapadāna

10.3. อัฑ์ฒเจฬกัต์เถรอปทาน

Sudhāvagga

Aḍḍhaceḷakattheraapadāna

14. 1782 [482] “Tissassāhaṁ bhagavato,
upaḍḍhadussamadāsahaṁ;
Paramakāpaññapattomhi,
duggatena samappito. [483] [484]

15. 1783 Upaḍḍhadussaṁ datvāna,
kappaṁ saggamhi modahaṁ;
Avasesesu kappesu,
kusalaṁ kāritaṁ mayā.

16. 1784 Dvenavute ito kappe,
yaṁ dussamadadiṁ tadā;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
dussadānassidaṁ phalaṁ.

17. 1785 Ekūnapaññāsakappamhi,
rājāno cakkavattino;
Samantacchadanā nāma,
bāttiṁsāsuṁ janādhipā. [485] [486]

18. 1786 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

1787 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā aḍḍhaceḷako[487] thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

1788 Aḍḍhaceḷakattherassāpadānaṁ tatiyaṁ.

  1. aḍḍhacelakattherāpadānaṁ (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Paramakāpaññapattomhi: paramakāruññapattomhi (ka-sī, syā2, syā3, ka-ma), parakāpaññapattomhi (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. duggatena: duggandhena (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. Ekūnapaññāsakappamhi: ekūnapaṇṇekappamhi (sī), ekapaññāsakappamhi (syā2, syā3), ekūnapaṇṇe kappamhi (pā1), ka-sī, cha1-2 (Word Variant) ↩
  5. bāttiṁsāsuṁ: battiṁsāsuṁ (sī), khattiyāsuṁ (syā2, syā3, ka-ma), battiṁsāsu (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  6. aḍḍhaceḷako: aḍḍhacelako (sī, syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
