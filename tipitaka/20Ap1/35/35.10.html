35.10. เอกั์ชลิยัต์เถรอปทาน

Ekapadumiyavagga

Ekañjaliyattheraapadāna

63. 3961 “Romaso nāma sambuddho,
nadīkūle vasī tadā;
Addasaṁ virajaṁ buddhaṁ,
pītaraṁsiṁva bhāṇumaṁ. [973]

64. 3962 Ukkāmukhapahaṭṭhaṁva,
khadiraṅgārasannibhaṁ;
Osadhiṁva virocantaṁ,
ekañjalimakāsahaṁ.

65. 3963 Catunnavutito kappe,
yaṁ añjalimakāsahaṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
añjaliyā idaṁ phalaṁ.

66. 3964 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”. (3133)

3965 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā ekañjaliyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

3966 Ekañjaliyattherassāpadānaṁ dasamaṁ.

3967 Ekapadumiyavaggo[974] pañcatiṁsatimo.

3968

3969 Padumī uppalamālī,
dhajo kiṅkaṇikaṁ naḷaṁ[975];
Campako padumo muṭṭhi,
tindukekañjalī tathā;
Cha ca saṭṭhi ca gāthāyo,
gaṇitāyo vibhāvibhi.

  1. "Romaso: revato (sī, syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Ekapadumiyavaggo: ekapadumavaggo (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. naḷaṁ: kiṅkiṇiko naḷo (sī), kiṅkhaṇiko nalo (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
