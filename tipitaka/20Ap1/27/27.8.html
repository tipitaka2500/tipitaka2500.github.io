27.8 Saḷalapupphiyattheraapadāna

27.8. สฬลปุป์ผิยัต์เถรอปทาน

Padumukkhipavagga

Saḷalapupphiyattheraapadāna

30. 3201 [800] “Candabhāgānadītīre,
ahosiṁ kinnaro tadā;
Vipassiṁ addasaṁ buddhaṁ,
raṁsijālasamākulaṁ.

31. 3202 Pasannacitto sumano,
paramāya ca pītiyā;
Paggayha saḷalaṁ[801] pupphaṁ,
vipassiṁ okiriṁ ahaṁ.

32. 3203 Ekanavutito kappe,
yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

33. 3204 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

3205 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā saḷalapupphiyo[802] thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

3206 Saḷalapupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.

  1. salalapupphiyattherāpadānaṁ (sī), salaḷapupphiyattherāpadānaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. saḷalaṁ: salala (sī), salaḷaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. saḷalapupphiyo: salalapupphiyo (sī), salaḷapupphiyo (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
