27.3 Aḍḍhacandiyattheraapadāna

27.3. อัฑ์ฒจัน์ทิยัต์เถรอปทาน

Padumukkhipavagga

Aḍḍhacandiyattheraapadāna

10. 3171 “Tissassa kho bhagavato,
bodhiyā pādaputtame;
Aḍḍhacandaṁ mayā dinnaṁ,
dharaṇīruhapādape.

11. 3172 Dvenavute ito kappe,
yaṁ candamabhiropayiṁ[797];
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
bodhipūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

12. 3173 Pañcavīse ito kappe,
devalo nāma khattiyo;
Sattaratanasampanno,
cakkavattī mahabbalo.

13. 3174 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

3175 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā aḍḍhacandiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

3176 Aḍḍhacandiyattherassāpadānaṁ tatiyaṁ.

  1. candamabhiropayiṁ: yaṁ pupphamabhiropayiṁ (sī, pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
