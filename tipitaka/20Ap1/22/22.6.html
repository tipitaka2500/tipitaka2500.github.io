22.6 Sandhitattheraapadāna

22.6. สัน์ธิตัต์เถรอปทาน

Hatthivagga

Sandhitattheraapadāna

27. 2858 [726] “Assatthe haritobhāse,
saṁvirūḷhamhi pādape;
Ekaṁ buddhagataṁ saññaṁ,
alabhiṁhaṁ patissato. [727]

28. 2859 Ekattiṁse ito kappe,
yaṁ saññamalabhiṁ tadā;
Tassā saññāya vāhasā,
patto me āsavakkhayo.

29. 2860 Ito terasakappamhi,
dhaniṭṭho nāma khattiyo;
Sattaratanasampanno,
cakkavattī mahabbalo.

30. 2861 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

2862 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā sandhito[728] thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2863 Sandhitattherassāpadānaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.

  1. saṇṭhitattherāpadānaṁ (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. alabhiṁhaṁ: alabhissaṁ (sī, pā1), alabhitthaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. sandhito: saṇṭhito (sī), saṇṭhita (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
