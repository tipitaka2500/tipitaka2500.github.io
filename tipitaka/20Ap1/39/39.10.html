Pubbakammapilotikabuddhaapadāna

64. 4258 [1027] Anotattasarāsanne,
ramaṇīye silātale;
Nānāratanapajjote,
nānāgandhavanantare.

65. 4259 Mahatā bhikkhusaṁghena,
pareto lokanāyako;
Āsīno byākarī tattha,
pubbakammāni attano. [1028]

66. 4260 [1029] “Suṇātha bhikkhavo mayhaṁ,
yaṁ kammaṁ pakataṁ mayā;
Pilotikassa kammassa,
buddhattepi vipaccati[1030].
