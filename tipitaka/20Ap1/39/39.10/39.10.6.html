39.10.6.

Avaṭaphalavagga

Pubbakammapilotikabuddhaapadāna

82. 4276 Hatthāroho pure āsiṁ,
paccekamunimuttamaṁ;
Piṇḍāya vicarantaṁ taṁ,
āsādesiṁ gajenahaṁ.

83. 4277 Tena kammavipākena,
bhanto nāḷāgirī[1036] gajo;
Giribbaje puravare,
dāruṇo samupāgami[1037]. [1035]

  1. bhanto: danto (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. nāḷāgirī: nālāgirī (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. samupāgami: maṁ upāgami (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
