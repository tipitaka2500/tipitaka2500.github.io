39.10.4.

Avaṭaphalavagga

Pubbakammapilotikabuddhaapadāna

78. 4272 Vemātubhātaraṁ pubbe,
dhanahetu haniṁ ahaṁ;
Pakkhipiṁ giriduggasmiṁ,
silāya ca apiṁsayiṁ.

79. 4273 Tena kammavipākena,
devadatto silaṁ khipi;
Aṅguṭṭhaṁ piṁsayī pāde,
mama pāsāṇasakkharā.
