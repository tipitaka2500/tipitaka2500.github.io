39.10.3

39.10.3.

Avaṭaphalavagga

Pubbakammapilotikabuddhaapadāna

73. 4267 Brāhmaṇo sutavā āsiṁ,
ahaṁ sakkatapūjito;
Mahāvane pañcasate,
mante vācemi māṇave.

74. 4268 Tatthāgato isi bhīmo,
pañcābhiñño mahiddhiko;
Tañcāhaṁ āgataṁ disvā,
abbhācikkhiṁ adūsakaṁ. [1032]

75. 4269 Tatohaṁ avacaṁ sisse,
kāmabhogī ayaṁ isi;
Mayhampi bhāsamānassa,
anumodiṁsu māṇavā.

76. 4270 Tato māṇavakā sabbe,
bhikkhamānaṁ kule kule;
Mahājanassa āhaṁsu,
kāmabhogī ayaṁ isi.

77. 4271 Tena kammavipākena,
pañca bhikkhusatā ime;
Abbhakkhānaṁ labhuṁ sabbe,
sundarikāya kāraṇā.

  1. Tatthāgato: tamāgato (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
