39.10.9.

Avaṭaphalavagga

Pubbakammapilotikabuddhaapadāna

88. 4282 Phussassāhaṁ pāvacane,
sāvake paribhāsayiṁ;
Yavaṁ khādatha bhuñjatha,
mā ca bhuñjatha sālayo.

89. 4283 Tena kammavipākena,
temāsaṁ khāditaṁ yavaṁ;
Nimantito brāhmaṇena,
verañjāyaṁ vasiṁ tadā.
