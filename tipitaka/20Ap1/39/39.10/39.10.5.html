39.10.5.

Avaṭaphalavagga

Pubbakammapilotikabuddhaapadāna

80. 4274 Purehaṁ dārako hutvā,
kīḷamāno mahāpathe;
Paccekabuddhaṁ disvāna,
magge sakalikaṁ[1033] khipiṁ[1034].

81. 4275 Tena kammavipākena,
idha pacchimake bhave;
Vadhatthaṁ maṁ devadatto,
abhimāre payojayi.

  1. sakalikaṁ: sakkhalikaṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. khipiṁ: dahiṁ (syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
