39.10.8.

Avaṭaphalavagga

Pubbakammapilotikabuddhaapadāna

86. 4280 Ahaṁ kevaṭṭagāmasmiṁ,
ahuṁ kevaṭṭadārako;
Macchake ghātite disvā,
janayiṁ somanassakaṁ.

87. 4281 Tena kammavipākena,
sīsadukkhaṁ ahū mama;
Sabbe sakkā ca haññiṁsu,
yadā hani viṭaṭūbho[1040].

  1. viṭaṭūbho: viḍūḍabho (sī), viṭaṭubho (syā2, syā3), viḍuḍabho (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
