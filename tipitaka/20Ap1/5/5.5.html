5.5 Pañcahatthiyattheraapadāna

5.5. ปั์จหัต์ถิยัต์เถรอปทาน

Upālivagga

Pañcahatthiyattheraapadāna

77. 1293 “Sumedho nāma sambuddho,
gacchate antarāpaṇe;
Okkhittacakkhu mitabhāṇī,
satimā saṁvutindriyo. [358]

78. 1294 Pañca uppalahatthāni,
āveḷatthaṁ ahaṁsu me;
Tena buddhaṁ apūjesiṁ,
pasanno sehi pāṇibhi.

79. 1295 Āropitā ca te pupphā,
chadanaṁ assu satthuno;
Samādhiṁsu mahānāgaṁ,
sissā ācariyaṁ yathā. [359]

80. 1296 Tiṁsakappasahassamhi,
yaṁ pupphamabhiropayiṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

81. 1297 Ito vīsakappasate,
ahesuṁ pañca khattiyā;
Hatthiyā nāma nāmena,
cakkavattī mahabbalā.

82. 1298 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

1299 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā pañcahatthiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

1300 Pañcahatthiyattherassāpadānaṁ pañcamaṁ.

  1. Okkhittacakkhu: khittacakkhu (ka-sī, pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Samādhiṁsu: saṁsāviṁsu (sī, pā1), samādhīsu (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
