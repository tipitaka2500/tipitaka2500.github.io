33.2 Pulinapūjakattheraapadāna

33.2. ปุลินปูชกัต์เถรอปทาน

Umāpupphiyavagga

Pulinapūjakattheraapadāna

7. 3594 [889] “Kakudhaṁ vilasantaṁva,
nisabhājāniyaṁ yathā;
Osadhiṁva virocantaṁ,
obhāsantaṁ narāsabhaṁ.

8. 3595 Añjaliṁ paggahetvāna,
avandiṁ satthuno ahaṁ;
Satthāraṁ parivaṇṇesiṁ,
sakakammena tosayiṁ[890].

9. 3596 Susuddhaṁ pulinaṁ[891] gayha,
gatamagge samokiriṁ;
Ucchaṅgena gahetvāna,
vipassissa mahesino.

10. 3597 Tato upaḍḍhapulinaṁ,
vippasannena cetasā;
Divāvihāre osiñciṁ,
dvipadindassa tādino.

11. 3598 Ekanavutito kappe,
pulinaṁ yamasiñcahaṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
pulinassa idaṁ phalaṁ.

12. 3599 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

3600 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā pulinapūjako[892] thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

3601 Pulinapūjakattherassāpadānaṁ dutiyaṁ.

  1. puḷinapūjakattherāpadānaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. tosayiṁ: tosito (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. pulinaṁ: puḷinaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. pulinapūjako: puḷinapūjako (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
