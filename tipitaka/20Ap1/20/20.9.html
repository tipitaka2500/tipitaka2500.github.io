20.9 Kiṃkaṇikapupphiyattheraapadāna

20.9. กิํกณิกปุป์ผิยัต์เถรอปทาน

Tamālapupphiyavagga

Kiṃkaṇikapupphiyattheraapadāna

47. 2728 [700] “Sumaṅgaloti nāmena,
sayambhū aparājito;
Pavanā nikkhamitvāna,
nagaraṁ pāvisī jino.

48. 2729 Piṇḍacāraṁ caritvāna,
nikkhamma nagarā muni;
Katakiccova sambuddho,
so vasī vanamantare.

49. 2730 Kiṁkaṇipupphaṁ paggayha,
buddhassa abhiropayiṁ;
Pasannacitto sumano,
sayambhussa mahesino. [701]

50. 2731 Catunnavutito kappe,
yaṁ pupphamabhiropayiṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

51. 2732 Chaḷāsītimhito kappe,
apilāsisanāmako;
Sattaratanasampanno,
cakkavattī mahabbalo.

52. 2733 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

2734 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā kiṁkaṇikapupphiyo[702] thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2735 Kiṁkaṇikapupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ navamaṁ.

  1. kiṅkaṇipupphiyattherāpadānaṁ (sī), tikaṇḍipupphiyattherāpadānaṁ (syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Kiṁkaṇipupphaṁ: tikaṇḍipupphaṁ (syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. kiṁkaṇikapupphiyo: kiṅkaṇipupphiyo (sī), tikaṇḍipupphiyo (syā2, syā3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
