4.4 Kāḷudāyittheraapadāna

4.4. กาฬุทายิต์เถรอปทาน

Kuṇḍadhānavagga

Kāḷudāyittheraapadāna

48. 1126 “Padumuttarabuddhassa,
lokajeṭṭhassa tādino;
Addhānaṁ paṭipannassa,
carato cārikaṁ tadā.

49. 1127 Suphullaṁ padumaṁ gayha,
uppalaṁ mallikañcahaṁ;
Paramannaṁ gahetvāna,
adāsiṁ satthuno ahaṁ.

50. 1128 Paribhuñji mahāvīro,
paramannaṁ subhojanaṁ;
Tañca pupphaṁ gahetvāna,
janassa sampadassayi.

51. 1129 Iṭṭhaṁ kantaṁ[311] piyaṁ loke,
jalajaṁ pupphamuttamaṁ;
Sudukkaraṁ kataṁ tena,
yo me pupphaṁ adāsidaṁ.

52. 1130 Yo pupphamabhiropesi,
paramannañcadāsi me;
Tamahaṁ kittayissāmi,
suṇātha mama bhāsato.

53. 1131 ‘Dasa aṭṭha cakkhattuṁ[312] so,
devarajjaṁ karissati;
Uppalaṁ padumañcāpi,
mallikañca taduttari.

54. 1132 Assa puññavipākena,
dibbagandhasamāyutaṁ;
Ākāse chadanaṁ katvā,
dhārayissati tāvade.

55. 1133 Pañcavīsatikkhattuñca,
cakkavattī bhavissati;
Pathabyā rajjaṁ pañcasataṁ,
vasudhaṁ āvasissati.

56. 1134 Kappasatasahassamhi,
okkākakulasambhavo;
Gotamo nāma gottena[313],
satthā loke bhavissati.

57. 1135 Sakakammābhiraddho so,
sukkamūlena codito;
Sakyānaṁ nandijanano,
ñātibandhu bhavissati.

58. 1136 So pacchā pabbajitvāna,
sukkamūlena codito;
Sabbāsave pariññāya,
nibbāyissatināsavo.

59. 1137 Paṭisambhidamanuppattaṁ,
katakiccamanāsavaṁ;
Gotamo lokabandhu taṁ[314],
etadagge ṭhapessati.

60. 1138 Padhānapahitatto so,
upasanto nirūpadhi;
Udāyī nāma nāmena,
hessati satthu sāvako’.

61. 1139 Rāgo doso ca moho ca,
māno makkho ca dhaṁsito;
Sabbāsave pariññāya,
viharāmi anāsavo.

62. 1140 Tosayiñcāpi sambuddhaṁ,
ātāpī nipako ahaṁ;
Pasādito ca sambuddho,
etadagge ṭhapesi maṁ. [315]

63. 1141 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

1142 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā kāḷudāyī[316] thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

1143 Kāḷudāyittherassāpadānaṁ catutthaṁ.

  1. kantaṁ: kantayidaṁ (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. cakkhattuṁ: dasa caṭṭhakkhattuṁ (sī), dasamaṭṭhakkhattuñca (syā2, syā3), dasa caṭṭha cakkhattuñ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. gottena: nāmena (syā2, syā3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. taṁ: so (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. Pasādito: pamodito (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  6. kāḷudāyī: kāḷudāyi (syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
