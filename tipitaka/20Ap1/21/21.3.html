21.3 Kiṅkaṇipupphiyattheraapadāna

21.3. กิงกณิปุป์ผิยัต์เถรอปทาน

Kaṇikārapupphiyavagga

Kiṅkaṇipupphiyattheraapadāna

10. 2763 [706] “Kañcanagghiyasaṅkāso,
sabbaññū lokanāyako;
Odakaṁ dahamoggayha,
sināyi lokanāyako.

11. 2764 Paggayha kiṅkaṇiṁ[707] pupphaṁ,
Vipassissābhiropayiṁ;
Udaggacitto sumano,
Dvipadindassa tādino.

12. 2765 Ekanavutito kappe,
yaṁ pupphamabhiropayiṁ;
Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,
buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

13. 2766 Sattavīsatikappamhi,
rājā bhīmaratho ahu;
Sattaratanasampanno,
cakkavattī mahabbalo.

14. 2767 Paṭisambhidā catasso,
…pe…
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

2768 Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā kiṅkaṇipupphiyo[708] thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2769 Kiṅkaṇipupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ tatiyaṁ.

  1. kiṅkiṇikapupphiyattherāpadānaṁ (sī), kiṁkaṇikapupphiyattherāpadānaṁ (syā2, syā3), kiṅkhaṇikapupphiyattherāpadānaṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. kiṅkaṇiṁ: kiṅkiṇiṁ (sī), kiṁkaṇi (syā2, syā3), kiṅkhaṇiṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. kiṅkaṇipupphiyo: kiṅkiṇikapupphiyo (sī), kiṁkaṇikapupphiyo (syā2, syā3), kiṅkhaṇikapupphiyo (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
