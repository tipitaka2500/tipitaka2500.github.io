9.1.7.2. ปัจ์จนียุท์ธาร

Mahantaraduka

Sārammaṇaduka

Pañhāvāra

Paccanīyuddhāra

45. 2411 Sārammaṇo dhammo sārammaṇassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo… sahajātapaccayena paccayo… upanissayapaccayena paccayo… kammapaccayena paccayo. (1)

2412 Sārammaṇo dhammo anārammaṇassa dhammassa sahajātapaccayena paccayo… pacchājātapaccayena paccayo… kammapaccayena paccayo. (2)

2413 Sārammaṇo dhammo sārammaṇassa ca anārammaṇassa ca dhammassa sahajātapaccayena paccayo… kammapaccayena paccayo. (3)

46. 2414 Anārammaṇo dhammo anārammaṇassa dhammassa sahajātapaccayena paccayo… āhārapaccayena paccayo… indriyapaccayena paccayo. (1)

2415 Anārammaṇo dhammo sārammaṇassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo… sahajātapaccayena paccayo… upanissayapaccayena paccayo… purejātapaccayena paccayo. (2)

2416 Sārammaṇo ca anārammaṇo ca dhammā sārammaṇassa dhammassa sahajātaṁ, purejātaṁ. (1)

2417 Sārammaṇo ca anārammaṇo ca dhammā anārammaṇassa dhammassa sahajātaṁ, pacchājātaṁ, āhāraṁ, indriyaṁ. (2)
