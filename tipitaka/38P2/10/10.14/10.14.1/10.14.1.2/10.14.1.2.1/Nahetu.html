3384 Upādinnaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca anupādinno dhammo uppajjati nahetupaccayā— ahetuke upādinne khandhe paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ. (2)

3385 Upādinnaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca upādinno ca anupādinno ca dhammā uppajjanti nahetupaccayā— ahetukaṁ upādinnaṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ paṭicca tayo khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañca rūpaṁ…pe… dve khandhe…pe… . (3)
