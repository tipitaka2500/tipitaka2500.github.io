301. 3057 Cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhāno dhammo cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo— cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhāne khandhe ārabbha cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānā khandhā uppajjanti.
