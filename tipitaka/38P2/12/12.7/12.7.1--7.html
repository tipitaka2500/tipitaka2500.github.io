11.7.1. ปฏิจ์จาทิวาร

Kilesagocchaka

Kilesakilesasampayuttaduka

Paṭiccādivāra

97. 4051 Kilesañceva kilesasampayuttañca dhammaṁ paṭicca kileso ceva kilesasampayutto ca dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— lobhaṁ paṭicca moho diṭṭhi thinaṁ uddhaccaṁ ahirikaṁ anottappaṁ. (Kilesasaṁkiliṭṭhadukasadisaṁ ninnānākaraṇaṁ, sabbe vārā.)

4052 Kilesakilesasampayuttadukaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
