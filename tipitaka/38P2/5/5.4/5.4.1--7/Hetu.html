Ganthaganthaniyaduka

Paṭiccādivāra

Hetu

103. 1842 Ganthañceva ganthaniyañca dhammaṁ paṭicca gantho ceva ganthaniyo ca dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— sīlabbataparāmāsaṁ kāyaganthaṁ paṭicca abhijjhākāyagantho, abhijjhākāyaganthaṁ paṭicca sīlabbataparāmāso kāyagantho, idaṁsaccābhinivesakāyaganthaṁ paṭicca abhijjhākāyagantho, abhijjhākāyaganthaṁ paṭicca idaṁsaccābhinivesakāyagantho. (1)

1843 Ganthañceva ganthaniyañca dhammaṁ paṭicca ganthaniyo ceva no ca gantho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— ganthe paṭicca sampayuttakā khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañca rūpaṁ. (2)

1844 Ganthañceva ganthaniyañca dhammaṁ paṭicca gantho ceva ganthaniyo ca ganthaniyo ceva no ca gantho ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā. (1)
