Anantara

25. 1668 Gantho dhammo ganthassa dhammassa anantarapaccayena paccayo— purimā purimā ganthā pacchimānaṁ pacchimānaṁ ganthānaṁ anantarapaccayena paccayo. (1)

1669 Gantho dhammo noganthassa dhammassa anantarapaccayena paccayo— purimā purimā ganthā pacchimānaṁ pacchimānaṁ noganthānaṁ khandhānaṁ anantarapaccayena paccayo; ganthā vuṭṭhānassa anantarapaccayena paccayo. (2)

1670 Gantho dhammo ganthassa ca noganthassa ca dhammassa anantarapaccayena paccayo— purimā purimā ganthā pacchimānaṁ pacchimānaṁ ganthānaṁ sampayuttakānañca khandhānaṁ anantarapaccayena paccayo. (3)

26. 1671 Nogantho dhammo noganthassa dhammassa anantarapaccayena paccayo… tīṇi. (Dve āvajjanā kātabbā, paṭhamo natthi.)

1672 Gantho ca nogantho ca dhammā ganthassa dhammassa anantarapaccayena paccayo… tīṇi. (Ekampi vuṭṭhānaṁ kātabbaṁ, majjhe.)
