1.1.7 Sabbapariññāsutta

1.1.7. สัพ์พปริ์าสุต์ต

Ekakanipāta

Paṭhamavagga

Sabbapariññāsutta

7. 32 Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ—

33 “Sabbaṁ, bhikkhave, anabhijānaṁ aparijānaṁ tattha cittaṁ avirājayaṁ appajahaṁ abhabbo dukkhakkhayāya. Sabbañca kho, bhikkhave, abhijānaṁ parijānaṁ tattha cittaṁ virājayaṁ pajahaṁ bhabbo dukkhakkhayāyā”ti. Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati—

34 “Yo sabbaṁ sabbato ñatvā,
sabbatthesu na rajjati;
Sa ve sabbapariññā[2] so,
sabbadukkhamupaccagā”ti. [3]

35 Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

36 Sattamaṁ.

  1. sabbapariññā: sabbaṁ pariññāya (sī), sabbaṁ pariññā (syā1-3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. sabbadukkhamupaccagā"ti: sabbaṁ dukkhaṁ upaccagāti (syā1-3), sabbadukkhaṁ upaccagāti (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
