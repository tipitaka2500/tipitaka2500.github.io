2.1.7 Ātāpīsutta

2.1.7. อาตาปีสุต์ต

Dukanipāta

Paṭhamavagga

Ātāpīsutta

34. 208 [64] Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ—

209 “Anātāpī, bhikkhave, bhikkhu anottāpī[65] abhabbo sambodhāya, abhabbo nibbānāya, abhabbo anuttarassa yogakkhemassa adhigamāya. Ātāpī ca kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu ottāpī[66] bhabbo sambodhāya, bhabbo nibbānāya, bhabbo anuttarassa yogakkhemassa adhigamāyā”ti. Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati—

210 “Anātāpī anottāpī,
kusīto hīnavīriyo;
Yo thinamiddhabahulo,
ahirīko anādaro;
Abhabbo tādiso bhikkhu,
phuṭṭhuṁ sambodhimuttamaṁ. [67]

211 Yo ca satimā nipako jhāyī,
Ātāpī ottāpī ca appamatto;
Saṁyojanaṁ jātijarāya chetvā,
Idheva sambodhimanuttaraṁ phuse”ti.

212 Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

213 Sattamaṁ.

  1. anātāpīsuttaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. anottāpī: anottappī (ka-sī, syā1-3, pā1) aṭṭhakathā (Word Variant) ↩
  3. ottāpī: ottappī (ka-sī, syā1-3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. ahirīko: ahiriko (syā1-3) aṭṭha. (Word Variant) ↩
