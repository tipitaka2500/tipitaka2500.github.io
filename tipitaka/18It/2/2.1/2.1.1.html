2.1.1 Dukkhavihārasutta

2.1.1. ทุก์ขวิหารสุต์ต

Dukanipāta

Paṭhamavagga

Dukkhavihārasutta

28. 172 [53] ( )[54] Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ—

173 “Dvīhi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu diṭṭheva dhamme dukkhaṁ viharati savighātaṁ saupāyāsaṁ sapariḷāhaṁ; kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā duggati pāṭikaṅkhā. Katamehi dvīhi? Indriyesu aguttadvāratāya[55] ca, bhojane amattaññutāya[56] ca. Imehi kho, bhikkhave, dvīhi dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu diṭṭheva dhamme dukkhaṁ viharati savighātaṁ saupāyāsaṁ sapariḷāhaṁ; kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā duggati pāṭikaṅkhā”ti. Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati—

174 “Cakkhu sotañca[57] ghānañca,
jivhā kāyo tathā mano;
Etāni yassa dvārāni,
aguttāni ca bhikkhuno.

175 Bhojanamhi amattaññū,
indriyesu asaṁvuto;
Kāyadukkhaṁ cetodukkhaṁ,
dukkhaṁ so adhigacchati.

176 Ḍayhamānena kāyena,
Ḍayhamānena cetasā;
Divā vā yadi vā rattiṁ,
Dukkhaṁ viharati tādiso”ti.

177 Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

178 Paṭhamaṁ.
