4.1.9 Kuhasutta

4.1.9. กุหสุต์ต

Catukkanipāta

Brāhmaṇadhammayāgavagga

Kuhasutta

108. 786 Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ—

787 [230] “Ye keci, bhikkhave, bhikkhū kuhā thaddhā lapā siṅgī unnaḷā asamāhitā, na me te[231], bhikkhave, bhikkhū māmakā. Apagatā ca te, bhikkhave, bhikkhū imasmā dhammavinayā; na ca te imasmiṁ dhammavinaye[232] vuddhiṁ virūḷhiṁ vepullaṁ āpajjanti. Ye ca kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhū nikkuhā nillapā dhīrā atthaddhā susamāhitā, te kho me, bhikkhave, bhikkhū māmakā. Anapagatā ca te, bhikkhave, bhikkhū imasmā dhammavinayā; te ca imasmiṁ dhammavinaye vuddhiṁ virūḷhiṁ vepullaṁ āpajjantī”ti. Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati—

788 [233] “Kuhā thaddhā lapā siṅgī,
unnaḷā asamāhitā;
Na te dhamme virūhanti,
sammāsambuddhadesite.

789 Nikkuhā nillapā dhīrā,
atthaddhā susamāhitā;
Te ve dhamme virūhanti,
sammāsambuddhadesite”ti.

790 Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

791 Navamaṁ.
