4.1.10 Nadīsotasutta

4.1.10. นทีโสตสุต์ต

Catukkanipāta

Brāhmaṇadhammayāgavagga

Nadīsotasutta

109. 792 [234] Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ—

793 “Seyyathāpi, bhikkhave, puriso nadiyā sotena ovuyheyya piyarūpasātarūpena. Tamenaṁ cakkhumā puriso tīre ṭhito disvā evaṁ vadeyya— ‘kiñcāpi kho tvaṁ, ambho purisa, nadiyā sotena ovuyhasi piyarūpasātarūpena, atthi cettha heṭṭhā rahado saūmi sāvaṭṭo sagaho sarakkhaso yaṁ tvaṁ, ambho purisa, rahadaṁ pāpuṇitvā maraṇaṁ vā nigacchasi maraṇamattaṁ vā dukkhan’ti. Atha kho so, bhikkhave, puriso tassa purisassa saddaṁ sutvā hatthehi ca pādehi ca paṭisotaṁ vāyameyya.

794 Upamā kho me ayaṁ, bhikkhave, katā atthassa viññāpanāya. Ayañcettha[235] attho— ‘nadiyā soto’ti kho, bhikkhave, taṇhāyetaṁ adhivacanaṁ.

795 ‘Piyarūpaṁ sātarūpan’ti kho, bhikkhave, channetaṁ ajjhattikānaṁ āyatanānaṁ adhivacanaṁ.

796 ‘Heṭṭhā rahado’ti kho, bhikkhave, pañcannaṁ orambhāgiyānaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ adhivacanaṁ.

797 ‘Ūmibhayan’ti kho[236], bhikkhave, kodhupāyāsassetaṁ adhivacanaṁ.

798 ‘Āvaṭṭan’ti kho[237], bhikkhave, pañcannetaṁ kāmaguṇānaṁ adhivacanaṁ.

799 ‘Gaharakkhaso’ti kho[238], bhikkhave, mātugāmassetaṁ adhivacanaṁ.

800 ‘Paṭisoto’ti kho, bhikkhave, nekkhammassetaṁ adhivacanaṁ.

801 ‘Hatthehi ca pādehi ca vāyāmo’ti kho, bhikkhave, vīriyārambhassetaṁ adhivacanaṁ.

802 ‘Cakkhumā puriso tīre ṭhito’ti kho, bhikkhave, tathāgatassetaṁ adhivacanaṁ arahato sammāsambuddhassā”ti. Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati—

803 “Sahāpi dukkhena jaheyya kāme,
Yogakkhemaṁ āyatiṁ patthayāno;
Sammappajāno suvimuttacitto,
Vimuttiyā phassaye tattha tattha;
Sa vedagū vūsitabrahmacariyo,
Lokantagū pāragatoti vuccatī”ti.

804 Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

805 Dasamaṁ.

  1. purisapiyarūpasuttaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Ayañcettha: ayamettha (sī), ayañcevettha (syā1-3) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. kho: saūmīti kho (sī, syā1-3), saummīti (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. kho: sāvaṭṭoti kho (bahūsu) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. kho: sagaho sarakkhasoti kho (bahūsu) (Word Variant) ↩
