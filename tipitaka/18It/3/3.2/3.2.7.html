3.2.7 Soceyyasutta

3.2.7. โสเจย์ยสุต์ต

Tikanipāta

Dutiyavagga

Soceyyasutta

66. 425 Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ—

426 “Tīṇimāni, bhikkhave, soceyyāni. Katamāni tīṇi? Kāyasoceyyaṁ, vacīsoceyyaṁ, manosoceyyaṁ— imāni kho, bhikkhave, tīṇi soceyyānī”ti. Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati—

427 “Kāyasuciṁ vacīsuciṁ[129],
cetosucimanāsavaṁ;
Suciṁ soceyyasampannaṁ,
āhu sabbappahāyinan”ti. [130]

428 Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

429 Sattamaṁ.

  1. vacīsuciṁ: vācāsuciṁ (sī, syā1-3, pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. sabbappahāyinan"ti: āhu sabbapahāyinanti (syā1-3, pā1), āhu ninhātapāpakanti (15A3:948) (Word Variant) ↩
