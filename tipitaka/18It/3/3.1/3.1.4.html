3.1.4 Dutiyavedanāsutta

3.1.4. ทุติยเวทนาสุต์ต

Tikanipāta

Paṭhamavagga

Dutiyavedanāsutta

53. 344 Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ—

345 “Tisso imā, bhikkhave, vedanā. Katamā tisso? Sukhā vedanā, dukkhā vedanā, adukkhamasukhā vedanā. Sukhā, bhikkhave, vedanā dukkhato daṭṭhabbā; dukkhā vedanā sallato daṭṭhabbā; adukkhamasukhā vedanā aniccato daṭṭhabbā. Yato kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno sukhā vedanā dukkhato diṭṭhā hoti, dukkhā vedanā sallato diṭṭhā hoti, adukkhamasukhā vedanā aniccato diṭṭhā hoti; ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, ‘bhikkhu ariyo sammaddaso acchecchi[111], taṇhaṁ, vivattayi[112] saṁyojanaṁ, sammā mānābhisamayā antamakāsi dukkhassā’”ti. Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati—

346 [113] “Yo sukhaṁ dukkhato adda[114],
Dukkhamaddakkhi sallato;
Adukkhamasukhaṁ santaṁ,
Adakkhi naṁ aniccato.

347 Sa ve sammaddaso bhikkhu,
yato tattha vimuccati;
Abhiññāvosito santo,
sa ve yogātigo munī”ti.

348 Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

349 Catutthaṁ.

  1. acchecchi: acchejji (pā1), acchijji (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. vivattayi: vāvattayi (sī), vivaṭṭayi (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. 13S4:1114; 19Th1:1336 (Cross Reference) ↩
  4. adda: ddakkhi (sī), addakkhi (syā1-3), dakkhi (pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
