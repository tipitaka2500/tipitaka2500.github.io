3.4.10 Devadattasutta

3.4.10. เทวทัต์ตสุต์ต

Tikanipāta

Catutthavagga

Devadattasutta

89. 622 Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ—

623 [187] “Tīhi, bhikkhave, asaddhammehi abhibhūto pariyādinnacitto devadatto āpāyiko nerayiko kappaṭṭho atekiccho. Katamehi tīhi? Pāpicchatāya, bhikkhave, abhibhūto pariyādinnacitto devadatto āpāyiko nerayiko kappaṭṭho atekiccho. Pāpamittatāya, bhikkhave, abhibhūto pariyādinnacitto devadatto āpāyiko nerayiko kappaṭṭho atekiccho. Sati kho pana uttarikaraṇīye[188] oramattakena visesādhigamena[189] antarā vosānaṁ āpādi. Imehi kho, bhikkhave, tīhi asaddhammehi abhibhūto pariyādinnacitto devadatto āpāyiko nerayiko kappaṭṭho atekiccho”ti. Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati—

624 “Mā jātu koci lokasmiṁ,
pāpiccho udapajjatha;
Tadamināpi jānātha,
pāpicchānaṁ yathā gati.

625 Paṇḍitoti samaññāto,
bhāvitattoti sammato;
Jalaṁva yasasā aṭṭhā,
devadattoti vissuto[190].

626 So samānamanuciṇṇo[191],
āsajja naṁ tathāgataṁ;
Avīcinirayaṁ patto,
catudvāraṁ bhayānakaṁ.

627 Aduṭṭhassa hi yo dubbhe,
pāpakammaṁ akubbato;
Tameva pāpaṁ phusati[192],
duṭṭhacittaṁ anādaraṁ.

628 Samuddaṁ visakumbhena,
yo maññeyya padūsituṁ;
Na so tena padūseyya,
bhesmā hi udadhi mahā.

629 Evamevaṁ tathāgataṁ,
yo vādena vihiṁsati;
Sammaggataṁ santacittaṁ,
vādo tamhi na rūhati. [193] [194]

630 Tādisaṁ mittaṁ kubbetha,
tañca seveyya paṇḍito;
Yassa maggānugo bhikkhu,
khayaṁ dukkhassa pāpuṇe”ti.

631 Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

632 Dasamaṁ.

633 Catuttho vaggo.

634

635 Vitakkāsakkārasadda,
cavanaloke asubhaṁ;
Dhammaandhakāramalaṁ,
devadattena te dasāti.
