12.2.2 Arūpāvacarakusala

12.2.2. อรูปาวจรกุสล

Jhānavibhaṅga

Abhidhammabhājanīya

Arūpāvacarakusala

626. 1880 Idha bhikkhu yasmiṁ samaye arūpūpapattiyā maggaṁ bhāveti sabbaso ākiñcaññāyatanaṁ samatikkamma nevasaññānāsaññāyatanasaññāsahagataṁ sukhassa ca pahānā…pe… catutthaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharati, tasmiṁ samaye duvaṅgikaṁ jhānaṁ hoti— upekkhā, cittassekaggatā. Idaṁ vuccati “catutthaṁ jhānaṁ”. Avasesā dhammā jhānasampayuttā.
