16.2.6 Chakkaniddesa

16.2.6. ฉัก์กนิท์เทส

Ñāṇavibhaṅga

Niddesa

Chakkaniddesa

805. 2547 Tattha katamā chasu abhiññāsu paññā? Iddhividhe ñāṇaṁ, sotadhātuvisuddhiyā ñāṇaṁ, paracitte ñāṇaṁ, pubbenivāsānussatiyā ñāṇaṁ, sattānaṁ cutūpapāte ñāṇaṁ, āsavānaṁ khaye ñāṇaṁ— imā chasu abhiññāsu paññā. Evaṁ chabbidhena ñāṇavatthu.

2548 Chakkaṁ.
