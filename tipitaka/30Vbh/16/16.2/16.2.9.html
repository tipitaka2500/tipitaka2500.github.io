16.2.9 Navakaniddesa

16.2.9. นวกนิท์เทส

Ñāṇavibhaṅga

Niddesa

Navakaniddesa

808. 2553 Tattha katamā navasu anupubbavihārasamāpattīsu paññā? Paṭhamajjhānasamāpattiyā paññā, dutiyajjhānasamāpattiyā paññā, tatiyajjhānasamāpattiyā paññā, catutthajjhānasamāpattiyā paññā, ākāsānañcāyatanasamāpattiyā paññā, viññāṇañcāyatanasamāpattiyā paññā, ākiñcaññāyatanasamāpattiyā paññā, nevasaññānāsaññāyatanasamāpattiyā paññā, saññāvedayitanirodhasamāpattiyā vuṭṭhitassa paccavekkhaṇāñāṇaṁ— imā navasu anupubbavihārasamāpattīsu paññā. Evaṁ navavidhena ñāṇavatthu.

2554 Navakaṁ.
