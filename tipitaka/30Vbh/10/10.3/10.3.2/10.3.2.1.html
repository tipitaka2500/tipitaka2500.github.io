10.3.2.1 Hetugocchaka

10.3.2.1. เหตุโคจ์ฉก

Bojjhaṅgavibhaṅga

Pañhāpucchaka

Duka

Hetugocchaka

485. 1618 Dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo hetu, cha bojjhaṅgā hetusampayuttā. Dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo hetu ceva sahetuko ca, cha bojjhaṅgā na vattabbā “hetū ceva sahetukā cā”ti, sahetukā ceva na ca hetū. Dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo hetu ceva hetusampayutto ca, cha bojjhaṅgā na vattabbā “hetū ceva hetusampayuttā cā”ti, hetusampayuttā ceva na ca hetū. Cha bojjhaṅgā na hetū sahetukā, dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo na vattabbo “na hetusahetuko”tipi, “na hetuahetuko”tipi.
