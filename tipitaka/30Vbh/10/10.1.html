10.1 Suttantabhājanīya

10.1. สุต์ตัน์ตภาชนีย

Bojjhaṅgavibhaṅga

Suttantabhājanīya

466. 1555 Satta bojjhaṅgā— satisambojjhaṅgo, dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo, vīriyasambojjhaṅgo, pītisambojjhaṅgo, passaddhisambojjhaṅgo, samādhisambojjhaṅgo, upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo.

467. 1556 Tattha katamo satisambojjhaṅgo? Idha bhikkhu satimā hoti paramena satinepakkena samannāgato, cirakatampi cirabhāsitampi saritā hoti anussaritā— ayaṁ vuccati “satisambojjhaṅgo”. (1)

1557 So tathā sato viharanto taṁ dhammaṁ paññāya pavicinati pavicarati parivīmaṁsamāpajjati— ayaṁ vuccati “dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo”. (2)

1558 Tassa taṁ dhammaṁ paññāya pavicinato pavicarato parivīmaṁsamāpajjato āraddhaṁ hoti vīriyaṁ asallīnaṁ— ayaṁ vuccati “vīriyasambojjhaṅgo”. (3)

1559 Āraddhavīriyassa uppajjati pīti nirāmisā— ayaṁ vuccati “pītisambojjhaṅgo”. (4)

1560 Pītimanassa kāyopi passambhati, cittampi passambhati— ayaṁ vuccati “passaddhisambojjhaṅgo”. (5)

1561 Passaddhakāyassa sukhino cittaṁ samādhiyati— ayaṁ vuccati “samādhisambojjhaṅgo”. (6)

1562 So tathā samāhitaṁ cittaṁ sādhukaṁ ajjhupekkhitā hoti— ayaṁ vuccati “upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo”. (7)

468. 1563 Satta bojjhaṅgā— satisambojjhaṅgo, dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo, vīriyasambojjhaṅgo, pītisambojjhaṅgo, passaddhisambojjhaṅgo, samādhisambojjhaṅgo, upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo.

469. 1564 Tattha katamo satisambojjhaṅgo? Atthi ajjhattaṁ dhammesu sati, atthi bahiddhā dhammesu sati. Yadapi ajjhattaṁ dhammesu sati tadapi satisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati. Yadapi bahiddhā dhammesu sati tadapi satisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati. (1)

1565 Tattha katamo dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo? Atthi ajjhattaṁ dhammesu pavicayo, atthi bahiddhā dhammesu pavicayo. Yadapi ajjhattaṁ dhammesu pavicayo tadapi dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati. Yadapi bahiddhā dhammesu pavicayo tadapi dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati. (2)

1566 Tattha katamo vīriyasambojjhaṅgo? Atthi kāyikaṁ vīriyaṁ, atthi cetasikaṁ vīriyaṁ. Yadapi kāyikaṁ vīriyaṁ tadapi vīriyasambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati. Yadapi cetasikaṁ vīriyaṁ tadapi vīriyasambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati. (3)

1567 Tattha katamo pītisambojjhaṅgo? Atthi savitakkasavicārā pīti, atthi avitakkaavicārā pīti. Yadapi savitakkasavicārā pīti tadapi pītisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati. Yadapi avitakkaavicārā pīti tadapi pītisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati. (4)

1568 Tattha katamo passaddhisambojjhaṅgo? Atthi kāyapassaddhi[66], atthi cittapassaddhi[67]. Yadapi kāyapassaddhi tadapi passaddhisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati. Yadapi cittapassaddhi tadapi passaddhisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati. (5)

1569 Tattha katamo samādhisambojjhaṅgo? Atthi savitakko savicāro[68] samādhi, atthi avitakko avicāro[69] samādhi. Yadapi savitakko savicāro samādhi tadapi samādhisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati. Yadapi avitakko avicāro samādhi tadapi samādhisambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati. (6)

1570 Tattha katamo upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo? Atthi ajjhattaṁ dhammesu upekkhā, atthi bahiddhā dhammesu upekkhā. Yadapi ajjhattaṁ dhammesu upekkhā tadapi upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati. Yadapi bahiddhā dhammesu upekkhā tadapi upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattati. (7)

470. 1571 Satta bojjhaṅgā— satisambojjhaṅgo, dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgo, vīriyasambojjhaṅgo, pītisambojjhaṅgo, passaddhisambojjhaṅgo, samādhisambojjhaṅgo, upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo.

471. 1572 Tattha katamo satisambojjhaṅgo? Idha bhikkhu satisambojjhaṅgaṁ bhāveti vivekanissitaṁ virāganissitaṁ nirodhanissitaṁ vossaggapariṇāmiṁ, dhammavicayasambojjhaṅgaṁ bhāveti…pe… vīriyasambojjhaṅgaṁ bhāveti… pītisambojjhaṅgaṁ bhāveti… passaddhisambojjhaṅgaṁ bhāveti… samādhisambojjhaṅgaṁ bhāveti… upekkhāsambojjhaṅgaṁ bhāveti vivekanissitaṁ virāganissitaṁ nirodhanissitaṁ vossaggapariṇāmiṁ.

1573 Suttantabhājanīyaṁ.
