Paṭiccasamuppādavibhaṅga

Abhidhammabhājanīya

Kusalaniddesa

Rūpāvacarakusalacitta

300. 1212 Katame dhammā kusalā? Yasmiṁ samaye rūpūpapattiyā maggaṁ bhāveti vivicceva kāmehi…pe… paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ[45] upasampajja viharati pathavīkasiṇaṁ[46], tasmiṁ samaye kusalamūlapaccayā saṅkhāro, saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇaṁ, viññāṇapaccayā nāmaṁ, nāmapaccayā chaṭṭhāyatanaṁ, chaṭṭhāyatanapaccayā phasso, phassapaccayā vedanā, vedanāpaccayā pasādo, pasādapaccayā adhimokkho, adhimokkhapaccayā bhavo, bhavapaccayā jāti, jātipaccayā jarāmaraṇaṁ. Evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hoti.

301. 1213 Tattha katame kusalamūlā? Alobho, adoso, amoho— ime vuccanti “kusalamūlā”.

1214 Tattha katamo kusalamūlapaccayā saṅkhāro? Yā cetanā sañcetanā sañcetayitattaṁ— ayaṁ vuccati “kusalamūlapaccayā saṅkhāro” …pe… tena vuccati “evametassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hotī”ti.

  1. jhānaṁ: paṭhamajjhānaṁ (ka-sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. pathavīkasiṇaṁ: paṭhavīkasiṇaṁ (sī, syā1-3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
