1095. 1764 Katame dhammā sappaṭighā? Cakkhāyatanaṁ…pe… phoṭṭhabbāyatanaṁ— ime dhammā sappaṭighā.

1096. 1765 Katame dhammā appaṭighā? Vedanākkhandho…pe… viññāṇakkhandho, yañca rūpaṁ anidassanaṁ appaṭighaṁ dhammāyatanapariyāpannaṁ, asaṅkhatā ca dhātu— ime dhammā appaṭighā.
