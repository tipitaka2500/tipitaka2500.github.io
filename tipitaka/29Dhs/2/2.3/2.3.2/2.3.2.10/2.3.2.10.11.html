Mahantaraduka

Cittasaṃsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānānuparivattiduka

1211. 1907 Katame dhammā cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānānuparivattino? Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho— ime dhammā cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānānuparivattino.

1212. 1908 Katame dhammā no cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānānuparivattino? Cittañca, sabbañca rūpaṁ, asaṅkhatā ca dhātu— ime dhammā no cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānānuparivattino.
