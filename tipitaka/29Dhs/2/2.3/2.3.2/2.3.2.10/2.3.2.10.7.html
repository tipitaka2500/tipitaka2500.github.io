2.3.2.10.7. จิต์ตสหภูทุก

Niddesa

Nikkhepakaṇḍa

Dukanikkhepa

Mahantaraduka

Cittasahabhūduka

1203. 1899 Katame dhammā cittasahabhuno? Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho, kāyaviññatti, vacīviññatti— ime dhammā cittasahabhuno.

1204. 1900 Katame dhammā no cittasahabhuno? Cittañca, avasesañca rūpaṁ, asaṅkhatā ca dhātu— ime dhammā no cittasahabhuno.
