2.3.3.12. สัส์สตทิฏ์ฐิทุก

Niddesa

Nikkhepakaṇḍa

Suttantikadukanikkhepa

Sassatadiṭṭhiduka

1322. 2027 Tattha katamā sassatadiṭṭhi? Sassato attā ca loko cāti, yā evarūpā diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṁ…pe… vipariyāsaggāho— ayaṁ vuccati sassatadiṭṭhi.

1323. 2028 Tattha katamā ucchedadiṭṭhi? Ucchijjissati attā ca loko cāti, yā evarūpā diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṁ…pe… vipariyāsaggāho— ayaṁ vuccati ucchedadiṭṭhi.
