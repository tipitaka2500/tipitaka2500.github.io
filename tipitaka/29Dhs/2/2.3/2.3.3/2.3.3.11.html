Suttantikadukanikkhepa

Bhavadiṭṭhiduka

1320. 2025 Tattha katamā bhavadiṭṭhi? Bhavissati attā ca loko cāti, yā evarūpā diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṁ…pe… vipariyāsaggāho— ayaṁ vuccati bhavadiṭṭhi.

1321. 2026 Tattha katamā vibhavadiṭṭhi? Na bhavissati attā ca loko cāti, yā evarūpā diṭṭhi diṭṭhigataṁ…pe… vipariyāsaggāho— ayaṁ vuccati vibhavadiṭṭhi.
