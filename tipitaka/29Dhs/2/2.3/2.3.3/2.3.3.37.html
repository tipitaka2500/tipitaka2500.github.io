2.3.3.37 Sīlavisuddhiduka

2.3.3.37. สีลวิสุท์ธิทุก

Niddesa

Nikkhepakaṇḍa

Suttantikadukanikkhepa

Sīlavisuddhiduka

1372. 2077 Tattha katamā sīlavisuddhi? Yo kāyiko avītikkamo, vācasiko avītikkamo, kāyikavācasiko avītikkamo— ayaṁ vuccati sīlavisuddhi. Sabbopi sīlasaṁvaro sīlavisuddhi.

1373. 2078 Tattha katamā diṭṭhivisuddhi? Kammassakatañāṇaṁ[125] saccānulomikañāṇaṁ[126] maggasamaṅgissa ñāṇaṁ phalasamaṅgissa ñāṇaṁ.

  1. Kammassakatañāṇaṁ: kammassakatāñāṇaṁ (sī), kammassakataṁ ñāṇaṁ (syā1), kammassa kataṁ ñāṇa (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. saccānulomikañāṇaṁ: saccānulomikaṁ ñāṇaṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
