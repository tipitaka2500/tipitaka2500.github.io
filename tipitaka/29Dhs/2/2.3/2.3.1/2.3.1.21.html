2.3.1.21 Ajjhattārammaṇattika

2.3.1.21. อัช์ฌัต์ตารัม์มณัต์ติก

Niddesa

Nikkhepakaṇḍa

Tikanikkhepa

Ajjhattārammaṇattika

1053. 1718 Katame dhammā ajjhattārammaṇā? Ajjhatte dhamme ārabbha ye uppajjanti cittacetasikā dhammā— ime dhammā ajjhattārammaṇā.

1054. 1719 Katame dhammā bahiddhārammaṇā? Bahiddhā dhamme ārabbha ye uppajjanti cittacetasikā dhammā— ime dhammā bahiddhārammaṇā.

1055. 1720 Katame dhammā ajjhattabahiddhārammaṇā? Ajjhattabahiddhā dhamme ārabbha ye uppajjanti cittacetasikā dhammā— ime dhammā ajjhattabahiddhārammaṇā.
