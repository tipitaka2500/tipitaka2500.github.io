Mahantaraduka

Cittasaṃsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānānuparivattiduka

1545. 2256 Katame dhammā cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānānuparivattino? Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho— ime dhammā cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānānuparivattino.

1546. 2257 Katame dhammā no cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānānuparivattino? Cittañca, rūpañca, nibbānañca— ime dhammā no cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānānuparivattino.
