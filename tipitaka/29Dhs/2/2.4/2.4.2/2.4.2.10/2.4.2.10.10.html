Mahantaraduka

Cittasaṃsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānasahabhūduka

1543. 2254 Katame dhammā cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānasahabhuno? Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho— ime dhammā cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānasahabhuno.

1544. 2255 Katame dhammā no cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānasahabhuno? Cittañca, rūpañca, nibbānañca— ime dhammā no cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānasahabhuno.
