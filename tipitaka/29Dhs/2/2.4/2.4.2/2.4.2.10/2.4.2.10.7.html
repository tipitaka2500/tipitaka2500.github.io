2.4.2.10.7. จิต์ตสหภูทุก

Dukaatthuddhāra

Mahantaraduka

Cittasahabhūduka

1537. 2248 Katame dhammā cittasahabhuno? Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho, kāyaviññatti, vacīviññatti— ime dhammā cittasahabhuno.

1538. 2249 Katame dhammā no cittasahabhuno? Cittañca, avasesañca rūpaṁ, nibbānañca— ime dhammā no cittasahabhuno.
