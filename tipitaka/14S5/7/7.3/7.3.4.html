7.3.4. สุท์ธิกสุต์ต

Iddhipādasaṃyutta

Ayoguḷavagga

Suddhikasutta

836. 1481 [332] “Cattārome, bhikkhave, iddhipādā. Katame cattāro? Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu chandasamādhippadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṁ iddhipādaṁ bhāveti, vīriyasamādhi…pe… cittasamādhi… vīmaṁsāsamādhippadhānasaṅkhārasamannāgataṁ iddhipādaṁ bhāveti— ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro iddhipādā”ti.
