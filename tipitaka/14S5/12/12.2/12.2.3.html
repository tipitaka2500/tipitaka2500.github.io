12.2.3 Khandhasutta

12.2.3. ขัน์ธสุต์ต

Saccasaṃyutta

Dhammacakkappavattanavagga

Khandhasutta

1083. 2226 “Cattārimāni, bhikkhave, ariyasaccāni. Katamāni cattāri? Dukkhaṁ ariyasaccaṁ[526], dukkhasamudayaṁ ariyasaccaṁ, dukkhanirodhaṁ ariyasaccaṁ dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā ariyasaccaṁ.

2227 Katamañca, bhikkhave, dukkhaṁ ariyasaccaṁ? ‘Pañcupādānakkhandhā’ tissa vacanīyaṁ, seyyathidaṁ[527]— rūpupādānakkhandho…pe… viññāṇupādānakkhandho. Idaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, dukkhaṁ ariyasaccaṁ.

2228 Katamañca, bhikkhave, dukkhasamudayaṁ ariyasaccaṁ? Yāyaṁ taṇhā ponobbhavikā nandirāgasahagatā tatratatrābhinandinī, seyyathidaṁ— kāmataṇhā, bhavataṇhā, vibhavataṇhā. Idaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, dukkhasamudayaṁ ariyasaccaṁ.

2229 Katamañca, bhikkhave, dukkhanirodhaṁ ariyasaccaṁ? Yo tassāyeva taṇhāya asesavirāganirodho cāgo paṭinissaggo mutti anālayo— idaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, dukkhanirodhaṁ ariyasaccaṁ.

2230 Katamañca, bhikkhave, dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā ariyasaccaṁ? Ayameva ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo, seyyathidaṁ— sammādiṭṭhi…pe… sammāsamādhi— idaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā ariyasaccaṁ. Imāni kho, bhikkhave, cattāri ariyasaccāni.

2231 Tasmātiha, bhikkhave, ‘idaṁ dukkhan’ti yogo karaṇīyo…pe… ‘ayaṁ dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā’ti yogo karaṇīyo”ti.

2232 Tatiyaṁ.

  1. ariyasaccaṁ: dukkhasamudayo ariyasaccaṁ dukkhanirodho ariyasaccaṁ (sī, syā1-3) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. seyyathidaṁ: katame pañca? seyyathīdaṁ (sī), katame pañca (syā1-3, kaṃ), seyyathīdaṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
