12.4.7 Paṭhamasūriyasutta

12.4.7. ปมสูริยสุต์ต

Saccasaṃyutta

Sīsapāvanavagga

Paṭhamasūriyasutta

1107. 2334 [552] “Sūriyassa, bhikkhave, udayato etaṁ pubbaṅgamaṁ etaṁ pubbanimittaṁ, yadidaṁ— aruṇuggaṁ. Evameva kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno catunnaṁ ariyasaccānaṁ yathābhūtaṁ abhisamayāya etaṁ pubbaṅgamaṁ etaṁ pubbanimittaṁ, yadidaṁ— sammādiṭṭhi. Tassetaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno pāṭikaṅkhaṁ— ‘idaṁ dukkhan’ti yathābhūtaṁ pajānissati…pe… ‘ayaṁ dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā’ti yathābhūtaṁ pajānissati. [553]

2335 Tasmātiha, bhikkhave, ‘idaṁ dukkhan’ti yogo karaṇīyo…pe… ‘ayaṁ dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā’ti yogo karaṇīyo”ti.

2336 Sattamaṁ.
