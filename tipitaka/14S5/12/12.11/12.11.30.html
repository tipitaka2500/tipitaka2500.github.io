12.11.30 Pettidevapettivisayasutta

12.11.13. เปต์ติเทวเปต์ติวิสยสุต์ต

Saccasaṃyutta

Pañcagatipeyyālavagga

Pettidevapettivisayasutta

1201. 2545 … “Evameva kho, bhikkhave, appakā te sattā ye pettivisayā cutā devesu paccājāyanti; atha kho eteva bahutarā sattā ye pettivisayā cutā pettivisaye paccājāyanti. Taṁ kissa hetu? Adiṭṭhattā, bhikkhave, catunnaṁ ariyasaccānaṁ. Katamesaṁ catunnaṁ? Dukkhassa ariyasaccassa, dukkhasamudayassa ariyasaccassa, dukkhanirodhassa ariyasaccassa, dukkhanirodhagāminiyā paṭipadāya ariyasaccassa.

2546 Tasmātiha, bhikkhave, ‘idaṁ dukkhan’ti yogo karaṇīyo, ‘ayaṁ dukkhasamudayo’ti yogo karaṇīyo, ‘ayaṁ dukkhanirodho’ti yogo karaṇīyo, ‘ayaṁ dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā’ti yogo karaṇīyo”ti.

2547 Idamavoca bhagavā. Attamanā te bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti.

2548 Tiṁsatimaṁ.

2549 Pañcagatipeyyālavaggo[645] ekādasamo.

2550

2551 Manussato cutā chāpi,
devā cutā nirayato;
Tiracchānapettivisayā,
tiṁsamatto gativaggoti.

2552 Saccasaṁyuttaṁ dvādasamaṁ.

2553 Mahāvaggo pañcamo.

2554

2555 Maggabojjhaṅgaṁ satiyā,
indriyaṁ sammappadhānaṁ;
Baliddhipādānuruddhā,
jhānānāpānasaṁyutaṁ;
Sotāpatti saccañcāti,
mahāvaggoti vuccatīti.

2556 Mahāvaggasaṁyuttapāḷi[646] niṭṭhitā.

2557 Saṁyuttanikāyo samatto.

  2. Mahāvaggasaṁyuttapāḷi: mahāvārasaṁyuttaṁ (syā1-3) (Word Variant) ↩
