12.6.5 Paṭhamamahāpathavīsutta

12.6.5. ปฐมมหาปถวีสุต์ต

Saccasaṃyutta

Abhisamayavagga

Paṭhamamahāpathavīsutta

1125. 2417 [590] [589] “Seyyathāpi, bhikkhave, puriso mahāpathaviyā[591] satta kolaṭṭhimattiyo guḷikā upanikkhipeyya. Taṁ kiṁ maññatha, bhikkhave, katamaṁ nu kho bahutaraṁ— yā vā satta kolaṭṭhimattiyo guḷikā upanikkhittā, ayaṁ vā mahāpathavī”ti? “Etadeva, bhante, bahutaraṁ, yadidaṁ— mahāpathavī; appamattikā satta kolaṭṭhimattiyo guḷikā upanikkhittā. Saṅkhampi na upenti, upanidhampi na upenti, kalabhāgampi na upenti mahāpathaviṁ upanidhāya satta kolaṭṭhimattiyo guḷikā upanikkhittā”ti. “Evameva kho, bhikkhave, ariyasāvakassa…pe… yogo karaṇīyo”ti.
