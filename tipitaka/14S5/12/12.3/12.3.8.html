12.3.8 Lokasutta

12.3.8. โลกสุต์ต

Saccasaṃyutta

Koṭigāmavagga

Lokasutta

1098. 2295 “Cattārimāni, bhikkhave, ariyasaccāni. Katamāni cattāri? Dukkhaṁ ariyasaccaṁ, dukkhasamudayaṁ ariyasaccaṁ, dukkhanirodhaṁ ariyasaccaṁ, dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā ariyasaccaṁ. Sadevake loke samārake sabrahmake sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiyā pajāya sadevamanussāya tathāgato ariyo; tasmā ‘ariyasaccānī’ti vuccanti.

2296 Tasmātiha, bhikkhave, ‘idaṁ dukkhan’ti yogo karaṇīyo…pe… ‘ayaṁ dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā’ti yogo karaṇīyo”ti.

2297 Aṭṭhamaṁ.
