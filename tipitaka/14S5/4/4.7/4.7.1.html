4.7.1 Saṃyojanasutta

4.7.1. สํโยชนสุต์ต

Indriyasaṃyutta

Bodhipakkhiyavagga

Saṃyojanasutta

531. 1229 [281] Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, indriyāni bhāvitāni bahulīkatāni saṁyojanappahānāya[282] saṁvattanti. Katamāni pañca? Saddhindriyaṁ…pe… paññindriyaṁ— imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañcindriyāni bhāvitāni bahulīkatāni saṁyojanappahānāya saṁvattantī”ti.

1230 Paṭhamaṁ.

  1. sambodhivaggo (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. saṁyojanappahānāya: saññojanānaṁ pahānāya (syā1-3, pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
