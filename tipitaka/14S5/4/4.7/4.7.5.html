4.7.5. ปมผลสุต์ต

Indriyasaṃyutta

Bodhipakkhiyavagga

Paṭhamaphalasutta

535. 1238 [284] “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, indriyāni. Katamāni pañca? Saddhindriyaṁ…pe… paññindriyaṁ— imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañcindriyāni. Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, pañcannaṁ indriyānaṁ bhāvitattā bahulīkatattā dvinnaṁ phalānaṁ aññataraṁ phalaṁ pāṭikaṅkhaṁ— diṭṭheva dhamme aññā, sati vā upādisese anāgāmitā”ti.
