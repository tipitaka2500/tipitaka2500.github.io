4.2.10 Āsavakkhayasutta

4.2.10. อาสวัก์ขยสุต์ต

Indriyasaṃyutta

Mudutaravagga

Āsavakkhayasutta

490. 1056 [229] “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, indriyāni. Katamāni pañca? Saddhindriyaṁ…pe… paññindriyaṁ— imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañcindriyāni. Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, pañcannaṁ indriyānaṁ bhāvitattā bahulīkatattā bhikkhu āsavānaṁ khayā anāsavaṁ cetovimuttiṁ paññāvimuttiṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja viharatī”ti.

1057 Dasamaṁ.

1058 Mudutaravaggo dutiyo.

1059

1060 Paṭilābho tayo saṁkhittā,
vitthārā apare tayo;
Paṭipanno ca sampanno[230],
dasamaṁ āsavakkhayanti.

  1. āsavānaṁ khayo (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. sampanno: paṭipanno cupasamo (syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
