4.3.1 Punabbhavasutta

4.3.1. ปุนัพ์ภวสุต์ต

Indriyasaṃyutta

Chaḷindriyavagga

Punabbhavasutta

491. 1061 [231] “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, indriyāni. Katamāni pañca? Saddhindriyaṁ…pe… paññindriyaṁ. Yāvakīvañcāhaṁ[232], bhikkhave, imesaṁ pañcannaṁ indriyānaṁ samudayañca atthaṅgamañca assādañca ādīnavañca nissaraṇañca yathābhūtaṁ nābbhaññāsiṁ, neva tāvāhaṁ, bhikkhave, sadevake loke samārake sabrahmake sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiyā pajāya sadevamanussāya ‘anuttaraṁ sammāsambodhiṁ abhisambuddho’ti paccaññāsiṁ[233]. Yato ca khvāhaṁ, bhikkhave, imesaṁ pañcannaṁ indriyānaṁ samudayañca atthaṅgamañca assādañca ādīnavañca nissaraṇañca yathābhūtaṁ abbhaññāsiṁ, athāhaṁ, bhikkhave, sadevake loke samārake sabrahmake sassamaṇabrāhmaṇiyā pajāya sadevamanussāya ‘anuttaraṁ sammāsambodhiṁ abhisambuddho’ti paccaññāsiṁ. Ñāṇañca pana me dassanaṁ udapādi— ‘akuppā me vimutti[234], ayamantimā jāti, natthi dāni punabbhavo’”ti.

1062 Paṭhamaṁ.

  1. nabbhavo or ñāṇavā (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Yāvakīvañcāhaṁ: yāvakīvāhaṁ (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. paccaññāsiṁ: abhisambuddho paccaññāsiṁ (sī, syā1-3, kaṃ) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. vimutti: cetovimutti (sī, pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
