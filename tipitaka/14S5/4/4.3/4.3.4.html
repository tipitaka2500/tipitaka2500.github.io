4.3.4. เอกพีชีสุต์ต

Indriyasaṃyutta

Chaḷindriyavagga

Ekabījīsutta

494. 1067 [238] “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, indriyāni. Katamāni pañca? Saddhindriyaṁ…pe… paññindriyaṁ— imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañcindriyāni. Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, pañcannaṁ indriyānaṁ samattā paripūrattā arahaṁ hoti, tato mudutarehi antarāparinibbāyī hoti, tato mudutarehi upahaccaparinibbāyī hoti, tato mudutarehi asaṅkhāraparinibbāyī hoti, tato mudutarehi sasaṅkhāraparinibbāyī hoti, tato mudutarehi uddhaṁsoto hoti akaniṭṭhagāmī, tato mudutarehi sakadāgāmī hoti, tato mudutarehi ekabījī[239] hoti, tato mudutarehi kolaṁkolo hoti, tato mudutarehi sattakkhattuparamo[240] hoti, tato mudutarehi dhammānusārī hoti, tato mudutarehi saddhānusārī hotī”ti.

1068 Catutthaṁ.

  1. ekābhiññaṁ (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. ekabījī: ekabīji (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. sattakkhattuparamo: sattakkhattuṁparamo (syā1-3) (Word Variant) ↩
