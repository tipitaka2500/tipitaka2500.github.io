4.1.4. ปฐมอรหัน์ตสุต์ต

Indriyasaṃyutta

Suddhikavagga

Paṭhamaarahantasutta

474. 1006 [218] “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, indriyāni. Katamāni pañca? Saddhindriyaṁ, vīriyindriyaṁ, satindriyaṁ, samādhindriyaṁ, paññindriyaṁ. Yato kho, bhikkhave, ariyasāvako imesaṁ pañcannaṁ indriyānaṁ assādañca[219] ādīnavañca nissaraṇañca yathābhūtaṁ viditvā anupādāvimutto hoti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, bhikkhu arahaṁ khīṇāsavo vusitavā katakaraṇīyo ohitabhāro anuppattasadattho parikkhīṇabhavasaṁyojano sammadaññāvimutto”ti.

  2. assādañca: samudayañca atthaṅgamañca assādañca (syā1-3, kaṃ, ka-ma) 12S2:1070 (Word Variant) ↩
