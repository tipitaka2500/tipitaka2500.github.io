1.14.1 Oghasutta

1.14.1. โอฆสุต์ต

Maggasaṃyutta

Oghavagga

Oghasutta

172. 335 Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Cattārome, bhikkhave, oghā. Katame cattāro? Kāmogho, bhavogho, diṭṭhogho, avijjogho— ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro oghā. Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, catunnaṁ oghānaṁ abhiññāya pariññāya parikkhayāya pahānāya…pe… ayaṁ ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo bhāvetabbo”ti. (Yathā esanā, evaṁ vitthāretabbaṁ.)

336 Paṭhamaṁ.
