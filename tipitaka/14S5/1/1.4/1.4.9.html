1.4.9 Paṭhamabrahmacariyasutta

1.4.9. ปมพ๎รห๎มจริยสุต์ต

Maggasaṃyutta

Paṭipattivagga

Paṭhamabrahmacariyasutta

39. 139 Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Brahmacariyañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi, brahmacariyaphalāni ca. Taṁ suṇātha. Katamañca, bhikkhave, brahmacariyaṁ? Ayameva ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo, seyyathidaṁ— sammādiṭṭhi…pe… sammāsamādhi. Idaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, brahmacariyaṁ. Katamāni ca, bhikkhave, brahmacariyaphalāni? Sotāpattiphalaṁ, sakadāgāmiphalaṁ, anāgāmiphalaṁ, arahattaphalaṁ— imāni vuccanti, bhikkhave, brahmacariyaphalānī”ti.

140 Navamaṁ.
