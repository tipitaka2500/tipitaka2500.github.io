1.3.6 Dutiyaasappurisasutta

1.3.6. ทุติยอสัป์ปุริสสุต์ต

Maggasaṃyutta

Micchattavagga

Dutiyaasappurisasutta

26. 101 Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Asappurisañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi, asappurisena asappurisatarañca. Sappurisañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi sappurisena sappurisatarañca. Taṁ suṇātha. Katamo ca, bhikkhave, asappuriso? Idha, bhikkhave, ekacco micchādiṭṭhiko hoti…pe… micchāsamādhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, asappuriso.

102 Katamo ca, bhikkhave, asappurisena asappurisataro? Idha, bhikkhave, ekacco micchādiṭṭhiko hoti…pe… micchāsamādhi, micchāñāṇī, micchāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, asappurisena asappurisataro.

103 Katamo ca, bhikkhave, sappuriso? Idha, bhikkhave, ekacco sammādiṭṭhiko hoti…pe… sammāsamādhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sappuriso.

104 Katamo ca, bhikkhave, sappurisena sappurisataro? Idha, bhikkhave, ekacco sammādiṭṭhiko hoti…pe… sammāsamādhi, sammāñāṇī, sammāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sappurisena sappurisataro”ti.

105 Chaṭṭhaṁ.
