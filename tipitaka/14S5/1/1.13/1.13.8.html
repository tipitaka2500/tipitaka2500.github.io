1.13.8. นีฆสุต์ต

Maggasaṃyutta

Esanāvagga

Nīghasutta

168. 324 “Tayome, bhikkhave, nīghā. Katame tayo? Rāgo nīgho, doso nīgho, moho nīgho— ime kho, bhikkhave, tayo nīghā. Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, tiṇṇannaṁ nīghānaṁ abhiññāya pariññāya parikkhayāya pahānāya…pe… ayaṁ ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo bhāvetabbo”ti.

325 Aṭṭhamaṁ.
