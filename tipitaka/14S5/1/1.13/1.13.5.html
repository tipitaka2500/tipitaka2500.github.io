1.13.5 Dukkhatāsutta

1.13.5. ทุก์ขตาสุต์ต

Maggasaṃyutta

Esanāvagga

Dukkhatāsutta

165. 318 “Tisso imā, bhikkhave, dukkhatā. Katamā tisso? Dukkhadukkhatā, saṅkhāradukkhatā, vipariṇāmadukkhatā— imā kho, bhikkhave, tisso dukkhatā. Imāsaṁ kho, bhikkhave, tissannaṁ dukkhatānaṁ abhiññāya pariññāya parikkhayāya pahānāya…pe… ayaṁ ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo bhāvetabbo”ti.

319 Pañcamaṁ.
