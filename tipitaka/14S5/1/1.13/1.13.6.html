1.13.6. ขิลสุต์ต

Maggasaṃyutta

Esanāvagga

Khilasutta

166. 320 “Tayome, bhikkhave, khilā. Katame tayo? Rāgo khilo, doso khilo, moho khilo— ime kho, bhikkhave, tayo khilā. Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, tiṇṇannaṁ khilānaṁ abhiññāya pariññāya parikkhayāya pahānāya…pe… ayaṁ ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo bhāvetabbo”ti.

321 Chaṭṭhaṁ.
