2.3.5. อปริหานิยสุต์ต

Bojjhaṅgasaṃyutta

Udāyivagga

Aparihāniyasutta

206. 474 “Satta vo, bhikkhave, aparihāniye dhamme desessāmi; taṁ suṇātha. Katame ca, bhikkhave, satta aparihāniyā dhammā? Yadidaṁ— satta bojjhaṅgā. Katame satta? Satisambojjhaṅgo…pe… upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo— ime kho, bhikkhave, satta aparihāniyā dhammā”ti.

475 Pañcamaṁ.
