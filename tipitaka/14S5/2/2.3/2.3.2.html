Udāyivagga

Bojjhaṅgadesanāsutta

203. 465 [81] “Satta vo, bhikkhave, bojjhaṅge desessāmi; taṁ suṇātha. Katame ca, bhikkhave, satta bojjhaṅgā? Satisambojjhaṅgo…pe… upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo— ime kho, bhikkhave, satta bojjhaṅgā”ti.
