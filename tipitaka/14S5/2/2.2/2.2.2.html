2.2.2 Paṭhamasūriyūpamasutta

2.2.2. ปฐมสูริยูปมสุต์ต

Bojjhaṅgasaṃyutta

Gilānavagga

Paṭhamasūriyūpamasutta

193. 426 [71] “Sūriyassa, bhikkhave, udayato etaṁ pubbaṅgamaṁ etaṁ pubbanimittaṁ, yadidaṁ— aruṇuggaṁ; evameva kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno sattannaṁ bojjhaṅgānaṁ uppādāya etaṁ pubbaṅgamaṁ etaṁ nimittaṁ[72], yadidaṁ— kalyāṇamittatā. Kalyāṇamittassetaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno pāṭikaṅkhaṁ— satta bojjhaṅge bhāvessati, satta bojjhaṅge bahulīkarissati.

427 Kathañca, bhikkhave, bhikkhu kalyāṇamitto satta bojjhaṅge bhāveti satta bojjhaṅge bahulīkaroti? Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu satisambojjhaṅgaṁ bhāveti vivekanissitaṁ…pe… upekkhāsambojjhaṅgaṁ bhāveti vivekanissitaṁ virāganissitaṁ nirodhanissitaṁ vossaggapariṇāmiṁ. Evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhu kalyāṇamitto satta bojjhaṅge bhāveti, satta bojjhaṅge bahulīkarotī”ti.

428 Dutiyaṁ.

  1. suriyasuttaṁ (sī), suriyassa upamā 1 (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. nimittaṁ: pubbanimittaṁ (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
