2.5.3. มารสุต์ต

Bojjhaṅgasaṃyutta

Cakkavattivagga

Mārasutta

224. 536 “Mārasenappamaddanaṁ vo, bhikkhave, maggaṁ desessāmi; taṁ suṇātha. Katamo ca, bhikkhave, mārasenappamaddano maggo? Yadidaṁ— satta bojjhaṅgā. Katame satta? Satisambojjhaṅgo…pe… upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo— ayaṁ kho, bhikkhave, mārasenappamaddano maggo”ti.

537 Tatiyaṁ.
