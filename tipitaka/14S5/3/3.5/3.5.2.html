3.5.2 Samudayasutta

3.5.2. สมุทยสุต์ต

Satipaṭṭhānasaṃyutta

Amatavagga

Samudayasutta

408. 941 “Catunnaṁ, bhikkhave, satipaṭṭhānānaṁ samudayañca atthaṅgamañca desessāmi. Taṁ suṇātha. Ko ca, bhikkhave, kāyassa samudayo? Āhārasamudayā kāyassa samudayo; āhāranirodhā kāyassa atthaṅgamo. Phassasamudayā vedanānaṁ samudayo; phassanirodhā vedanānaṁ atthaṅgamo. Nāmarūpasamudayā cittassa samudayo; nāmarūpanirodhā cittassa atthaṅgamo. Manasikārasamudayā dhammānaṁ samudayo; manasikāranirodhā dhammānaṁ atthaṅgamo”ti.

942 Dutiyaṁ.
