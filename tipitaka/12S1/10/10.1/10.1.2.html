10.1.2 Sakkanāmasutta

10.1.2. สัก์กนามสุต์ต

Yakkhasaṃyutta

Indakavagga

Sakkanāmasutta

236. 1455 [480] Ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā rājagahe viharati gijjhakūṭe pabbate. Atha kho sakkanāmako yakkho yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ gāthāya ajjhabhāsi—

1456 “Sabbaganthappahīnassa,
vippamuttassa te sato;
Samaṇassa na taṁ sādhu,
yadaññamanusāsasī”ti. [481]

1457 “Yena kenaci vaṇṇena,
saṁvāso sakka jāyati;
Na taṁ arahati sappañño,
manasā anukampituṁ.

1458 Manasā ce pasannena,
yadaññamanusāsati;
Na tena hoti saṁyutto,
yānukampā anuddayā”ti. [482]

  1. sakkasuttaṁ (sī, syā1-3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. yadaññamanusāsasī"ti: yadaññamanusāsatīti (sī, syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. yānukampā: sānukampā (sī, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
