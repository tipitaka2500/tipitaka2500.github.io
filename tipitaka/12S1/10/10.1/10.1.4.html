10.1.4 Maṇibhaddasutta

10.1.4. มณิภัท์ทสุต์ต

Yakkhasaṃyutta

Indakavagga

Maṇibhaddasutta

238. 1465 Ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā magadhesu viharati maṇimālike cetiye maṇibhaddassa yakkhassa bhavane. Atha kho maṇibhaddo yakkho yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

1466 “Satīmato sadā bhaddaṁ,
satimā sukhamedhati;
Satīmato suve seyyo,
verā ca parimuccatī”ti.

1467 “Satīmato sadā bhaddaṁ,
satimā sukhamedhati;
Satīmato suve seyyo,
verā na parimuccati.

1468 Yassa sabbamahorattaṁ[488],
ahiṁsāya rato mano;
Mettaṁso sabbabhūtesu,
veraṁ tassa na kenacī”ti.

  1. sabbamahorattaṁ: sabbamahorrattiṁ (syā1-3, kaṃ, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
