11.1.8 Verocanaasurindasutta

11.1.8. เวโรจนอสุริน์ทสุต์ต

Sakkasaṃyutta

Paṭhamavagga

Verocanaasurindasutta

254. 1595 [526] Sāvatthiyaṁ jetavane. Tena kho pana samayena bhagavā divāvihāragato hoti paṭisallīno. Atha kho sakko ca devānamindo verocano ca asurindo yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkamiṁsu; upasaṅkamitvā paccekaṁ dvārabāhaṁ nissāya aṭṭhaṁsu. Atha kho verocano asurindo bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

1596 “Vāyametheva puriso,
yāva atthassa nipphadā;
Nipphannasobhano attho[528],
verocanavaco idan”ti. [527]

1597 “Vāyametheva puriso,
yāva atthassa nipphadā;
Nipphannasobhano attho[529],
khantyā bhiyyo na vijjatī”ti.

1598 “Sabbe sattā atthajātā,
tattha tattha yathārahaṁ;
Saṁyogaparamā tveva,
sambhogā sabbapāṇinaṁ;
Nipphannasobhano attho,
verocanavaco idan”ti.

1599 “Sabbe sattā atthajātā,
tattha tattha yathārahaṁ;
Saṁyogaparamā tveva,
sambhogā sabbapāṇinaṁ;
Nipphannasobhano attho,
khantyā bhiyyo na vijjatī”ti.

  1. virocanaasurindasuttaṁ (syā1-3, pā2), virocana-asurindo (attho) (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Nipphannasobhano: nipphannasobhino (sī, pā2), nippannasobhaṇo (pā1), nipphannasobhaṇo (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. attho: atthā (sī, pā2) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. attho: nipphannasobhino atthā (sī, syā1, kaṃ, pā2), nippannasobhaṇo attho (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
