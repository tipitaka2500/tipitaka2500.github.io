6.1.8 Katamodakatissasutta

6.1.8. กตโมทกติส์สสุต์ต

Brahmasaṃyutta

Paṭhamavagga

Katamodakatissasutta

179. 1072 [362] Sāvatthinidānaṁ. Tena kho pana samayena bhagavā divāvihāragato hoti paṭisallīno. Atha kho subrahmā ca paccekabrahmā suddhāvāso ca paccekabrahmā yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkamiṁsu; upasaṅkamitvā paccekaṁ dvārabāhaṁ nissāya aṭṭhaṁsu. Atha kho suddhāvāso paccekabrahmā katamodakatissakaṁ[363] bhikkhuṁ ārabbha bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

1073 “Appameyyaṁ paminanto,
Kodha vidvā vikappaye;
Appameyyaṁ pamāyinaṁ,
Nivutaṁ taṁ maññe akissavan”ti.

  1. katamorakatissasuttaṁ (sī), tissakasuttaṁ (syā1-3), tissako (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. katamodakatissakaṁ: katamorakatissaṁ (sī), katamorakatissakaṁ (syā1-3, kaṃ) (Word Variant) ↩
