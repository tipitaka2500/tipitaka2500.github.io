8.1.12 Vaṅgīsasutta

8.1.12. วํคีสสุต์ต

Vaṅgīsasaṃyutta

Vaṅgīsavagga

Vaṅgīsasutta

220. 1369 Ekaṁ samayaṁ āyasmā vaṅgīso sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena āyasmā vaṅgīso aciraarahattappatto hutvā[456] vimuttisukhaṁ paṭisaṁvedī[457] tāyaṁ velāyaṁ imā gāthāyo abhāsi—

1370 “Kāveyyamattā vicarimha pubbe,
Gāmā gāmaṁ purā puraṁ;
Athaddasāma sambuddhaṁ,
Saddhā no upapajjatha.

1371 So me dhammamadesesi,
khandhāyatanadhātuyo;
Tassāhaṁ dhammaṁ sutvāna,
pabbajiṁ anagāriyaṁ. [458]

1372 Bahunnaṁ vata atthāya,
bodhiṁ ajjhagamā muni;
Bhikkhūnaṁ bhikkhunīnañca,
ye niyāmagataddasā.

1373 Svāgataṁ vata me āsi,
mama buddhassa santike;
Tisso vijjā anuppattā,
kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ.

1374 Pubbenivāsaṁ jānāmi,
Dibbacakkhuṁ visodhitaṁ;
Tevijjo iddhipattomhi,
Cetopariyāyakovido”ti.

1375

1376 Nikkhantaṁ arati ceva,
pesalā atimaññanā;
Ānandena subhāsitā,
sāriputtapavāraṇā;
Parosahassaṁ koṇḍañño,
moggallānena gaggarā;
Vaṅgīsena dvādasāti.

1377 Vaṅgīsasaṁyuttaṁ samattaṁ.

  1. hutvā: hoti (sī, syā1-3, kaṃ) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. paṭisaṁvedī: vimuttisukhapaṭisaṁvedī (sī, pā2), vimuttisukhapatisamvedī (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. khandhāyatanadhātuyo: khandhe āyatanāni dhātuyo (syā1-3, kaṃ, ka-ma), khandhe āyatānāni dhātuyo ca (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
