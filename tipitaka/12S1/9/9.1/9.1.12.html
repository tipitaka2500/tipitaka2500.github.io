9.1.12 Majjhanhikasutta

9.1.12. มัช์ฌน๎หิกสุต์ต

Vanasaṃyutta

Vanavagga

Majjhanhikasutta

232. 1428 [475] Ekaṁ samayaṁ aññataro bhikkhu kosalesu viharati aññatarasmiṁ vanasaṇḍe. Atha kho tasmiṁ vanasaṇḍe adhivatthā devatā yena so bhikkhu tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā tassa bhikkhuno santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

1429 “Ṭhite majjhanhike[476] kāle,
sannisīvesu pakkhisu;
Saṇateva brahāraññaṁ,
taṁ bhayaṁ paṭibhāti maṁ. [477]

1430 Ṭhite majjhanhike kāle,
sannisīvesu pakkhisu;
Saṇateva brahāraññaṁ,
sā rati paṭibhāti man”ti.

  1. majjhantikasuttaṁ (sī, syā1-3, pā2), majjhantiko (saṇika) (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. majjhanhike: majjhantike (sī, syā1-3, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. sannisīvesu: sannisinnesu (ka-sī, syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
