9.1.2 Upaṭṭhānasutta

9.1.2. อุปัฏ์านสุต์ต

Vanasaṃyutta

Vanavagga

Upaṭṭhānasutta

222. 1383 Ekaṁ samayaṁ aññataro bhikkhu kosalesu viharati aññatarasmiṁ vanasaṇḍe. Tena kho pana samayena so bhikkhu divāvihāragato supati. Atha kho yā tasmiṁ vanasaṇḍe adhivatthā devatā tassa bhikkhuno anukampikā atthakāmā taṁ bhikkhuṁ saṁvejetukāmā yena so bhikkhu tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā taṁ bhikkhuṁ gāthāhi ajjhabhāsi—

1384 “Uṭṭhehi bhikkhu kiṁ sesi,
ko attho supitena[460] te;
Āturassa hi kā niddā,
sallaviddhassa ruppato.

1385 Yāya saddhāya pabbajito[461],
agārasmānagāriyaṁ;
Tameva saddhaṁ brūhehi,
mā niddāya vasaṁ gamī”ti.

1386 “Aniccā addhuvā kāmā,
yesu mandova mucchito;
Baddhesu muttaṁ asitaṁ,
kasmā pabbajitaṁ tape. [462]

1387 Chandarāgassa vinayā,
avijjāsamatikkamā;
Taṁ ñāṇaṁ paramodānaṁ[463],
kasmā pabbajitaṁ tape.

1388 Chetvā avijjaṁ vijjāya,
āsavānaṁ parikkhayā;
Asokaṁ anupāyāsaṁ,
kasmā pabbajitaṁ tape. [464]

1389 Āraddhavīriyaṁ pahitattaṁ,
Niccaṁ daḷhaparakkamaṁ;
Nibbānaṁ abhikaṅkhantaṁ,
Kasmā pabbajitaṁ tape”ti.

  1. supitena: supinena (sī, pā2) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. pabbajito: yāya saddhāpabbajito (sī, syā1-3, kaṃ, pā2) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Baddhesu: khandhesu (sī), bandhesu (pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. paramodānaṁ: paramavodānaṁ (sī), pariyodātaṁ (ka-sī, pā1-2), paramodātaṁ (syā1-3, kaṃ) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. Dhetvā: bhetvā (sī, syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
