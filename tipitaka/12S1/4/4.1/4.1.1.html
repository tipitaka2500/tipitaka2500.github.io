4.1.1 Tapokammasutta

4.1.1. ตโปกัม์มสุต์ต

Mārasaṃyutta

Paṭhamavagga

Tapokammasutta

137. 769 [251] Evaṁ me sutaṁ— ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā uruvelāyaṁ viharati najjā nerañjarāya tīre ajapālanigrodhamūle paṭhamābhisambuddho. Atha kho bhagavato rahogatassa paṭisallīnassa evaṁ cetaso parivitakko udapādi— “mutto vatamhi tāya dukkarakārikāya. Sādhu mutto vatamhi tāya anatthasaṁhitāya dukkarakārikāya. Sādhu vatamhi mutto bodhiṁ samajjhagan”ti. [252]

770 Atha kho māro pāpimā bhagavato cetasā cetoparivitakkamaññāya yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ gāthāya ajjhabhāsi—

771 “Tapokammā apakkamma,
ye na sujjhanti māṇavā;
Asuddho maññasi suddho,
suddhimaggā aparaddho[253]”ti.

772 Atha kho bhagavā “māro ayaṁ pāpimā” iti viditvā māraṁ pāpimantaṁ gāthāhi ajjhabhāsi—

773 “Anatthasaṁhitaṁ ñatvā,
yaṁ kiñci amaraṁ tapaṁ[254];
Sabbaṁ natthāvahaṁ hoti,
phiyārittaṁva dhammani[255].

774 Sīlaṁ samādhi paññañca,
Maggaṁ bodhāya bhāvayaṁ;
Pattosmi paramaṁ suddhiṁ,
Nihato tvamasi antakā”ti.

775 Atha kho māro pāpimā “jānāti maṁ bhagavā, jānāti maṁ sugato”ti, dukkhī dummano tatthevantaradhāyīti.

  1. āyuvaggo (sī), āyuvaggo paṭhamo (pā2) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. samajjhagan"ti: sādhu ṭhito sato bodhiṁ samajjhaganti (sī, pā1-2), sādhu vatamhi satto bodhisamajjhagūti (syā1-3, kaṃ) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. aparaddho: suddhimaggamaparaddho (sī, pā1-2), suddhimaggaṁ aparaddho (syā1-3, kaṃ) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. tapaṁ: aparaṁ tapaṁ (syā1-3, pā1-2, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. dhammani: dhammaniṁ (pā1), phiyārittaṁ vajahmani (pā2), jammaniṁ (ka-ma) etthāyaṁ dhammasaddo sakkate dhanvan-saddena sadiso maruvācakoti veditabbo, yathā (Word Variant) ↩
