1.6.9 Dutiyasutta

1.6.9. ทุติยสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Jarāvagga

Dutiyasutta

59. 304 [91] “Kiṁsu dutiyā[92] purisassa hoti,
Kiṁsu cenaṁ pasāsati;
Kissa cābhirato macco,
Sabbadukkhā pamuccatī”ti.

305 “Saddhā dutiyā purisassa hoti,
Paññā cenaṁ pasāsati;
Nibbānābhirato macco,
Sabbadukkhā pamuccatī”ti.

  1. dutiyāsuttaṁ (sī, pā2), dutiyauppathasuttaṁ (ka-sī) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. dutiyā: dutiyaṁ (syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
