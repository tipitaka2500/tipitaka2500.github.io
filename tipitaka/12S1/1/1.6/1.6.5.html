1.6.5 Paṭhamajanasutta

1.6.5. ปฐมชนสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Jarāvagga

Paṭhamajanasutta

55. 296 [88] “Kiṁsu janeti purisaṁ,
kiṁsu tassa vidhāvati;
Kiṁsu saṁsāramāpādi,
kiṁsu tassa mahabbhayan”ti.

297 “Taṇhā janeti purisaṁ,
cittamassa vidhāvati;
Satto saṁsāramāpādi,
dukkhamassa mahabbhayan”ti.
