1.6.6 Dutiyajanasutta

1.6.6. ทุติยชนสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Jarāvagga

Dutiyajanasutta

56. 298 [89] “Kiṁsu janeti purisaṁ,
kiṁsu tassa vidhāvati;
Kiṁsu saṁsāramāpādi,
kismā na parimuccatī”ti.

299 “Taṇhā janeti purisaṁ,
cittamassa vidhāvati;
Satto saṁsāramāpādi,
dukkhā na parimuccatī”ti.
