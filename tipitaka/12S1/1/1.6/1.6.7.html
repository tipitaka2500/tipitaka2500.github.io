1.6.7 Tatiyajanasutta

1.6.7. ตติยชนสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Jarāvagga

Tatiyajanasutta

57. 300 [90] “Kiṁsu janeti purisaṁ,
kiṁsu tassa vidhāvati;
Kiṁsu saṁsāramāpādi,
kiṁsu tassa parāyanan”ti.

301 “Taṇhā janeti purisaṁ,
cittamassa vidhāvati;
Satto saṁsāramāpādi,
kammaṁ tassa parāyanan”ti.
