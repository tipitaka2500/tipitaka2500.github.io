1.2.8 Hirīsutta

1.2.8. หิรีสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Nandanavagga

Hirīsutta

18. 58 [27] “Hirīnisedho puriso,
koci lokasmiṁ vijjati;
Yo nindaṁ[29] apabodhati[30],
asso bhadro kasāmivā”ti. [28]

59 “Hirīnisedhā tanuyā,
ye caranti sadā satā;
Antaṁ dukkhassa pappuyya,
caranti visame saman”ti.

  1. hirisuttaṁ (sī, syā2, syā3, pā2) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. "Hirīnisedho: hirinisedho (sī, syā2, syā3) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. nindaṁ: niddaṁ (18Dh:153) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. apabodhati: appabodhati (sī, pā1), apabodheti (syā1-3, kaṃ, ka-ma 18Dh:153), appabodheti (pā2) (Word Variant) ↩
