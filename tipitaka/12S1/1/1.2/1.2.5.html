1.2.5 Saṇamānasutta

1.2.5. สณมานสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Nandanavagga

Saṇamānasutta

15. 51 [18] “Ṭhite majjhanhike[19] kāle,
sannisīvesu pakkhisu;
Saṇateva brahāraññaṁ[21],
taṁ bhayaṁ paṭibhāti man”ti. [20]

52 “Ṭhite majjhanhike kāle,
sannisīvesu pakkhisu;
Saṇateva brahāraññaṁ,
sā rati paṭibhāti man”ti.

  1. sanamānasuttaṁ (sī), sakamānasuttaṁ (ka-sī, syā1-3), sakamāno (pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. majjhanhike: majjhantike (sabbattha) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Saṇateva: sanateva (sī), palāteva (syā1-3) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. brahāraññaṁ: mahāraññaṁ (ka-sī, syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1-2, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
