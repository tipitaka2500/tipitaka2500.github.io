1.8.3 Vittasutta

1.8.3. วิต์ตสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Chetvāvagga

Vittasutta

73. 339 “Kiṁsūdha vittaṁ purisassa seṭṭhaṁ,
Kiṁsu suciṇṇo sukhamāvahati;
Kiṁsu have sādutaraṁ[106] rasānaṁ,
Kathaṁjīviṁ jīvitamāhu seṭṭhan”ti. [107]

340 “Saddhīdha vittaṁ purisassa seṭṭhaṁ,
Dhammo suciṇṇo sukhamāvahati;
Saccaṁ have sādutaraṁ rasānaṁ,
Paññājīviṁ jīvitamāhu seṭṭhan”ti.

  1. sādutaraṁ: sādhutaraṁ (syā1-3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Kathaṁjīviṁ: kathaṁjīvī (syā2, syā3), kiṁsujīviṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
