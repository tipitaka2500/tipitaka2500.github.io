1.8.6 Najīratisutta

1.8.6. นชีรติสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Chetvāvagga

Najīratisutta

76. 346 “Kiṁ jīrati kiṁ na jīrati,
kiṁsu uppathoti vuccati;
Kiṁsu dhammānaṁ paripantho,
kiṁsu rattindivakkhayo;
Kiṁ malaṁ brahmacariyassa,
kiṁ sinānamanodakaṁ.

347 Kati lokasmiṁ chiddāni,
yattha vittaṁ[110] na tiṭṭhati;
Bhagavantaṁ puṭṭhumāgamma,
kathaṁ jānemu taṁ mayan”ti.

348 “Rūpaṁ jīrati maccānaṁ,
nāmagottaṁ na jīrati;
Rāgo uppathoti vuccati.

349 Lobho dhammānaṁ paripantho,
Vayo rattindivakkhayo;
Itthī malaṁ brahmacariyassa,
Etthāyaṁ sajjate pajā;
Tapo ca brahmacariyañca,
Taṁ sinānamanodakaṁ.

350 Cha lokasmiṁ chiddāni,
yattha vittaṁ na tiṭṭhati;
Ālasyañca pamādo ca,
anuṭṭhānaṁ asaṁyamo;
Niddā tandī[112] ca te chidde,
sabbaso taṁ vivajjaye”ti. [111]

  1. vittaṁ: cittaṁ (sī, syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. Ᾱlasyañca: ālassañca (sī, pā2), alassañca (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. tandī: tandi (sī) (Word Variant) ↩
