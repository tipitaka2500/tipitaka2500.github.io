1.3.1 Sattisutta

1.3.1. สัต์ติสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Sattivagga

Sattisutta

21. 96 Sāvatthinidānaṁ. Ekamantaṁ ṭhitā kho sā devatā bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

97 [38] “Sattiyā viya omaṭṭho,
ḍayhamānova matthake;
Kāmarāgappahānāya,
sato bhikkhu paribbaje”ti. [39]

98 “Sattiyā viya omaṭṭho,
ḍayhamānova matthake;
Sakkāyadiṭṭhippahānāya,
sato bhikkhu paribbaje”ti.

  1. 12S1:446; 19Th1:88-90, 19Th1:1534-1535; 27Ne:791; 27Pe:247 (Cross Reference) ↩
  2. ḍayhamānova: ḍayhamāneva (ka-sī, syā1-3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
