1.7.1 Nāmasutta

1.7.1. นามสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Addhavagga

Nāmasutta

61. 311 [93] “Kiṁsu sabbaṁ addhabhavi[94],
kismā bhiyyo na vijjati;
Kissassu ekadhammassa,
sabbeva vasamanvagū”ti. [95]

312 “Nāmaṁ sabbaṁ addhabhavi,
nāmā bhiyyo na vijjati;
Nāmassa ekadhammassa,
sabbeva vasamanvagū”ti.

  1. anvavaggo (sī), andhavaggo (syā1-3, pā2) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. addhabhavi: anvabhavi (sī), andhabhavi (syā1-3) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. vasamanvagū"ti: vasamaddhagū (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
