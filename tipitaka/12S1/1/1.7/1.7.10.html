1.7.10 Lokasutta

1.7.10. โลกสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Addhavagga

Lokasutta

70. 329 “Kismiṁ loko samuppanno,
kismiṁ kubbati santhavaṁ;
Kissa loko upādāya,
kismiṁ loko vihaññatī”ti.

330 “Chasu loko samuppanno,
chasu kubbati santhavaṁ;
Channameva upādāya,
chasu loko vihaññatī”ti.

331 Addhavaggo[101] sattamo.

332

333 Nāmaṁ cittañca taṇhā ca,
saṁyojanañca bandhanā;
Abbhāhatuḍḍito pihito,
icchā lokena te dasāti.

  1. Addhavaggo: anvavaggo (sī), andhavaggo (syā1-3) (Word Variant) ↩
