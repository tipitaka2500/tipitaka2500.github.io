1.7.2. จิต์ตสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Addhavagga

Cittasutta

62. 313 “Kenassu nīyati loko,
kenassu parikassati;
Kissassu ekadhammassa,
sabbeva vasamanvagū”ti.

314 “Cittena nīyati loko,
cittena parikassati;
Cittassa ekadhammassa,
sabbeva vasamanvagū”ti.
