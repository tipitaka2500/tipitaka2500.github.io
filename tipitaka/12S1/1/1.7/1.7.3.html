1.7.3. ตัณ๎หาสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Addhavagga

Taṇhāsutta

63. 315 “Kenassu nīyati loko,
kenassu parikassati;
Kissassu ekadhammassa,
sabbeva vasamanvagū”ti.

316 “Taṇhāya nīyati loko,
taṇhāya parikassati;
Taṇhāya ekadhammassa,
sabbeva vasamanvagū”ti.
