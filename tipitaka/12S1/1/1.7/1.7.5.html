1.7.5 Bandhanasutta

1.7.5. พัน์ธนสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Addhavagga

Bandhanasutta

65. 319 “Kiṁsu sambandhano loko,
kiṁsu tassa vicāraṇaṁ;
Kissassu vippahānena,
sabbaṁ chindati bandhanan”ti.

320 “Nandīsambandhano loko,
vitakkassa vicāraṇaṁ;
Taṇhāya vippahānena,
sabbaṁ chindati bandhanan”ti.
