1.7.4. สํโยชนสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Addhavagga

Saṃyojanasutta

64. 317 “Kiṁsu saṁyojano loko,
kiṁsu tassa vicāraṇaṁ;
Kissassu vippahānena,
nibbānaṁ iti vuccatī”ti.

318 “Nandīsaṁyojano loko,
vitakkassa vicāraṇaṁ;
Taṇhāya vippahānena,
nibbānaṁ iti vuccatī”ti. [96]

  1. "Nandīsaṁyojano: nandi saṁyojano (sī), nandisaññojano (syā1-3, kaṃ), nandisaṁyojano (pā2) (Word Variant) ↩
