1.7.9. อิจ์ฉาสุต์ต

Devatāsaṃyutta

Addhavagga

Icchāsutta

69. 327 “Kenassu bajjhatī loko,
kissa vinayāya muccati;
Kissassu vippahānena,
sabbaṁ chindati bandhanan”ti.

328 “Icchāya bajjhatī loko,
icchāvinayāya muccati;
Icchāya vippahānena,
sabbaṁ chindati bandhanan”ti.
