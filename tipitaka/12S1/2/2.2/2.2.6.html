2.2.6 Vāsudattasutta

2.2.6. วาสุทัต์ตสุต์ต

Devaputtasaṃyutta

Anāthapiṇḍikavagga

Vāsudattasutta

97. 446 Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho vāsudatto devaputto bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

447 [130] “Sattiyā viya omaṭṭho,
ḍayhamānova matthake;
Kāmarāgappahānāya,
sato bhikkhu paribbaje”ti. [131]

448 “Sattiyā viya omaṭṭho,
ḍayhamānova matthake;
Sakkāyadiṭṭhippahānāya,
sato bhikkhu paribbaje”ti.

  1. 12S1:96; 19Th1:88-90, 19Th1:1534-1535; 27Ne:791; 27Pe:247 (Cross Reference) ↩
  2. ḍayhamānova: ḍayhamāneva (syā1-3, kaṃ) (Word Variant) ↩
