2.2.2 Veṇḍusutta

2.2.2. เวณ์ฑุสุต์ต

Devaputtasaṃyutta

Anāthapiṇḍikavagga

Veṇḍusutta

93. 433 Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho veṇḍu[125] devaputto bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

434 “Sukhitāva te[126] manujā,
sugataṁ payirupāsiya;
Yuñjaṁ gotamasāsane,
appamattā nu sikkhare”ti. [127]

435 “Ye me pavutte siṭṭhipade[128], (veṇḍūti bhagavā)
Anusikkhanti jhāyino;
Kāle te appamajjantā,
Na maccuvasagā siyun”ti.

  1. veṇḍu: veṇhu (sī, pā2) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. te: sukhitā vata te (sī, syā1-3, kaṃ, pā2) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. Yuñjaṁ: yuñja (syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. siṭṭhipade: satthipade (sī, syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1-2) (Word Variant) ↩
