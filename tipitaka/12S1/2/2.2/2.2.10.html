2.2.10 Anāthapiṇḍikasutta

2.2.10. อนาถปิณ์ฑิกสุต์ต

Devaputtasaṃyutta

Anāthapiṇḍikavagga

Anāthapiṇḍikasutta

101. 462 Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho anāthapiṇḍiko devaputto bhagavato santike imā gāthāyo abhāsi—

463 “Idañhi taṁ jetavanaṁ,
isisaṁghanisevitaṁ;
Āvutthaṁ dhammarājena,
pītisañjananaṁ mama.

464 Kammaṁ vijjā ca dhammo ca,
sīlaṁ jīvitamuttamaṁ;
Etena maccā sujjhanti,
na gottena dhanena vā.

465 Tasmā hi paṇḍito poso,
sampassaṁ atthamattano;
Yoniso vicine dhammaṁ,
evaṁ tattha visujjhati.

466 Sāriputtova paññāya,
sīlena upasamena ca;
Yopi pāraṅgato bhikkhu,
etāvaparamo siyā”ti.

467 Idamavoca anāthapiṇḍiko devaputto. Idaṁ vatvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā padakkhiṇaṁ katvā tatthevantaradhāyi.

468 Atha kho bhagavā tassā rattiyā accayena bhikkhū āmantesi— “imaṁ, bhikkhave, rattiṁ aññataro devaputto abhikkantāya rattiyā abhikkantavaṇṇo kevalakappaṁ jetavanaṁ obhāsetvā yenāhaṁ tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā maṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ aṭṭhāsi. Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho, bhikkhave, so devaputto mama santike imā gāthāyo abhāsi—

469 ‘Idañhi taṁ jetavanaṁ,
isisaṁghanisevitaṁ;
Āvutthaṁ dhammarājena,
pītisañjananaṁ mama.

470 Kammaṁ vijjā ca dhammo ca,
sīlaṁ jīvitamuttamaṁ;
Etena maccā sujjhanti,
na gottena dhanena vā.

471 Tasmā hi paṇḍito poso,
sampassaṁ atthamattano;
Yoniso vicine dhammaṁ,
evaṁ tattha visujjhati.

472 Sāriputtova paññāya,
sīlena upasamena ca;
Yopi pāraṅgato bhikkhu,
etāvaparamo siyā’ti.

473 Idamavoca, bhikkhave, so devaputto. Idaṁ vatvā maṁ abhivādetvā padakkhiṇaṁ katvā tatthevantaradhāyī”ti.

474 Evaṁ vutte, āyasmā ānando bhagavantaṁ etadavoca— “so hi nūna, bhante, anāthapiṇḍiko devaputto bhavissati. Anāthapiṇḍiko gahapati āyasmante sāriputte abhippasanno ahosī”ti. “Sādhu sādhu, ānanda, yāvatakaṁ kho, ānanda, takkāya pattabbaṁ anuppattaṁ taṁ tayā. Anāthapiṇḍiko hi so, ānanda, devaputto”ti.

475 Anāthapiṇḍikavaggo dutiyo.

476

477 Candimaso ca veṇḍu[139] ca,
Dīghalaṭṭhi ca nandano;
Candano vāsudatto ca,
Subrahmā kakudhena ca;
Uttaro navamo vutto,
Dasamo anāthapiṇḍikoti. [138]

  1. Candimaso: candimāso (pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. veṇḍu: veṇhu (sī, ka-ma), veṇhū (pā2) (Word Variant) ↩
