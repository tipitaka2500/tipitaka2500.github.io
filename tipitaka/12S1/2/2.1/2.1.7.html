2.1.7 Pañcālacaṇḍasutta

2.1.7. ปั์จาลจัณ์ฑสุต์ต

Devaputtasaṃyutta

Paṭhamavagga

Pañcālacaṇḍasutta

88. 395 Sāvatthinidānaṁ. Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho pañcālacaṇḍo devaputto bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

396 “Sambādhe vata okāsaṁ,
Avindi bhūrimedhaso;
Yo jhānamabujjhi[119] buddho,
Paṭilīnanisabho munī”ti.

397 “Sambādhe vāpi vindanti, (pañcālacaṇḍāti bhagavā)
Dhammaṁ nibbānapattiyā;
Ye satiṁ paccalatthaṁsu,
Sammā te susamāhitā”ti.

  1. jhānamabujjhi: jhānamabudhā (sī, pā2), jhānamabuddhi (syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
