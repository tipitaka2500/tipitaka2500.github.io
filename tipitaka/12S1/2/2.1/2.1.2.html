2.1.2. ทุติยกัส์สปสุต์ต

Devaputtasaṃyutta

Paṭhamavagga

Dutiyakassapasutta

83. 374 Sāvatthinidānaṁ. Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho kassapo devaputto bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

375 “Bhikkhu siyā jhāyī vimuttacitto,
Ākaṅkhe ce hadayassānupattiṁ;
Lokassa ñatvā udayabbayañca,
Sucetaso anissito tadānisaṁso”ti.
