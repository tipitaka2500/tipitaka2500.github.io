1.5.7 Khemasutta

1.5.7. เขมสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Dhammikavagga

Khemasutta

49. 369 Ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena āyasmā ca khemo āyasmā ca sumano sāvatthiyaṁ viharanti andhavanasmiṁ. Atha kho āyasmā ca khemo āyasmā ca sumano yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkamiṁsu; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdiṁsu. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho āyasmā khemo bhagavantaṁ etadavoca—

370 “Yo so, bhante, bhikkhu arahaṁ khīṇāsavo vusitavā katakaraṇīyo ohitabhāro anuppattasadattho parikkhīṇabhavasaṁyojano sammadaññāvimutto tassa na evaṁ hoti— ‘atthi me seyyoti vā atthi me sadisoti vā atthi me hīnoti vā’”ti. Idamavocāyasmā khemo. Samanuñño satthā ahosi. Atha kho āyasmā khemo “samanuñño me satthā”ti uṭṭhāyāsanā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā padakkhiṇaṁ katvā pakkāmi.

371 Atha kho āyasmā sumano acirapakkante āyasmante kheme bhagavantaṁ etadavoca— “yo so, bhante, bhikkhu arahaṁ khīṇāsavo vusitavā katakaraṇīyo ohitabhāro anuppattasadattho parikkhīṇabhavasaṁyojano sammadaññāvimutto tassa na evaṁ hoti— ‘natthi me seyyoti vā natthi me sadisoti vā natthi me hīnoti vā’”ti. Idamavocāyasmā sumano. Samanuñño satthā ahosi. Atha kho āyasmā sumano “samanuñño me satthā”ti uṭṭhāyāsanā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā padakkhiṇaṁ katvā pakkāmi.

372 Atha kho bhagavā acirapakkantesu āyasmante ca kheme āyasmante ca sumane bhikkhū āmantesi— “evaṁ kho, bhikkhave, kulaputtā aññaṁ byākaronti. Attho ca vutto attā ca anupanīto. Atha ca pana idhekacce moghapurisā hasamānakā[139] maññe aññaṁ byākaronti. Te pacchā vighātaṁ āpajjantīti.

373 Na ussesu na omesu,
Samatte nopanīyare[140];
Khīṇā jāti vusitaṁ brahmacariyaṁ,
Caranti saṁyojanavippamuttā”ti.

374 Sattamaṁ.

  1. hasamānakā: hasamānakaṁ (ka-ma) 3V:1147 (Word Variant) ↩
  2. nopanīyare: nopaniyyare (syā1-3, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
