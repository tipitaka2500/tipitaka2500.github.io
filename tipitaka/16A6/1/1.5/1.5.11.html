1.5.11 Appamādasutta

1.5.11. อัป์ปมาทสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Dhammikavagga

Appamādasutta

53. 392 Atha kho aññataro brāhmaṇo yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavatā saddhiṁ sammodi. Sammodanīyaṁ kathaṁ sāraṇīyaṁ vītisāretvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho so brāhmaṇo bhagavantaṁ etadavoca—

393 “Atthi nu kho, bho gotama, eko dhammo bhāvito bahulīkato yo ubho atthe samadhiggayha tiṭṭhati— diṭṭhadhammikañceva atthaṁ, yo ca attho samparāyiko”ti? “Atthi kho, brāhmaṇa, eko dhammo bhāvito bahulīkato yo ubho atthe samadhiggayha tiṭṭhati— diṭṭhadhammikañceva atthaṁ, yo ca attho samparāyiko”ti.

394 “Katamo pana, bho gotama, eko dhammo bhāvito bahulīkato yo ubho atthe samadhiggayha tiṭṭhati— diṭṭhadhammikañceva atthaṁ, yo ca attho samparāyiko”ti? “Appamādo kho, brāhmaṇa, eko dhammo bhāvito bahulīkato ubho atthe samadhiggayha tiṭṭhati— diṭṭhadhammikañceva atthaṁ, yo ca attho samparāyiko.

395 Seyyathāpi, brāhmaṇa, yāni kānici jaṅgalānaṁ[145] pāṇānaṁ padajātāni, sabbāni tāni hatthipade samodhānaṁ gacchanti; hatthipadaṁ tesaṁ aggamakkhāyati, yadidaṁ mahantattena. Evamevaṁ kho, brāhmaṇa, appamādo eko dhammo bhāvito bahulīkato ubho atthe samadhiggayha tiṭṭhati— diṭṭhadhammikañceva atthaṁ, yo ca attho samparāyiko. (1)

396 Seyyathāpi, brāhmaṇa, kūṭāgārassa yā kāci gopānasiyo sabbā tā kūṭaṅgamā kūṭaninnā kūṭasamosaraṇā, kūṭaṁ tāsaṁ aggamakkhāyati; evamevaṁ kho, brāhmaṇa…pe… . (2)

397 Seyyathāpi, brāhmaṇa, pabbajalāyako pabbajaṁ[146] lāyitvā agge gahetvā odhunāti nidhunāti nicchādeti; evamevaṁ kho, brāhmaṇa…pe… . (3)

398 Seyyathāpi, brāhmaṇa, ambapiṇḍiyā vaṇṭacchinnāya yāni kānici ambāni vaṇṭūpanibandhanāni sabbāni tāni tadanvayāni bhavanti; evamevaṁ kho, brāhmaṇa…pe… . (4)

399 Seyyathāpi, brāhmaṇa, ye keci khuddarājāno[147] sabbete rañño cakkavattissa anuyantā[148] bhavanti, rājā tesaṁ cakkavattī aggamakkhāyati; evamevaṁ kho, brāhmaṇa…pe… . (5)

400 Seyyathāpi, brāhmaṇa, yā kāci tārakarūpānaṁ pabhā sabbā tā candassa pabhāya kalaṁ nāgghanti soḷasiṁ, candappabhā tāsaṁ aggamakkhāyati; evamevaṁ kho, brāhmaṇa, appamādo eko dhammo bhāvito bahulīkato ubho atthe samadhiggayha tiṭṭhati— diṭṭhadhammikañceva atthaṁ yo ca attho samparāyiko. (6)

401 Ayaṁ kho, brāhmaṇa, eko dhammo bhāvito bahulīkato ubho atthe samadhiggayha tiṭṭhati— diṭṭhadhammikañceva atthaṁ, yo ca attho samparāyiko”ti.

402 “Abhikkantaṁ, bho gotama, abhikkantaṁ, bho gotama…pe… upāsakaṁ maṁ bhavaṁ gotamo dhāretu ajjatagge pāṇupetaṁ saraṇaṁ gatan”ti.

403 Ekādasamaṁ.

  1. jaṅgalānaṁ: jaṅgamānaṁ (sī, pā1) 9M:1053; 17A10:79 (Word Variant) ↩
  2. pabbajaṁ: babbajalāyako babbajaṁ (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. khuddarājāno: kuḍḍarājāno (sī), khuddakarājāno (syā1-3) 17A10:79 (Word Variant) ↩
  4. anuyantā: anuyuttā (sī, syā1-3, kaṃ, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
