1.1.9 Anussatiṭṭhānasutta

1.1.9. อนุส์สติฏ์ฐานสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Āhuneyyavagga

Anussatiṭṭhānasutta

9. 30 [5] “Chayimāni, bhikkhave, anussatiṭṭhānāni. Katamāni cha? Buddhānussati, dhammānussati, saṁghānussati, sīlānussati, cāgānussati, devatānussati. Imāni kho, bhikkhave, cha anussatiṭṭhānānī”ti.
