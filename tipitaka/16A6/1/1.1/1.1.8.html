1.1.8 Anuttariyasutta

1.1.8. อนุต์ตริยสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Āhuneyyavagga

Anuttariyasutta

8. 28 [4] “Chayimāni, bhikkhave, anuttariyāni. Katamāni cha? Dassanānuttariyaṁ, savanānuttariyaṁ, lābhānuttariyaṁ, sikkhānuttariyaṁ, pāricariyānuttariyaṁ, anussatānuttariyaṁ. Imāni kho, bhikkhave, cha anuttariyānī”ti.

29 Aṭṭhamaṁ.
