Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Rāgapeyyāla

140. 775 “Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya cha dhammā bhāvetabbā. Katame cha? Dassanānuttariyaṁ, savanānuttariyaṁ, lābhānuttariyaṁ, sikkhānuttariyaṁ, pāricariyānuttariyaṁ, anussatānuttariyaṁ. Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya ime cha dhammā bhāvetabbā”ti.
