2.4.8 Paṭhamaabhabbaṭṭhānasutta

2.4.8. ปมอภัพ์พัฏ์านสุต์ต

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Sītivagga

Paṭhamaabhabbaṭṭhānasutta

92. 703 “Chayimāni, bhikkhave, abhabbaṭṭhānāni. Katamāni cha? Abhabbo diṭṭhisampanno puggalo satthari agāravo viharituṁ appatisso, abhabbo diṭṭhisampanno puggalo dhamme agāravo viharituṁ appatisso, abhabbo diṭṭhisampanno puggalo saṁghe agāravo viharituṁ appatisso, abhabbo diṭṭhisampanno puggalo sikkhāya agāravo viharituṁ appatisso, abhabbo diṭṭhisampanno puggalo anāgamanīyaṁ vatthuṁ paccāgantuṁ, abhabbo diṭṭhisampanno puggalo aṭṭhamaṁ bhavaṁ nibbattetuṁ. Imāni kho, bhikkhave, cha abhabbaṭṭhānānī”ti.

704 Aṭṭhamaṁ.
