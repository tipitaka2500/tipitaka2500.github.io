2.4.7 Abhabbasutta

2.4.7. อภัพ์พสุต์ต

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Sītivagga

Abhabbasutta

91. 701 “Cha, bhikkhave, dhamme abhabbo diṭṭhisampanno puggalo uppādetuṁ. Katame cha? Sakkāyadiṭṭhiṁ, vicikicchaṁ, sīlabbataparāmāsaṁ, apāyagamanīyaṁ rāgaṁ, apāyagamanīyaṁ dosaṁ, apāyagamanīyaṁ mohaṁ. Ime kho, bhikkhave, cha dhamme abhabbo diṭṭhisampanno puggalo uppādetun”ti.

702 Sattamaṁ.
