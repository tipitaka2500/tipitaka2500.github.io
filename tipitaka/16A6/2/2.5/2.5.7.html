2.5.7 Anavatthitasutta

2.5.7. อนวัต์ถิตสุต์ต

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Ānisaṃsavagga

Anavatthitasutta

102. 728 “Cha, bhikkhave, ānisaṁse sampassamānena alameva bhikkhunā sabbasaṅkhāresu anodhiṁ karitvā aniccasaññaṁ upaṭṭhāpetuṁ. Katame cha? ‘Sabbasaṅkhārā ca me anavatthitā[281] khāyissanti, sabbaloke ca me mano nābhiramissati[282], sabbalokā ca me mano vuṭṭhahissati, nibbānapoṇañca me mānasaṁ bhavissati, saṁyojanā ca me pahānaṁ gacchissanti[283], paramena ca sāmaññena samannāgato bhavissāmī’ti. Ime kho, bhikkhave, cha ānisaṁse sampassamānena alameva bhikkhunā sabbasaṅkhāresu anodhiṁ karitvā aniccasaññaṁ upaṭṭhāpetun”ti.

729 Sattamaṁ.

  1. anavatthitā: anavaṭṭhitato (sī, syā1-3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. nābhiramissati: na ramissati (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. gacchissanti: gacchanti (sī, syā1-3, pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
