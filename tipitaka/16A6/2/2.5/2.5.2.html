2.5.2 Ānisaṃsasutta

2.5.2. อานิสํสสุต์ต

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Ānisaṃsavagga

Ānisaṃsasutta

97. 716 “Chayime, bhikkhave, ānisaṁsā sotāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya. Katame cha? Saddhammaniyato hoti[276], aparihānadhammo hoti, pariyantakatassa dukkhaṁ hoti, asādhāraṇena ñāṇena samannāgato hoti, hetu cassa sudiṭṭho, hetusamuppannā ca dhammā. Ime kho, bhikkhave, cha ānisaṁsā sotāpattiphalasacchikiriyāyā”ti.

717 Dutiyaṁ.
