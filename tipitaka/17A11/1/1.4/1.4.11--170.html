1.4.3.

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Rāgapeyyāla

513--672. 228 “Dosassa…pe… mohassa… kodhassa… upanāhassa… makkhassa… paḷāsassa… issāya… macchariyassa… māyāya… sāṭheyyassa… thambhassa… sārambhassa… mānassa… atimānassa… madassa… pamādassa abhiññāya…pe… pariññāya… parikkhayāya… pahānāya… khayāya… vayāya… virāgāya… nirodhāya… cāgāya… paṭinissaggāya ime ekādasa dhammā bhāvetabbā”ti.

229 Idamavoca bhagavā. Attamanā te bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti.

230 Rāgapeyyālaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.

231 Nava suttasahassāni,
bhiyyo pañca[55]satāni ca;
Sattapaññāsa suttantā[56],
aṅguttarasamāyutāti. [57]

232 Ekādasakanipātapāḷi niṭṭhitā.

233 Aṅguttaranikāyo samatto.

  1. ca: pañca suttasatāni ca (aṭṭha.) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. suttantā: suttāni (aṭṭha.) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. aṅguttarasamāyutāti: honti aṅguttarāgame (aṭṭha.) (Word Variant) ↩
