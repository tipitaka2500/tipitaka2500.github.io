90. 227 Nasappaṭighaṁ nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sappaṭigho sanidassanasappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… ekaṁ.

228 Naappaṭighaṁ nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sappaṭigho sanidassanasappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… ekaṁ.

229 Nasappaṭighaṁ nasanidassanasappaṭighañca naappaṭighaṁ nasanidassanasappaṭighañca dhammaṁ paṭicca sappaṭigho sanidassanasappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… ekaṁ. (Sabbattha tīṇi.) Hetuyā tīṇi, adhipatiyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.
