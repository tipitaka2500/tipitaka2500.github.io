Hetuduka, Atītattika

80. 194 Nahetu napaccuppanno dhammo hetussa paccuppannassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

195 Ārammaṇe nava, adhipatiyā nava, anantare samanantare nava, upanissaye nava, natthiyā vigate nava.
