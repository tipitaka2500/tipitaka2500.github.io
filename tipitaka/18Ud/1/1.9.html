1.9 Jaṭilasutta

1.9. ชฏิลสุต์ต

Bodhivagga

Jaṭilasutta

9. 46 Evaṁ me sutaṁ— ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā gayāyaṁ viharati gayāsīse. Tena kho pana samayena sambahulā jaṭilā sītāsu hemantikāsu rattīsu antaraṭṭhake himapātasamaye gayāyaṁ ummujjantipi nimujjantipi, ummujjanimujjampi karonti osiñcantipi, aggimpi juhanti— “iminā suddhī”ti.

47 Addasā kho bhagavā te sambahule jaṭile sītāsu hemantikāsu rattīsu antaraṭṭhake himapātasamaye gayāyaṁ ummujjantepi nimujjantepi ummujjanimujjampi karonte[29] osiñcantepi aggimpi juhante— “iminā suddhī”ti.

48 Atha kho bhagavā etamatthaṁ viditvā tāyaṁ velāyaṁ imaṁ udānaṁ udānesi—

49 [30] “Na udakena sucī hoti,
bahvettha nhāyatī[31] jano;
Yamhi saccañca dhammo ca,
so sucī so ca brāhmaṇo”ti. (9)

50 Navamaṁ.
