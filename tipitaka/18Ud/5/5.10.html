5.10 Cūḷapanthakasutta

5.10. จูฬปัน์ถกสุต์ต

Soṇavagga

Cūḷapanthakasutta

50. 449 [227] Evaṁ me sutaṁ— ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena āyasmā cūḷapanthako[228] bhagavato avidūre nisinno hoti pallaṅkaṁ ābhujitvā ujuṁ kāyaṁ paṇidhāya parimukhaṁ satiṁ upaṭṭhapetvā.

450 Addasā kho bhagavā āyasmantaṁ cūḷapanthakaṁ avidūre nisinnaṁ pallaṅkaṁ ābhujitvā ujuṁ kāyaṁ paṇidhāya parimukhaṁ satiṁ upaṭṭhapetvā.

451 Atha kho bhagavā etamatthaṁ viditvā tāyaṁ velāyaṁ imaṁ udānaṁ udānesi—

452 “Ṭhitena kāyena ṭhitena cetasā,
Tiṭṭhaṁ nisinno uda vā sayāno;
Etaṁ satiṁ bhikkhu adhiṭṭhahāno,
Labhetha pubbāpariyaṁ visesaṁ;
Laddhāna pubbāpariyaṁ visesaṁ,
Adassanaṁ maccurājassa gacche”ti. (61) [229]

453 Dasamaṁ.

454 Soṇavaggo[230] pañcamo.

455

456 Piyo appāyukā kuṭṭhī,
kumārakā uposatho;
Soṇo ca revato bhedo,
sadhāya panthakena cāti. [231]

  3. Etaṁ: evaṁ (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  4. Soṇavaggo: soṇattheravaggo (syā1-3, kaṃ, ka-ma), soṇassa therassa vaggo (pā1), mahāvaggo (aṭṭhakathāya sameti) (Word Variant) ↩
  5. sadhāya: saddhāya (sī), saddāyamānā (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
