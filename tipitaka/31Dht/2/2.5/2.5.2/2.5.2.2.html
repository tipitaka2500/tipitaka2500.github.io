2.5.2.2 Cūḷantaraduka

2.5.2.2. จูฬัน์ตรทุก

Niddesa

Pañcamanaya Asaṅgahitenaasaṅgahitapadaniddesa

Duka

Cūḷantaraduka

214. 288 Appaccayehi dhammehi ye dhammā… asaṅkhatehi dhammehi ye dhammā khandhasaṅgahena asaṅgahitā āyatanasaṅgahena asaṅgahitā dhātusaṅgahena asaṅgahitā, tehi dhammehi ye dhammā…pe… te dhammā dvīhi khandhehi ekādasahāyatanehi sattarasahi dhātūhi asaṅgahitā.

215. 289 Sanidassanehi dhammehi ye dhammā… sappaṭighehi dhammehi ye dhammā khandhasaṅgahena asaṅgahitā āyatanasaṅgahena asaṅgahitā dhātusaṅgahena asaṅgahitā, tehi dhammehi ye dhammā…pe… te dhammā catūhi khandhehi dvīhāyatanehi aṭṭhahi dhātūhi asaṅgahitā.

216. 290 Rūpīhi dhammehi ye dhammā khandhasaṅgahena asaṅgahitā āyatanasaṅgahena asaṅgahitā dhātusaṅgahena asaṅgahitā, tehi dhammehi ye dhammā…pe… te dhammā ekena khandhena ekenāyatanena sattahi dhātūhi asaṅgahitā.

217. 291 Arūpīhi dhammehi ye dhammā… lokuttarehi dhammehi ye dhammā khandhasaṅgahena asaṅgahitā āyatanasaṅgahena asaṅgahitā dhātusaṅgahena asaṅgahitā, tehi dhammehi ye dhammā…pe… te dhammā ekena khandhena dasahāyatanehi dasahi dhātūhi asaṅgahitā.
