2.6.3. ธาตุ

Niddesa

Chaṭṭhanaya Sampayogavippayogapadaniddesa

Dhātu

233. 310 Cakkhudhātu…pe… phoṭṭhabbadhātu…pe… sampayuttāti? Natthi. Katihi vippayuttā? Catūhi khandhehi ekenāyatanena sattahi dhātūhi vippayuttā; ekenāyatanena ekāya dhātuyā kehici vippayuttā.

234. 311 Cakkhuviññāṇadhātu…pe… manodhātu… manoviññāṇadhātu tīhi khandhehi sampayuttā; ekenāyatanena ekāya dhātuyā kehici sampayuttā. Katihi vippayuttā? Ekena khandhena dasahāyatanehi soḷasahi dhātūhi vippayuttā; ekenāyatanena ekāya dhātuyā kehici vippayuttā.
