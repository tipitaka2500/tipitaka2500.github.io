2.9.2.3. อาสวโคจ์ฉก

Niddesa

Navamanaya Sampayuttenasampayuttapadaniddesa

Duka

Āsavagocchaka

344. 430 Āsavehi dhammehi ye dhammā… āsavehi ceva sāsavehi ca dhammehi ye dhammā… āsavehi ceva āsavasampayuttehi ca dhammehi ye dhammā sampayuttā, tehi dhammehi ye dhammā sampayuttā… te dhammā tīhi khandhehi ekenāyatanena ekāya dhātuyā sampayuttā; ekena khandhena ekenāyatanena ekāya dhātuyā kehici sampayuttā.

345. 431 Āsavasampayuttehi ceva no ca āsavehi dhammehi ye dhammā sampayuttā, tehi dhammehi ye dhammā sampayuttā… te dhammā ekena khandhena ekenāyatanena ekāya dhātuyā kehici sampayuttā.
