3. 930 Kusalo sahetuko dhammo kusalassa sahetukassa dhammassa hetupaccayena paccayo. (1)

936 Kusalo sahetuko dhammo kusalassa sahetukassa dhammassa adhipatipaccayena paccayo. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

4. 937 Hetuyā tīṇi, ārammaṇe nava, adhipatiyā satta, anantare pañca, samanantare pañca, sahajāte tīṇi, aññamaññe tīṇi, nissaye tīṇi, upanissaye nava, āsevane tīṇi, kamme pañca, vipāke ekaṁ, āhāre…pe… sampayutte tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)
