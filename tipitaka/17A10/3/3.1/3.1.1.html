3.1.1 Samaṇasaññāsutta

3.1.1. สมณสั์าสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Samaṇasaññāvagga

Samaṇasaññāsutta

101. 925 “Tisso imā, bhikkhave, samaṇasaññā bhāvitā bahulīkatā satta dhamme paripūrenti. Katamā tisso? Vevaṇṇiyamhi ajjhupagato, parapaṭibaddhā me jīvikā, añño me ākappo karaṇīyoti— imā kho, bhikkhave, tisso samaṇasaññā bhāvitā bahulīkatā satta dhamme paripūrenti.

926 Katame satta? Santatakārī[206] hoti santatavutti[207] sīlesu, anabhijjhālu hoti, abyāpajjo hoti, anatimānī hoti, sikkhākāmo hoti, “idamatthan”tissa hoti jīvitaparikkhāresu, āraddhavīriyo ca[208] viharati. Imā kho, bhikkhave, tisso samaṇasaññā bhāvitā bahulīkatā ime satta dhamme paripūrentī”ti.

927 Paṭhamaṁ.

  1. Santatakārī: niccaṁ santatakārī (sī), satatakārī (syā1-3, pā1, ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. santatavutti: satatavuttī (syā1-3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. ca: āraddhaviriyo ca (sī, pā1), āraddhaviriyo (syā1-3) (Word Variant) ↩
