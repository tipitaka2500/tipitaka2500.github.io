3.4.10 Dukkhudrayasutta

3.4.10. ทุก์ขุท๎รยสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Sādhuvagga

Dukkhudrayasutta

143. 1124 “Dukkhudrayañca vo, bhikkhave, dhammaṁ desessāmi sukhudrayañca. Taṁ suṇātha…pe… katamo ca, bhikkhave, dukkhudrayo dhammo? Micchādiṭṭhi…pe… micchāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, dukkhudrayo dhammo. Katamo ca, bhikkhave, sukhudrayo dhammo? Sammādiṭṭhi…pe… sammāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sukhudrayo dhammo”ti.

1125 Dasamaṁ.
