Dhammasutta

138. 1114 “Dhammañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi adhammañca. Taṁ suṇātha…pe… katamo ca, bhikkhave, adhammo? Micchādiṭṭhi…pe… micchāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, adhammo. Katamo ca, bhikkhave, dhammo? Sammādiṭṭhi…pe… sammāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, dhammo”ti.
