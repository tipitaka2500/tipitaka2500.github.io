3.4.6. สาสวสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Sādhuvagga

Sāsavasutta

139. 1116 “Sāsavañca vo, bhikkhave, dhammaṁ desessāmi anāsavañca. Taṁ suṇātha…pe… katamo ca, bhikkhave, sāsavo dhammo? Micchādiṭṭhi…pe… micchāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sāsavo dhammo. Katamo ca, bhikkhave, anāsavo dhammo? Sammādiṭṭhi…pe… sammāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, anāsavo dhammo”ti.

1117 Chaṭṭhaṁ.
