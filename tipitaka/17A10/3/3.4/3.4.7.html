3.4.7. สาวัช์ชสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Sādhuvagga

Sāvajjasutta

140. 1118 “Sāvajjañca vo, bhikkhave, dhammaṁ desessāmi anavajjañca. Taṁ suṇātha…pe… katamo ca, bhikkhave, sāvajjo dhammo? Micchādiṭṭhi…pe… micchāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sāvajjo dhammo. Katamo ca, bhikkhave, anavajjo dhammo? Sammādiṭṭhi…pe… sammāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, anavajjo dhammo”ti.

1119 Sattamaṁ.
