3.4.3. อกุสลสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Sādhuvagga

Akusalasutta

136. 1110 [239] “Akusalañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi kusalañca. Taṁ suṇātha…pe… katamañca, bhikkhave, akusalaṁ? Micchādiṭṭhi…pe… micchāvimutti— idaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, akusalaṁ. Katamañca, bhikkhave, kusalaṁ? Sammādiṭṭhi…pe… sammāvimutti— idaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, kusalan”ti.
