3.3.9 Navamasutta

3.3.9. นวมสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Parisuddhavagga

Navamasutta

131. 1098 “Dasayime, bhikkhave, dhammā bhāvitā bahulīkatā ekantanibbidāya virāgāya nirodhāya upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā. Katame dasa? Sammādiṭṭhi…pe… sammāvimutti— ime kho, bhikkhave, dasa dhammā bhāvitā bahulīkatā ekantanibbidāya virāgāya nirodhāya upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā”ti.

1099 Navamaṁ.
