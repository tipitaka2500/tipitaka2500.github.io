3.3.5. ปัญ์จมสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Parisuddhavagga

Pañcamasutta

127. 1090 “Dasayime, bhikkhave, dhammā ekantanibbidāya virāgāya nirodhāya upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā. Katame dasa? Sammādiṭṭhi…pe… sammāvimutti— ime kho, bhikkhave, dasa dhammā ekantanibbidāya virāgāya nirodhāya upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā”ti.

1091 Pañcamaṁ.
