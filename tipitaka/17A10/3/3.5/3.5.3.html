3.5.3. สัท์ธัม์มสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Ariyavagga

Saddhammasutta

147. 1133 “Saddhammañca vo, bhikkhave, dhammaṁ desessāmi asaddhammañca. Taṁ suṇātha…pe… katamo ca, bhikkhave, asaddhammo? Micchādiṭṭhi…pe… micchāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, asaddhammo. Katamo ca, bhikkhave, saddhammo? Sammādiṭṭhi…pe… sammāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, saddhammo”ti.

1134 Tatiyaṁ.
