3.5.1 Ariyamaggasutta

3.5.1. อริยมัค์คสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Ariyavagga

Ariyamaggasutta

145. 1129 [241] “Ariyamaggañca vo, bhikkhave, dhammaṁ desessāmi anariyamaggañca. Taṁ suṇātha…pe… katamo ca, bhikkhave, anariyo maggo? Micchādiṭṭhi…pe… micchāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, anariyo maggo. Katamo ca, bhikkhave, ariyo maggo? Sammādiṭṭhi…pe… sammāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, ariyo maggo”ti.

1130 Paṭhamaṁ.
