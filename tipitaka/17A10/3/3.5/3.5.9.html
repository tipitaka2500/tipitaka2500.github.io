3.5.9 Anussaritabbasutta

3.5.9. อนุส์สริตัพ์พสุต์ต

Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Ariyavagga

Anussaritabbasutta

153. 1145 “Anussaritabbañca vo, bhikkhave, dhammaṁ desessāmi na anussaritabbañca. Taṁ suṇātha…pe… katamo ca, bhikkhave, na anussaritabbo dhammo? Micchādiṭṭhi…pe… micchāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, na anussaritabbo dhammo. Katamo ca, bhikkhave, anussaritabbo dhammo? Sammādiṭṭhi…pe… sammāvimutti— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, anussaritabbo dhammo”ti.

1146 Navamaṁ.
