1.4.2 Pātimokkhaṭṭhapanāsutta

1.4.2. ปาติโมก์ขัฏ์ปนาสุต์ต

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Upālivagga

Pātimokkhaṭṭhapanāsutta

32. 270 [66] “Kati nu kho, bhante, pātimokkhaṭṭhapanā”ti? “Dasa kho, upāli, pātimokkhaṭṭhapanā. Katame dasa? Pārājiko tassaṁ parisāyaṁ nisinno hoti, pārājikakathā vippakatā hoti, anupasampanno tassaṁ parisāyaṁ nisinno hoti, anupasampannakathā vippakatā hoti, sikkhaṁ paccakkhātako tassaṁ parisāyaṁ nisinno hoti, sikkhaṁ paccakkhātakakathā[67] vippakatā hoti, paṇḍako tassaṁ parisāyaṁ nisinno hoti, paṇḍakakathā vippakatā hoti, bhikkhunidūsako tassaṁ parisāyaṁ nisinno hoti, bhikkhunidūsakakathā vippakatā hoti— ime kho, upāli, dasa pātimokkhaṭṭhapanā”ti.
