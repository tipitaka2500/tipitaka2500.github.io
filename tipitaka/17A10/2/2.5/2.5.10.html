2.5.10 Abhabbasutta

2.5.10. อภัพ์พสุต์ต

Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

Upālivagga

Abhabbasutta

100. 918 “Dasayime, bhikkhave, dhamme appahāya abhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātuṁ. Katame dasa? Rāgaṁ, dosaṁ, mohaṁ, kodhaṁ, upanāhaṁ, makkhaṁ, paḷāsaṁ, issaṁ, macchariyaṁ, mānaṁ— ime kho, bhikkhave, dasa dhamme appahāya abhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātuṁ.

919 Dasayime, bhikkhave, dhamme pahāya bhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātuṁ. Katame dasa? Rāgaṁ, dosaṁ, mohaṁ, kodhaṁ, upanāhaṁ, makkhaṁ, paḷāsaṁ, issaṁ, macchariyaṁ, mānaṁ— ime kho, bhikkhave, dasa dhamme pahāya bhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātun”ti.
