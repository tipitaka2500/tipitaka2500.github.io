4.3.4. อัต์ถสุต์ต

Catutthapaṇṇāsaka

Sādhuvagga

Atthasutta

181. 1330 “Atthañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi anatthañca. Taṁ suṇātha…pe… katamo ca, bhikkhave, anattho? Pāṇātipāto…pe… micchādiṭṭhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, anattho.

1331 Katamo ca, bhikkhave, attho? Pāṇātipātā veramaṇī…pe… sammādiṭṭhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, attho”ti.

1332 Catutthaṁ.
