4.4.10. สัจ์ฉิกาตัพ์พสุต์ต

Catutthapaṇṇāsaka

Ariyamaggavagga

Sacchikātabbasutta

198. 1382 “Sacchikātabbañca vo, bhikkhave, dhammaṁ desessāmi na sacchikātabbañca. Taṁ suṇātha…pe… katamo ca, bhikkhave, na sacchikātabbo dhammo? Pāṇātipāto…pe… micchādiṭṭhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, na sacchikātabbo dhammo.

1383 Katamo ca, bhikkhave, sacchikātabbo dhammo? Pāṇātipātā veramaṇī…pe… sammādiṭṭhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sacchikātabbo dhammo”ti.

1384 Dasamaṁ.

1385 Ariyamaggavaggo[295] catuttho.
