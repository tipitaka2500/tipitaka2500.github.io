4.4.2 Kaṇhamaggasutta

4.4.2. กัณ๎หมัค์คสุต์ต

Catutthapaṇṇāsaka

Ariyamaggavagga

Kaṇhamaggasutta

190. 1358 “Kaṇhamaggañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi sukkamaggañca. Taṁ suṇātha…pe… katamo ca, bhikkhave, kaṇho maggo? Pāṇātipāto…pe… micchādiṭṭhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, kaṇho maggo.

1359 Katamo ca, bhikkhave, sukko maggo? Pāṇātipātā veramaṇī…pe… sammādiṭṭhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sukko maggo”ti.

1360 Dutiyaṁ.
