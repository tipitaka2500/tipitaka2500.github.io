4.4.9. อนุส์สริตัพ์พสุต์ต

Catutthapaṇṇāsaka

Ariyamaggavagga

Anussaritabbasutta

197. 1379 “Anussaritabbañca vo, bhikkhave, dhammaṁ desessāmi nānussaritabbañca. Taṁ suṇātha…pe… katamo ca, bhikkhave, nānussaritabbo dhammo? Pāṇātipāto…pe… micchādiṭṭhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, nānussaritabbo dhammo.

1380 Katamo ca, bhikkhave, anussaritabbo dhammo? Pāṇātipātā veramaṇī…pe… sammādiṭṭhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, anussaritabbo dhammo”ti.

1381 Navamaṁ.
