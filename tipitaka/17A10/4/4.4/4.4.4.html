4.4.4. สัป์ปุริสธัม์มสุต์ต

Catutthapaṇṇāsaka

Ariyamaggavagga

Sappurisadhammasutta

192. 1364 “Sappurisadhammañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi asappurisadhammañca. Taṁ suṇātha…pe… katamo ca, bhikkhave, asappurisadhammo? Pāṇātipāto…pe… micchādiṭṭhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, asappurisadhammo.

1365 Katamo ca, bhikkhave, sappurisadhammo? Pāṇātipātā veramaṇī…pe… sammādiṭṭhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sappurisadhammo”ti.

1366 Catutthaṁ.
