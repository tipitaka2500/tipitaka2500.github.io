3.15 Āyusaṅkhāraossajjana

3.15. อายุสํขารโอส์สัช์ชน

Mahāparinibbānasutta

Āyusaṅkhāraossajjana

169. 362 Atha kho bhagavā cāpāle cetiye sato sampajāno āyusaṅkhāraṁ ossaji. Ossaṭṭhe ca bhagavatā āyusaṅkhāre mahābhūmicālo ahosi bhiṁsanako salomahaṁso[172], devadundubhiyo[173] ca phaliṁsu. Atha kho bhagavā etamatthaṁ viditvā tāyaṁ velāyaṁ imaṁ udānaṁ udānesi—

363 [174] “Tulamatulañca sambhavaṁ,
Bhavasaṅkhāramavassaji muni;
Ajjhattarato samāhito,
Abhindi kavacamivattasambhavan”ti.

  1. salomahaṁso: lomahaṁso (syā1-3, kaṃ), lomahaṁsano (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. devadundubhiyo: devadundabhiyo (syā1-3), devadudrabhiyo (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. 14S5:1365; 17A8:688; 18Ud:457; 27Ne:312; 27Pe:392 (Cross Reference) ↩
