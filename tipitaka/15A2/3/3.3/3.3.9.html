Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

Dānavagga

150. 241 “Dveme, bhikkhave, anuggahā. Katame dve? Āmisānuggaho ca dhammānuggaho ca. Ime kho, bhikkhave, dve anuggahā. Etadaggaṁ, bhikkhave, imesaṁ dvinnaṁ anuggahānaṁ yadidaṁ dhammānuggaho”ti.
