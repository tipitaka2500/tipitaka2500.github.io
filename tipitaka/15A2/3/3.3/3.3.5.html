Dānavagga

146. 237 “Dveme, bhikkhave, bhogā. Katame dve? Āmisabhogo ca dhammabhogo ca. Ime kho, bhikkhave, dve bhogā. Etadaggaṁ, bhikkhave, imesaṁ dvinnaṁ bhogānaṁ yadidaṁ dhammabhogo”ti.
