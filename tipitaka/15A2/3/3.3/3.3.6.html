Dānavagga

147. 238 “Dveme, bhikkhave, sambhogā. Katame dve? Āmisasambhogo ca dhammasambhogo ca. Ime kho, bhikkhave, dve sambhogā. Etadaggaṁ, bhikkhave, imesaṁ dvinnaṁ sambhogānaṁ yadidaṁ dhammasambhogo”ti.
