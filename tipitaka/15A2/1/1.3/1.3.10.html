Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Bālavagga

31. 67 “Dvāhaṁ, bhikkhave, atthavase sampassamāno araññavanapatthāni[34] pantāni senāsanāni paṭisevāmi. Katame dve? Attano ca diṭṭhadhammasukhavihāraṁ sampassamāno, pacchimañca janataṁ anukampamāno. Ime kho ahaṁ, bhikkhave, dve atthavase sampassamāno araññavanapatthāni pantāni senāsanāni paṭisevāmī”ti.

  1. araññavanapatthāni: araññe vanapatthāni (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
