1.4.1.1 Ajjhattanandikkhayasutta

1.4.1.1. อัช์ฌัต์ตนัน์ทิก์ขยสุต์ต

Saḷāyatanasaṃyutta

Catutthapaṇṇāsaka

Nandikkhayavagga

Ajjhattanandikkhayasutta

156. 861 [231] “Aniccaṁyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhu cakkhuṁ aniccanti passati, sāssa[232] hoti sammādiṭṭhi. Sammā passaṁ nibbindati. Nandikkhayā rāgakkhayo; rāgakkhayā nandikkhayo. Nandirāgakkhayā cittaṁ suvimuttanti vuccati…pe… aniccaṁyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhu jivhaṁ aniccanti passati, sāssa hoti sammādiṭṭhi. Sammā passaṁ nibbindati. Nandikkhayā rāgakkhayo; rāgakkhayā…pe… cittaṁ suvimuttanti vuccati…pe… aniccaṁyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhu manaṁ aniccanti passati, sāssa hoti sammādiṭṭhi. Sammā passaṁ nibbindati. Nandikkhayā rāgakkhayo; rāgakkhayā nandikkhayo. Nandirāgakkhayā cittaṁ suvimuttanti vuccatī”ti.
