Saḷāyatanasaṃyutta

Catutthapaṇṇāsaka

Nandikkhayavagga

Bāhiranandikkhayasutta

157. 863 [233] “Anicceyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhu rūpe aniccāti passati, sāssa hoti sammādiṭṭhi. Sammā passaṁ nibbindati. Nandikkhayā rāgakkhayo; rāgakkhayā nandikkhayo. Nandirāgakkhayā cittaṁ suvimuttanti vuccati. Anicceyeva, bhikkhave, bhikkhu sadde… gandhe… rase… phoṭṭhabbe… dhamme aniccāti passati, sāssa hoti sammādiṭṭhi. Sammā passaṁ nibbindati. Nandikkhayā rāgakkhayo; rāgakkhayā nandikkhayo. Nandirāgakkhayā cittaṁ suvimuttanti vuccatī”ti.
