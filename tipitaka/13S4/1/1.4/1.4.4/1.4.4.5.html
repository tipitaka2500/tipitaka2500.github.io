1.4.4.5. ทุติยทารุก์ขัน์โธปมสุต์ต

Saḷāyatanasaṃyutta

Catutthapaṇṇāsaka

Āsīvisavagga

Dutiyadārukkhandhopamasutta

242. 1035 Ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā kimilāyaṁ[298] viharati gaṅgāya nadiyā tīre. Addasā kho bhagavā mahantaṁ dārukkhandhaṁ gaṅgāya nadiyā sotena vuyhamānaṁ. Disvāna bhikkhū āmantesi— “passatha no tumhe, bhikkhave, amuṁ mahantaṁ dārukkhandhaṁ gaṅgāya nadiyā sotena vuyhamānan”ti? “Evaṁ, bhante” …pe… evaṁ vutte, āyasmā kimilo bhagavantaṁ etadavoca— “kiṁ nu kho, bhante, orimaṁ tīraṁ…pe… “katamo ca, kimila, antopūtibhāvo. Idha, kimila, bhikkhu aññataraṁ saṁkiliṭṭhaṁ āpattiṁ āpanno hoti yathārūpāya āpattiyā na vuṭṭhānaṁ[299] paññāyati. Ayaṁ vuccati, kimila, antopūtibhāvo”ti.

1036 Pañcamaṁ.

  1. kimilāyaṁ: kimbilāyaṁ (sī, pā1), kimmilāyaṁ (syā1-3, kaṃ) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. vuṭṭhānaṁ: āpattiyā vuṭṭhānaṁ (sī, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
