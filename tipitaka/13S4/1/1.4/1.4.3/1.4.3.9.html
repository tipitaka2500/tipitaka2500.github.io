1.4.3.9 Paṭhamahatthapādopamasutta

1.4.3.9. ปฐมหัต์ถปาโทปมสุต์ต

Saḷāyatanasaṃyutta

Catutthapaṇṇāsaka

Samuddavagga

Paṭhamahatthapādopamasutta

236. 991 [278] “Hatthesu, bhikkhave, sati ādānanikkhepanaṁ paññāyati; pādesu sati abhikkamapaṭikkamo paññāyati; pabbesu sati samiñjanapasāraṇaṁ paññāyati; kucchismiṁ sati jighacchā pipāsā paññāyati. Evameva kho, bhikkhave, cakkhusmiṁ sati cakkhusamphassapaccayā uppajjati ajjhattaṁ sukhaṁ dukkhaṁ…pe… jivhāya sati jivhāsamphassapaccayā uppajjati ajjhattaṁ sukhaṁ dukkhaṁ…pe… manasmiṁ sati manosamphassapaccayā uppajjati ajjhattaṁ sukhaṁ dukkhaṁ…pe… .

992 Hatthesu, bhikkhave, asati ādānanikkhepanaṁ na paññāyati; pādesu asati abhikkamapaṭikkamo na paññāyati; pabbesu asati samiñjanapasāraṇaṁ na paññāyati; kucchismiṁ asati jighacchā pipāsā na paññāyati. Evameva kho, bhikkhave, cakkhusmiṁ asati cakkhusamphassapaccayā nuppajjati ajjhattaṁ sukhaṁ dukkhaṁ…pe… jivhāya asati jivhāsamphassapaccayā nuppajjati…pe… manasmiṁ asati manosamphassapaccayā nuppajjati ajjhattaṁ sukhaṁ dukkhan”ti.
