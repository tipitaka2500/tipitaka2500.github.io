1.1.2.9. ปมทุก์ขุป์ปาทสุต์ต

Saḷāyatanasaṃyutta

Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

Yamakavagga

Paṭhamadukkhuppādasutta

21. 54 [26] “Yo, bhikkhave, cakkhussa uppādo ṭhiti abhinibbatti pātubhāvo, dukkhasseso uppādo, rogānaṁ ṭhiti, jarāmaraṇassa pātubhāvo. Yo sotassa…pe… yo ghānassa… yo jivhāya… yo kāyassa… yo manassa uppādo ṭhiti abhinibbatti pātubhāvo, dukkhasseso uppādo, rogānaṁ ṭhiti, jarāmaraṇassa pātubhāvo.

55 Yo ca kho, bhikkhave, cakkhussa nirodho vūpasamo atthaṅgamo, dukkhasseso nirodho, rogānaṁ vūpasamo, jarāmaraṇassa atthaṅgamo. Yo sotassa… yo ghānassa… yo jivhāya… yo kāyassa… yo manassa nirodho vūpasamo atthaṅgamo, dukkhasseso nirodho, rogānaṁ vūpasamo, jarāmaraṇassa atthaṅgamo”ti.

56 Navamaṁ.
