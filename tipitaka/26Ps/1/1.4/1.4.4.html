1.4.4 Catutthasuttantaniddesa

2.4.4. จตุต์ถสุต์ตัน์ตนิท์เทส

Mahāvagga

Indriyakathā

Catutthasuttantaniddesa

198. 1073 Purimanidānaṁ[195]. “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, indriyāni. Katamāni pañca? Saddhindriyaṁ, vīriyindriyaṁ, satindriyaṁ, samādhindriyaṁ, paññindriyaṁ— imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañcindriyāni. Imāni pañcindriyāni katihākārehi kenaṭṭhena daṭṭhabbāni? Imāni pañcindriyāni chahākārehi tenaṭṭhena daṭṭhabbāni— ādhipateyyaṭṭhena ādivisodhanaṭṭhena adhimattaṭṭhena, adhiṭṭhānaṭṭhena, pariyādānaṭṭhena, patiṭṭhāpakaṭṭhena”.
