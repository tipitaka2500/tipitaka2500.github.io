2.1.7. ภํคานุปัส์สนาาณนิท์เทส

Mahāvagga

Ñāṇakathā

Bhaṅgānupassanāñāṇaniddesa

51. 366 Kathaṁ ārammaṇaṁ paṭisaṅkhā bhaṅgānupassane paññā vipassane ñāṇaṁ? Rūpārammaṇatā cittaṁ uppajjitvā bhijjati. Taṁ ārammaṇaṁ paṭisaṅkhā tassa cittassa bhaṅgaṁ anupassati.

367 Anupassatīti, kathaṁ anupassati? Aniccato anupassati, no niccato. Dukkhato anupassati, no sukhato. Anattato anupassati, no attato. Nibbindati, no nandati, virajjati, no rajjati. Nirodheti, no samudeti. Paṭinissajjati, no ādiyati.

52. 368 Aniccato anupassanto niccasaññaṁ pajahati. Dukkhato anupassanto sukhasaññaṁ pajahati. Anattato anupassanto attasaññaṁ pajahati. Nibbindanto nandiṁ pajahati. Virajjanto rāgaṁ pajahati. Nirodhento samudayaṁ pajahati. Paṭinissajjanto ādānaṁ pajahati.

369 Vedanārammaṇatā…pe… saññārammaṇatā… saṅkhārārammaṇatā… viññāṇārammaṇatā… cakkhu…pe… jarāmaraṇārammaṇatā cittaṁ uppajjitvā bhijjati. Taṁ ārammaṇaṁ paṭisaṅkhā tassa cittassa bhaṅgaṁ anupassati. Anupassatīti, kathaṁ anupassati? Aniccato anupassati, no niccato. Dukkhato anupassati, no sukhato. Anattato anupassati, no attato. Nibbindati, no nandati. Virajjati, no rajjati. Nirodheti, no samudeti. Paṭinissajjati, no ādiyati.

370 Aniccato anupassanto niccasaññaṁ pajahati. Dukkhato anupassanto sukhasaññaṁ pajahati. Anattato anupassanto attasaññaṁ pajahati. Nibbindanto nandiṁ pajahati. Virajjanto rāgaṁ pajahati. Nirodhento samudayaṁ pajahati. Paṭinissajjanto ādānaṁ pajahati.

371 Vatthusaṅkamanā ceva,
paññāya ca vivaṭṭanā;
Āvajjanā balañceva,
paṭisaṅkhā vipassanā.

372 Ārammaṇaanvayena,
ubho ekavavatthanā[67];
Nirodhe adhimuttatā,
vayalakkhaṇavipassanā. [66]

373 Ārammaṇañca paṭisaṅkhā,
bhaṅgañca anupassati;
Suññato ca upaṭṭhānaṁ,
adhipaññā vipassanā.

374 Kusalo tīsu anupassanāsu,
Catasso ca[68] vipassanāsu;
Tayo upaṭṭhāne kusalatā,
Nānādiṭṭhīsu na kampatīti.

375 Taṁ ñātaṭṭhena ñāṇaṁ, pajānanaṭṭhena paññā. Tena vuccati— “ārammaṇaṁ paṭisaṅkhā bhaṅgānupassane paññā vipassane ñāṇaṁ”.

376 Bhaṅgānupassanāñāṇaniddeso sattamo.

  1. Ᾱrammaṇaanvayena: ārammaṇaṁ anvayena (sī, syā1-3), ārammaṇā anvayena (pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
  2. ekavavatthanā: ekavavatthānā (ka-ma) (Word Variant) ↩
  3. ca: catūsu ca (syā1-3, pā1) (Word Variant) ↩
